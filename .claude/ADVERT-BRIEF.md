# Advert Brief: required before every advert

Owner's standing rule. Every advert request (new ad, variant, rebuild, script review)
starts with this brief. It runs at intake, before strategy, concepts, scripts, or any
generation spend, and it feeds the Campaign Director's Campaign Bible. It does not
replace or override any Service Pow policy or gate.

## The eight fields

1. **Advert ID:** a unique name for this advert (client, product, version, date).
2. **Intended audience:** exactly who should stop and watch.
3. **Objective:** the one action or result this advert is for (calls, bookings, leads, sales).
4. **One main message:** a single sentence the viewer should remember.
5. **Verified offer:** the offer exactly as the client confirmed it, with its proof.
6. **Opening hook:** the first one to three seconds that earn attention.
7. **Available footage supporting the message:** real clips, photos, and assets on hand.
8. **Call to action:** the exact words and the exact next step.

## Two ways to fill it

- **Walk-through:** ask the operator the fields one at a time, in order, and record each answer.
- **From a script:** when the operator supplies a script, identify each field in it, quote
  the line it came from, and mark any field the script does not answer as MISSING.

## Rules

- Show the completed brief back to the operator and get confirmation before moving on.
- Never fill a field with an invented fact. A missing answer stays MISSING and is asked about.
- "Verified offer" is only verified when the client confirmed it; otherwise mark it UNVERIFIED
  (claims-and-proof policy applies).
- Flag a field that is weak, not just empty: more than one main message, a hook that does not
  match the audience, a call to action that does not serve the objective, or footage that
  cannot support the message.
