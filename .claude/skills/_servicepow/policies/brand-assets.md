# POLICY — BRAND ASSETS (canonical LB24)
Always-on company law. The single home of the brand-truth rule. Skills reference this file;
they do not restate it. Lineage: LB24 "LOGO LAW" (2026-08-16), reconciled per Run 2 Phase 4A —
the law's scope is exact identity assets, deliberately NOT widened to all design work.

## 1. The law — exact identity assets are never regenerated
**Real approved client assets are the only source of brand truth** for:
- logos and wordmarks,
- exact brand marks (including on vehicles, uniforms, signage),
- packaging text and label copy,
- legal copy, and
- any other client-owned exact identity asset.

An AI-painted mark is always slightly wrong, and nearly-right reads faker than absent. These
assets enter production **only as the client's real files**: composited onto the approved still
BEFORE animation (with the mark locked sharp, correctly spelled, unchanged in the video prompt),
or corner-pinned in post. End cards are always built from real files in the edit.

## 2. What this law does NOT cover
This law is **not** a ban on generated design work. Generated layout, UI, typography
exploration, palettes, supporting visuals, scene art, and non-identity design are legitimate —
**subordinate to the client's brand standards** (`01_Brand_Standards` outranks any generated
palette or font pairing) and to `policies/realism-and-disclosure.md`. Widening §1 to cover them
is a policy violation, not extra safety.

## 3. Composite flags
Any shot containing an item from §1 is marked **COMPOSITE** before generation: the shot is
produced so the real asset can be composited, never so the model must paint it. The
brand-fidelity gate reviews outputs against the real files. Registry gates: BC-21 (correct
client + correct brand assets); the storyboard carries the COMPOSITE marking.

## 4. Vendor subordination
Vendor tools that generate marks (e.g. Higgsfield brandkit's SVG logo routes, Marketing Studio
brand kits scraped from a URL) may be used for **Service Pow-internal or prospective/spec work
only with APPROVER sign-off**, and **never** as source truth for a real client's identity
assets. For client work, §1 governs. See `vendor/higgsfield/PRECEDENCE.md`.
