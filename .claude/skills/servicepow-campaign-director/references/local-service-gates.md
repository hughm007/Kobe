# Local-service engagement gates
Single home of the home-services intake and measurement gates the Campaign Director runs
before any paid-media plan for a local-service client. Method derived from `claude-ads`
`local-service.md` (MIT, Daniel Agrici), re-expressed for Service Pow; qualitative, unscored.

## Five required gates (all pass before planning)
1. **Context** — document the service lines, service area, hours, dispatch capacity,
   seasonality, licensing, campaign objective, what a QUALIFIED outcome is, budget authority
   (maps to SPEND_APPROVER), and the escalation owner (APPROVER).
2. **Evidence** — verify current services, locations, availability, pricing, offers, reviews,
   and license facts through approved inputs with source IDs; anything that becomes ad copy
   needs an Evidence Record (`../../_servicepow/policies/claims-and-proof.md`).
3. **Regulatory** — identify local licensing display rules, call-recording consent,
   accessibility, emergency-service and category requirements; route uncertainty to the
   APPROVER, never guess (registry checks BC-16…BC-20 apply at delivery).
4. **Measurement** — define qualified calls, forms, bookings, completed work, cancellations,
   duplicates, and spam handling, plus offline reconciliation; there is no universal
   call-quality rule, so the definition is per-client and written into the Bible.
5. **Capability** — verify the account/region actually has the location, call, lead, schedule
   and audience features a plan would rely on, per the capability ladder
   (`../../_servicepow/vendor/CAPABILITY-LADDER.md`); never plan on `discovered` features.

## Planning questions (answered in the Bible before channels are chosen)
- Which services and places can the business actually fulfill right now?
- What distinguishes an inquiry, a qualified opportunity, a booking, and completed work?
- Which claims, offers, reviews, credentials and availability statements are approved (EV ids)?
- How do hours, dispatch capacity, response time and seasonality shape demand?
- Which search / local / social / remarketing roles are eligible AND measurable here?
- Which exclusions are supported by query or lead evidence — without overblocking demand?
- How will consent, call handling, routing, follow-up and customer data be governed?

## Output guardrails
Recommendations connect media to lead quality and fulfilled work, never clicks alone. Output
is qualitative options with source IDs, operational dependencies, owners, and checks — never
prescriptive radii, schedules, channel shares, bids, automatic account-action rules, or
lead-cost targets invented without account evidence.
