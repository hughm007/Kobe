# The Service Pow Advertising Standard

The bar every piece of creative is held to. Owned by `servicepow-creative-director`; other
sibling skills cite this file from their own SKILL.md via the standard cross-skill reference form
(one directory deeper, `../../…`, from within their own `references/` files). Cited by every
creative skill; restated by none.

## 1. Service Pow is not an AI video company

Service Pow is excellent at: **customer understanding → offer → positioning → ad strategy →
creative ideas → story → production → realism → editing → audio → distribution → testing →
learning → business results.**

Higgsfield and every other generation tool are *production tools inside that system*.
Technology serves the advertisement. **The advertisement does not exist to showcase AI.** An
ad that is impressive as a demo and weak as an ad has failed.

## 2. What the ad must be

Compelling · clear · memorable · specific · believable · emotionally effective where
appropriate · visually excellent · coherent · professionally edited · natural-sounding ·
platform-native · commercially useful · measurable.

## 3. What the viewer must understand

1. Who this is for
2. Why it matters
3. What is being offered
4. Why they should believe it
5. What to do next

If a viewer cannot answer all five after one watch, the ad is not finished — regardless of how
good it looks.

## 4. The diversity rule (canonical home)

**Diversity means different arguments, not different edits: three ideas that all argue "we're
fast" are one swing, not three.** A genuinely different variant changes the mechanism, the
argument, or the proof — never just the lighting, the grade, or the cut.

This is why the unit of work is a **pack** — one concept family × several genuinely different
hooks — rather than a single polished ad. The marginal cost of another variant is near-zero
with generative production; gilding one asset throws that advantage away. Quality is a floor,
not a ceiling: clear the blocking checks and the realism laws absolutely, then spend the next
hour on another variant, not a better version of the same one.

### Evidence (dated, not timeless doctrine)

- Per the ad-producer v4.0 consolidation (2026-08-20), an aggregate analysis of $1.29B in
  measured Meta spend across 578,750 creatives found roughly **5% of creatives are winners,
  and they absorb about 55% of the spend**.
- Per Meta's 1.1M-creative study, as recorded in the same 2026 consolidation, **creative
  diversity is worth up to a 32% CPA improvement** — the single largest lever measured.

These lines are recorded as what their sources state, not as independently measured here.
Re-verify before quoting them externally or after they age.

## 5. The AI tells that get work killed

**Structural:** random disconnected scenes · beautiful shots with no purpose ·
AI-clip→AI-clip→AI-clip editing · meaningless cinematic filler · poor scene-to-scene logic ·
generic scripts · sound that does not correspond with the image.

**Human:** dead eyes · robotic movement · bad hands · fake skin · bad lip sync · generic AI
narration.

**World:** morphing objects · incorrect physics · random camera movement · inconsistent
people · inconsistent products · inconsistent environments · incorrect branding.

**A beautiful shot that does not belong in the advertisement is rejected.** Cost already spent
on it is not an argument — spend discipline and the sunk-spend posture are governed by
`../../_servicepow/policies/generation-and-spend.md`.

## 6. The judgment standard (this is not optional politeness)

No Service Pow skill behaves like a yes-man. When the APPROVER proposes something generic,
strategically weak, poorly sequenced, unlikely to convert, too expensive, likely to generate
badly, unrealistic, unnecessary, or simply worse than an available alternative — **say so
plainly, then recommend the stronger option.** Do not preserve the idea at the expense of the
client's result.

Disagreement is delivered once, with reasoning and a recommendation. If the APPROVER reaffirms
after hearing it, that is their call — proceed and note the decision in the Campaign Bible.

## 7. Where the enforceable version lives

This file is the philosophy. The **enforceable** gates are:

- the canonical blocking-check registry (`../../_servicepow/data/blocking-checks.yaml`) — the
  only home of the delivery-gate list;
- the always-on policies in `../../_servicepow/policies/` (brand assets, claims and proof,
  realism and disclosure, generation and spend);
- ServicePow-6 scoring — `servicepow-creative-critic` owns the scorecard and its floors
  (registry gate BC-22).

Read those before declaring anything client-ready.
