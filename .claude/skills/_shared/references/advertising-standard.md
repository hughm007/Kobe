# The Service Pow advertising standard

The bar every piece of creative is held to. Cited by every creative skill; restated by none.

## Service Pow is not an AI video company

Service Pow is excellent at: **customer understanding → offer → positioning → ad strategy →
creative ideas → story → production → realism → editing → audio → distribution → testing →
learning → business results.**

Higgsfield and every other model are *production tools inside that system*. Technology serves
the advertisement. **The advertisement does not exist to showcase AI.** An ad that is
impressive as a demo and weak as an ad has failed.

## What the ad must be

Compelling · clear · memorable · specific · believable · emotionally effective where
appropriate · visually excellent · coherent · professionally edited · natural-sounding ·
platform-native · commercially useful · measurable.

## What the viewer must understand

1. Who this is for
2. Why it matters
3. What is being offered
4. Why they should believe it
5. What to do next

If a viewer cannot answer all five after one watch, the ad is not finished — regardless of how
good it looks.

## The AI tells that get work killed

Structural: random disconnected scenes · beautiful shots with no purpose · AI-clip→AI-clip→
AI-clip editing · meaningless cinematic filler · poor scene-to-scene logic · generic scripts ·
sound that does not correspond with the image.

Human: dead eyes · robotic movement · bad hands · fake skin · bad lip sync · generic AI
narration.

World: morphing objects · incorrect physics · random camera movement · inconsistent people ·
inconsistent products · inconsistent environments · incorrect branding.

**A beautiful shot that does not belong in the advertisement is rejected.** Cost already spent
on it is not an argument — see the sunk-credit rule in the production playbook.

## The judgment standard (this is not optional politeness)

ORION does not behave like a yes-man. When Karl proposes something generic, strategically weak,
poorly sequenced, unlikely to convert, too expensive, likely to generate badly, unrealistic,
unnecessary, or simply worse than an available alternative — **say so plainly, then recommend
the stronger option.** Do not preserve his idea at the expense of the client's result.

Disagreement is delivered once, with reasoning and a recommendation. If he reaffirms after
hearing it, that is his call — proceed and note the decision in the Bible.
