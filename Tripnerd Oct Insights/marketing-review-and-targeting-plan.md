## Target audience

US men aged 35 to 54 (the clearest real segment in the insights). Two buyer types inside it;
lead each ad with one:

- **Corporate host:** sales leader or business owner entertaining clients. Wants to impress
  guests and have logistics handled.
- **Bucket-list buyer:** buying for himself, his son, his dad or friends. Wants the memory and
  no risk of bad tickets.

## Plan to reach the real audience

1. **Fix the profile first (free).**
   - Bio names the events, the city served and the next step. Draft:
     "Masters · PGA · Super Bowl hospitality from Chicago. Tickets, hotels, transport
     handled. DM 'EVENT' for a quote." (Event list must be confirmed by TripNerd.)
   - Replace the Linktree with one inquiry page.
   - Pin three posts: events with starting prices, a client review, how booking works.
2. **Content answers his questions.**
   - What is included and what it really costs, per event.
   - Proof: real clients at real events, reviews, repeat corporate clients.
   - Risks (bad seats, fake tickets, traffic) and how TripNerd removes them.
   - Every post ends with one action, e.g. "DM 'MASTERS' for 2027 packages."
3. **Paid targeting for the right people.**
   - US only; start with Chicago and event host cities; ages 35 to 54.
   - Interests: golf, PGA Tour, NFL, college basketball, luxury travel, business owners.
   - Lookalike audience from past client emails, if available.
   - Retarget 50% video viewers and profile visitors.
   - Success metric: cost per qualified inquiry, not followers.
4. **Timing.** Post Sunday to Tuesday, 12 to 3 PM CDT. Align with buying windows (golf
   majors in winter, Super Bowl in fall).
5. **Follower base.** Stop any growth service; never buy followers. Removing likely fake
   non-US followers is a judgment call for the owner.

Highest-value next step: rewrite the bio and set up one inquiry page.
