Why this is worth doing first

  • No media cost.Both changes use the account and tools TripNerd already has. The only cost is time, plus hosting if the page goes on a new site.
  • Every future post and ad benefits.Paid ads send people to the profile and the link. If those don't convert, ad spend leaks. Fixing them first protects that spend.
  • Speaks to the buyer the data shows.The clearest real audience is US men aged 35 to 54. The new wording is built for two of them: the business owner hosting clients, and the fan buying a bucket-list trip.
  • Easy to undo.The old bio can be put back in a minute. Nothing is deleted.

What we need from you

  1. Approve the bio wording, or edit it.
  2. Confirm the events to list and a starting price for each, or approve "price on request."
  3. Confirm the keyword for messages ("EVENT" or another word).
  4. Name who answers inquiries, and how fast TripNerd will reply.
  5. Choose where the page lives: a page on TripNerd's website, or a simple landing-page tool.
  6. Approve 2 or 3 real client photos or reviews for the pinned posts, with the clients' permission.

Nothing goes live without your sign-off. The event list, "Based in Chicago," and the category change come from TripNerd's public profiles and need your confirmation. Blanks such as prices and reply time are yours to fill. No reviews or prices have been invented.
