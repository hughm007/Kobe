# Revision 3 — the judged synthesis

**How this spine was made.** Not by one author. Three rival spines were designed in parallel from
different angles — *product-first* (Kobe's prescription), *story-loop* (the loop as the native
unit), *fewest-words* (dwell doctrine) — then judged comparatively by three lenses (muted cold
viewer · claims-and-gate · craft-and-pacing), synthesized from the consensus winner (**story-loop**,
2 of 3 first places) with the losers' best elements grafted in, and adversarially checked with every
number recomputed. **Checker verdict: PASS, 0 blocking of 8 issues.** Two S1s acted on anyway:
`2 concepts × 2 hooks` restored to the `services.md` verbatim, and the endcard CTA raised to 64px
for real 390px-viewport legibility. Full design record: the workflow journal; drawing prototypes:
`build/proto-hand.html`, `build/proto-van.html` (4 iterations each, self-reviewed against renders).

**What Rev 3 answers.** Every R-item on `repair-board.md`: product named at 0.5s · one pain beat,
second · claims verbatim with both qualifiers · the labelled compliance triple with each tick
landing a visible change on a real ad · the signed line at 6.9s, the longest hold in the film · the
endcard instruction pointing down the page · every string in the 15–70% band on a left text rail ·
three snaps (frames 210 / 822 / 1464) escalating generic → your world → you · the grey placeholder
extinct · **a seam that is a story beat**: the endcard wordmark retracts into the persistent corner
mark while the marker starts drawing the next grid — frame 0 is frame 1799 plus one frame of the
same stroke. The film performs its own signed promise at the loop point.
