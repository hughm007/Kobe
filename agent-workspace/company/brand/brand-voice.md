# Service Pow Brand Voice

How Service Pow writes and speaks — in its own materials. For client work, the client's
voice governs; see that client's `brand-guide.md`.

## Voice attributes

## Rules

**NEEDS INPUT** unless noted.

- **Person:** "we" or "Service Pow"? First or third person?
- **Reader address:** "you" throughout?
- **Formality:** contractions allowed? Slang? Humour — and where's the line?
- **Sentence length:** house preference for short and punchy, or considered and longer?
- **Jargon:** how much marketing terminology does the audience actually want?
- **Spelling: US English.** Applies to all Service Pow materials. Client materials follow
  the client's own variant where it differs — check their `brand-guide.md`.
- **Punctuation:** Oxford comma? Em dashes? Exclamation marks — how many is too many?

## Applying voice

- Headlines: what's the house pattern — benefit-led, curiosity-led, plain-descriptive?
- CTAs: standard wording, so it's consistent across the site and ads.
- Email: subject line style, sign-off, sender name.
- Social: does the voice change per platform, and how much?
