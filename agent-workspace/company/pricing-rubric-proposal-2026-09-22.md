## Decisions Karl must make (⚠️)

1. **Paid-ad usage rights included at +0%.** Your rule prices rights as a separate line
   (+30–100%). The market-fairness review found that uplift is a UGC *likeness* convention;
   agency video retainers include paid usage; and a service business asking "why does running
   my own ad cost extra?" fails the zero-questions brief. Margin was recovered by raising
   Starter, not by keeping the line. **RECOMMENDATION: include rights.** Alternative: keep
   rights as a +30% line and hold Starter at $2,000 (page must then show $2,600 effective).
2. **Starter $2,500 instead of the recommended $2,000.** CFO lens: at an itemized 22
   operator-hours/month, $2,000 nets ~$67/hr after AI cost, below your own $75/hr flag;
   $2,500 nets ~$90/hr. Inside the $1,500–4,000 home-services band. **RECOMMENDATION: $2,500.**
3. **Ad-account management as a priced line.** services.md says "not in any package unless
   quoted separately"; OPEN-QUESTIONS D6 asks whether it is sellable at all. It is on the page
   because a $2–5M shop asks for it and its absence forces a call. It is fenced: optional,
   retainer clients only, separate line. **RECOMMENDATION: keep only if Hugh/Karl can actually
   run accounts monthly; otherwise delete the row and the FAQ stays clean without it.**
4. **Pilot at $1,500 with rights included is a founding price.** CFO lens: at 21.5 itemized
   hours it is ~$51/hr; at your 10–14h assumption it clears. **Time pilot #1 to the quarter
   hour; reprice to $1,800–2,000 if measured hours exceed 16.**
5. **New commercial terms introduced** (none existed): 3-month initial term then
   month-to-month with 30 days' notice; price lock for the initial term; $300 kill fee before
   storyboard approval, non-refundable after; "miss on our side = that month free"; reserved
   capacity + 25% deposit for a dated start; no late fees (pause-at-7-days stays); 5%
   prepay only; Central Time business days, 3 pm cutoff. These resolve OPEN-QUESTIONS Tier 3
   (payment terms, minimum term, notice, media billing, ownership) **if adopted**.
6. **Live servicepow.com pricing conflicts** (Brand Kit $2,500+, Social Launch $1,200/mo
   below floor, Lead Engine $2,500/mo, Full Pipeline $4,500/mo — decision 0006). Publishing
   this page while the site says something else recreates the questions it exists to remove.
   Reconcile the site or keep this page private until you do.
7. **Capacity.** Working ceiling is ~4 finished ads/month across ALL clients, never measured.
   Do not sign a second retainer until one full month is timed. Today's revenue ceiling is
   one Growth retainer plus pilots.

  Starter ~$90/hr · Growth ~$104/hr · Pilot ~$51/hr. At workspace hours (10–14h):
