# Services and Offers

What Service Pow sells. Each service records what's included, what's explicitly excluded,
what the client has to provide, and what "done" looks like. Scope disputes are almost
always caused by one of those four being unwritten.

Use this file when scoping a proposal, writing a services page, or deciding whether an
incoming request is in scope.

---

## Website design and build

**What it is.** Design and build of a new website, or a rebuild of an existing one.

**Typically includes**
- Discovery: goals, audience, competitors, required pages, success measures
- Information architecture and sitemap
- Design (or template selection and customisation)
- Build and responsive implementation
- Copy — *scope varies; see below*
- On-page SEO baseline: titles, meta descriptions, headings, alt text, sitemap, indexing
- Analytics and conversion tracking
- Pre-launch QA and launch
- Handover documentation and training

**Commonly excluded unless quoted**
- Ongoing content writing after launch
- Photography and video production
- Hosting, domain and third-party licence fees
- Ongoing maintenance and security updates
- Complex custom functionality, integrations, or e-commerce beyond the standard build

**Client provides:** brand assets, existing copy, product/service detail, access to
domain and hosting, a named decision-maker, and timely review.

**Definition of done:** the pre-launch checklist in
[`../playbooks/web/website-build.md`](../playbooks/web/website-build.md) is fully ticked
and handover is complete.

**NEEDS INPUT:** Which build platform(s) — WordPress, Webflow, Shopify, Squarespace,
custom? This determines a great deal about scope, timeline and pricing.

## Advertising

**What it is.** Planning, creative, buying and optimisation of paid media.

**NEEDS INPUT:** Which platforms Service Pow actually runs — Meta, Google (Search /
Performance Max / YouTube), TikTok, LinkedIn, programmatic display, out-of-home, print,
radio? The playbooks are written channel-agnostically until this is answered.

**Typically includes**
- Objective setting and target cost-per-outcome
- Audience and targeting strategy
- Creative concepting and production
- Campaign build, tracking setup and QA
- Ongoing optimisation and creative testing
- Reporting against the agreed measures

**Commonly excluded unless quoted:** media spend itself, landing page builds,
large-scale video production, ongoing creative refresh beyond an agreed cadence.

**Client provides:** ad account access, budget authority, product/offer detail, brand
assets, approval on creative and claims.

**Definition of done:** for a campaign — launched, tracking verified, and a reporting
cadence agreed. For a retainer — the agreed reporting rhythm is being met.

## Scope discipline

Two rules the agent enforces on every proposal:

1. **Nothing is "included" unless it's written down.** If a proposal doesn't list it,
   it's out of scope, and out-of-scope requests get quoted rather than absorbed.
2. **Every deliverable names its dependency.** If Service Pow needs something from the
   client to proceed, the proposal says so, and says what happens to the timeline if it
   doesn't arrive.
