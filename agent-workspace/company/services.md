# Services

## 0. Free Growth Audit — the lead-generation front door

**Owner-confirmed 2026-08-31.** This is the offer the website's primary button carries and
the single conversion path the intro video drives to. It sits *before* everything below:
the funnel is **pain → capability → proof → free growth audit**, and the audit is how a
sales conversation starts.

> A free, no-obligation review of a prospective client's current marketing and growth
> system, designed to identify the highest-impact opportunities, leaks and next actions.

**What it may review** — as relevant, and only from what is publicly available or what the
prospect supplies:

brand and first impression · website and landing-page conversion · Google Business Profile
and local search presence · reviews and reputation · advertising and creative where visible
· competitor positioning · lead capture · speed-to-lead and missed-call handling · follow-up
and CRM systems · tracking and attribution where information is supplied.

**What the prospect receives:**

1. The biggest problems and opportunities we identify.
2. The highest-priority recommended actions.
3. Where Service Pow could potentially help.

**Terms:** free · no purchase required · no obligation.

**Never promised, in any copy, on any surface** — these are the claim boundaries, not
stylistic preferences:

- ❌ revenue increases or any performance figure
- ❌ guaranteed results
- ❌ private-account analysis — we review what is public or what the prospect hands us
- ❌ access to or review of information the prospect has not supplied

Copy referring to the audit is bound by `../operations/compliance.md` like any other claim.

## 1. AI video ad production — the primary offering

**The unit of sale is a tested-variation pack, never a single video.** 2–3 concepts ×
hook variants, labeled by angle and hook so results are readable, delivered native 9:16
plus a 1:1/16:9 cutdown, captioned, platform-ready.

- **Pilot pack (the front door):** 2 concepts × 2 hooks = 4 finished ads, one strategy
  call, 5–7 business days from receipt of client assets, paid 100% up front.
- **Starter retainer (the engine):** 4 finished ads/month with hook variants, angle
  rotation guaranteed (no two consecutive deliverables argue the same thing), monthly
  performance report, one strategy call/month, 3-month minimum.
- **Hybrid real+AI is the default promise:** client's real phone footage as hero shots,
  AI environments and B-roll. This beats the "it looks AI" objection and is the moat.
- Production runs through the Video Production playbook and its blocking checks —
  see `../playbooks/ads/video-production.md`. Nothing ships around the gates.

## 2. Email marketing — demand capture and nurture

**Not a newsletter. The product is a list that didn't previously exist**, plus the
sequences that turn it into revenue. For clients with considered purchases (weeks-to-
months decision windows). Full doctrine: `../playbooks/content/email-marketing.md`.
Status: runbook written, test rig (burner domain + two-week warmup) not yet started.

## 5. Motion graphics / edit layer

Code-rendered graphics via Remotion (Node + Chromium): branded end cards, lower thirds,
word-synced captions, animated counters, kinetic type, grade passes. **The non-photoreal
layer only** — it never generates a logo (real client files, composited, always) and an
animated counter ("500+ jobs") is a factual claim needing written substantiation.
The end card is the recurring weakness this layer fixes: clean real logo on brand color,
2–3s, CTA that restates the ad's core benefit, audio resolving rather than cutting dead.
