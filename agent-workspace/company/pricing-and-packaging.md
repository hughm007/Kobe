# Pricing

**Policy: FIXED FLOORS, QUOTED PER DEAL** (Karl's decision, 2026-08-17). Nobody —
including any AI drafting a proposal — quotes below a floor without Karl's explicit,
logged approval.

**The prime rule: never price on what it cost to make; price on what it's worth to
them.** The comparison set is a $150–500 UGC creator or a $1,000–16,000 traditional
production — never a credit balance. The governing sentence: *if we would not be glad to
do this work at this price for the next twelve months, the price is wrong.*

## The floors (hard minimums)

| Item | Floor | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Pilot pack | **$1,200** | Recommended quote **$1,500** — confirm after first measured pilot |
| Monthly retainer | **$1,500/mo** | Recommended quote **$2,000** (4 ads/mo) — same caveat |
| Single one-off ad | **$400** | Discouraged; quote at floor, use as bridge to pilot |
| Rush (<5 business days) | **+50%** | Rush costs quality gates, and gates are the product |
| Pure-AI client (no real footage) | **+25%** | More generations, more rejects, more risk — a real cost, not a penalty |

Market bands for quoting above floors: per finished short ad $200–500 baseline,
$400–1,000 with proof; starter retainers $1,500–4,000/mo; growth $3,000–8,000/mo.
**Caveat: those bands come from DTC/app operators — no verified pricing data exists for
local-service AI ads. Treat local pricing as a hypothesis the first three pilots test.**

## Usage rights — a separate paid line, always

Standard delivery = organic social use. Paid-ad rights **+30–100%** · category
exclusivity **+50–150%** · perpetual buyout **+100–200%**. Most local-service clients
want paid-ad rights — quote it in from the start. Service Pow retains portfolio/spec
use unless exclusivity is purchased, stated in the proposal.

## The true-cost model

- **Credits are a fixed cost** (Higgsfield Ultra subscription), so they're prepaid
  capacity, not COGS — an idle month is the expensive one. Observed: ~700–950 credits
  per finished ad at current practice.
- **The real cost is operator hours.** Every quote is sanity-checked as effective
  hourly: (price − variable costs) / hours. `ASSUMPTION:` a pilot takes 10–14 operator
  hours — **never measured; measuring it on the first paid pilot is the single most
  important unknown in this file.** Price so the bad month (20 hrs) is still worth doing.
- **Honest margin is 50–65%** after iteration, QC and PM labor — any model above 65% is
  missing a cost.

## Discipline

- Revisions: **two rounds included**; round three billed. A new direction after approval
  is new work, not a revision — stated in the proposal before it's ever needed.
- Concept changes after storyboard approval are new work. The cost ladder is why: fixing a
  frame is one image job, fixing a clip is a video generation, fixing a master is a rebuild.
- Discounts: never to win a price objection; reduce scope instead; legitimate discounts
  capped at 15% (prepay, founding-client-for-case-study), always logged with reason.
  Never pay-on-results.
- Payment: pilot 100% up front; retainers monthly in advance; work pauses at 7 days
  overdue with warning. No net-30.
- Profitability flags per client: effective hourly < **$75** · routine 3+ revision
  rounds · consistently late payment · never sends media AND complains about AI look.
  Any flag → reprice, rescope, or exit at renewal.
