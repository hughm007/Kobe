# Pricing and Packaging

How Service Pow charges. The agent uses this to build proposals and to sanity-check
whether a scope and a number belong together.

> **Guardrail.** Never quote a price to a client that isn't in this file or explicitly
> approved for that engagement by a human. If a proposal needs a number this file
> doesn't have, leave `**NEEDS INPUT: price for X**` in the draft and flag it. A wrong
> number sent to a client is expensive to walk back.

## Packages

**NEEDS INPUT:** If Service Pow sells named tiers, define each: what's in it, who it's
for, and the price. If it doesn't, say so here — the agent will scope bespoke each time
rather than inventing tiers.

## Commercial terms

**NEEDS INPUT** for each:

- Payment terms and schedule (deposit? milestones? monthly in advance or arrears?)
- Minimum retainer term and notice period
- What happens to media spend — billed through Service Pow or paid direct by client?
- Out-of-scope work — how it's quoted and approved
- Late payment policy
- Ownership of assets and accounts at end of engagement

That last one matters more than it looks: who owns the ad account, the website, the
domain and the creative files after a client leaves should be settled in writing at the
start, and the agent should check it's addressed in every proposal.

## Discounting

**NEEDS INPUT:** Is discounting allowed, by whom, and to what floor? If the answer is
"no", writing it down here saves the agent from ever drafting one.
