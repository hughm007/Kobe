Bottom line
The build is a legitimate, well-typeset B2B site — strong hierarchy, consistent Archivo/Hanken
Grotesk pairing, disciplined ivory/navy/gold palette, no fabricated stats, no console errors, no
broken requests. It is not yet at the "futuristic, screaming we are the future" bar Karl set (the
@webloved TikTok reference, Spline/Three.js-style interaction quality, Apple-level polish). Right
now it reads as a clean, competent trade-services marketing site. The motion is basic
CSS/scroll transitions, not the choreographed "disciplined spectacle" he asked for — the three
intended wow-moments (growth system, ad-testing showcase, converging final CTA) exist as
content sections, not as spectacle. That's the biggest gap between what's built and what he's
asked for, and it's a design/animation investment, not a bug fix.
Below that ambition-level gap, there are concrete, fixable issues — several of which are things
Karl already approved fixing on 2026-09-18 and that are still not shipped in this build. Fix
those first; they're free wins with a standing sign-off.

Priority 2 — New issues found in this pass
1. Hero carousel mockups render at partial opacity on first paint. On a couple of loads, the
three phone-mockup images in the hero (911 Drain, TripNerd) rendered visibly ghosted/faded —
well below full opacity — rather than their normal full-contrast state. This reads as an
entrance-animation (fade-in) that either fires inconsistently or whose "resting" state is too faint.
Karl's standing creative rule is explicit: avoid ghosted/faded imagery. OBSERVED, and
inconsistent across reloads — worth reproducing deliberately (throttle CPU/network, reload a
few times) rather than assuming it's a one-off. If it's a scroll- or load-triggered opacity animation,
check its initial/rest opacity value and easing.

2. Hero H1 line-height is smaller than its own font-size. Computed styles: font-size:
43.578px, line-height: 40.963px on the four-line hero heading ("Good work / deserves /
to get / noticed."). Line-height under font-size is unusual — it can look fine at this exact viewport
and still crowd or clip ascenders/descenders at other zoom levels, font-loading states, or if the
copy changes length. VERIFIED via computed style. Recommend setting line-height to a small
positive multiple of font-size (e.g. 0.95–1.05em, explicitly, not smaller than 1) rather than
whatever's currently producing a sub-1 ratio.

3. Primary CTA button text renders at 11.5px. The "Free Growth Audit" nav button computed
to font-size: 11.52px, padding: 10px 16px, border-radius: 0. That's small for a
primary conversion button — it reads fine in caps at this size but is below the ~14px floor
typically recommended for interactive text, and this is the button carrying the entire site's
primary conversion action. VERIFIED via computed style. Worth bumping a step and confirming
the tap target is still comfortable on mobile (the nav button is even more cramped at the 390px
viewport).

4. Awkward empty gap in the mobile hero. At 390×844, there's a large blank vertical gap
(roughly 350–460px in scroll position) between the hero's "CAROUSEL / WEBSITE / STATIC
AD" strip and the "SELECTED WORK" heading that follows. Nothing renders there — it isn't a
loading image, it's dead space. OBSERVED. This is the kind of specific thing that adds up to the
"overlong mobile experience" Karl flagged in the earlier design review; worth checking the
mobile spacing/margin tokens around that section boundary specifically rather than a general
mobile pass.

5. /pricing retainer tiers may have changed since the last documented review, worth
confirming as intentional. This build shows exactly two Monthly Growth System tiers — Social
Launch ($1,200-tier, from the earlier notes) and Lead Engine (from $2,500/mo, marked "our
recommended starting point"). The previously-documented third tier, "Full Pipeline," does not
appear anywhere in the current page (checked the rendered HTML directly, not just the visible
viewport). If dropping Full Pipeline was a deliberate scope simplification, this looks like a clean
resolution to the "lacked distinct documented scope" problem flagged earlier. If it was dropped
by accident, that's a real gap — confirm which before treating it as fixed. VERIFIED absence in
HTML; the reason for its absence is not verifiable from the browser.

6. Below-the-fold sections may not be present in the initial server-rendered HTML. On a
fresh /pricing load, document.body.innerText returned only ~3,000 characters — just
the hero and "Foundations" tier cards — while the full page (confirmed by scrolling) is much
longer. The rest of the page's text wasn't in the DOM until scrolled into view. If this is
implemented as client-side mount-on-scroll rather than server-rendered-and-then-animated-in,
it's worth checking: content that isn't in the initial HTML response is invisible to anything that
doesn't execute JS and scroll (this matters for SEO crawlers and for the FAQPage/future
LocalBusiness schema actually matching what's rendered). OBSERVED, worth a source
check rather than a guess — Next.js SSRs by default, so this would have to be a deliberate
client-only conditional render.




Priority 3 — The ambition gap (design/animation, not bugs)
This is the honest read on "make it look more professional and advanced," separate from
bug-fixing:

   -​   What's working: consistent type system (Archivo for display, Hanken Grotesk for body),
        a real palette (warm ivory rgb(246,244,239) background, near-black navy text, gold
        #F4B318 for action), sharp 0-radius geometry that reads intentional rather than default,
        real work shown instead of fabricated stats, monospace micro-labels (STAGE 01 — 07,
        WHO WE SERVE) giving it a technical/systems feel that fits "for the trades, built like
        software."
   -​   What's missing against the stated bar: the reference points Karl gave (the
        @webloved TikTok, Parker & Sons "Not Your Average Joe," Apple-tier polish,
        Spline/Three.js-style scenes) all imply choreographed motion and depth — parallax, 3D,
        scroll-linked reveals with real easing craft, a cursor or interaction layer that feels alive.
        What's here is mostly static layout with basic transitions. The "How It Connects"
        staircase is the one section attempting a signature moment, and it's currently
        undermined by the duplicate-DOM bug above. The "Ad Creative" section and the final
        CTA ("Start with a clear plan") are both plain content blocks, not the "wow moments" the
        three-moment plan called for.
   -​   Recommendation: treat this as a separate, larger scope item from the bug list above —
        it's an animation/interaction design pass (GSAP ScrollTrigger, since that's already
        cleared for commercial use per your build notes), not a two-line fix. I'd sequence it after
        Priority 1 and 2 are closed, since right now the foundation has real cracks (duplicate
        DOM, no analytics, a form field that was supposed to be gone) that would undercut any
        amount of animation polish layered on top.
