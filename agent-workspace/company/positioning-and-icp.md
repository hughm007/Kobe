# Positioning and Ideal Client Profile

Who Service Pow is for, and why a prospect should choose it over the alternatives.

This file governs every piece of marketing Service Pow does for itself: the website, the
pitch deck, the outbound, the social. When those drift from this file, they stop
compounding.

## Positioning statement

> For **[who]** who **[situation / need]**, Service Pow is the **[category]** that
> **[distinct value]**. Unlike **[main alternative]**, we **[key difference]**.

**NEEDS INPUT:** Complete the statement. The hardest and most valuable field is the last
one — the difference has to be something a competitor would find awkward to claim.

## Anti-profile — who we're not for

**NEEDS INPUT:** The clients Service Pow should decline. Specific and honest: budget
below a floor, industries that don't fit, buyers who want a channel we don't run, anyone
expecting results on a timeline the work can't deliver.

Writing this down is what lets the agent flag a bad-fit lead early instead of producing
a proposal that shouldn't be sent.
