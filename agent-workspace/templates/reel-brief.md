## 4. What must clear before posting

- ☐ Third-party marks: none on screen; names in copy only per the client's rule, with the disclaimer line
- ☐ Faces: consent for every identifiable person (guest, staff, minor with a parent's consent), or the no-face fallback
- ☐ Claims: every line sourced or shown instead of said; no superlatives, counts or guarantees without an Evidence Record
- ☐ Generation: none, or declared; upscaling and tone are touch-ups; check whether the platform auto-labels the upload
- ☐ Music: licensed or original
- ☐ Voice per the client's `brand-guide.md`; link and call to action match the live destination
- ☐ Approvals: Service Pow APPROVER, then the client's approver, in writing
