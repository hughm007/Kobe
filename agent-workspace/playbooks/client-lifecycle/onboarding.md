# Client Onboarding

**When to use:** from signature to first deliverable.

The first three weeks set the tone for the engagement. A client who feels organised
handling in week one forgives a lot later; one who feels chaos never quite stops watching.

---

## 1. Set up the workspace

```bash
cp -r clients/_template clients/<client-slug>
```

- ☐ Folder created from the template
- ☐ `client-brief.md` filled from the discovery notes
- ☐ Client added to the index table in [`../../clients/README.md`](../../clients/README.md)
- ☐ Account owner assigned and recorded

## 2. Confirm the agreement

- ☐ Scope in writing, including what's explicitly excluded
- ☐ Deliverables and dates
- ☐ Commercial terms — fee, schedule, term, notice
- ☐ Who approves what, and how fast we can expect approvals
- ☐ Asset ownership at end of engagement settled in writing
- ☐ Signed before work starts

## 3. Gather

- ☐ Brand assets — logo files, fonts, colours, existing guidelines
- ☐ Existing copy, photography, video
- ☐ Product/service detail, pricing, terms
- ☐ Customer testimonials and reviews
- ☐ Anything a previous agency produced
- ☐ `brand-guide.md` completed from what arrives; gaps marked as inferred

Send one consolidated request rather than five separate ones. Chasing assets piecemeal is
the most common cause of a slipped first deadline.

## 4. Access

- ☐ Access requested for everything in the register in `access-and-accounts.md`
- ☐ **Delegated agency access preferred** over shared logins wherever the platform offers it
- ☐ Access confirmed working — not just granted
- ☐ Register completed with pointers *(never credentials — see the hard rule in that file)*
- ☐ Baseline metrics recorded before we change anything

That last point matters: without a pre-engagement baseline, there is no honest way to
show what the work achieved.

## 5. Establish the rhythm

- ☐ Reporting cadence and format agreed —
      see [`reporting.md`](reporting.md)
- ☐ Meeting rhythm set
- ☐ Communication channel agreed, and response-time expectations stated in both directions
- ☐ Escalation path — who to call when something's urgent
- ☐ Named contacts on both sides

## 6. First deliverable

Ship something concrete early, even if small. Momentum in week one buys patience in
month three.

- ☐ Delivered on or before the promised date
- ☐ Meets [`../../operations/quality-bar.md`](../../operations/quality-bar.md)
- ☐ Feedback captured into `brand-guide.md` as standing dos and don'ts

## 7. Thirty-day check

- ☐ Is the brief still accurate? Update it if not.
- ☐ Is the scope holding, or has creep started? Address it now, while it's small.
- ☐ Is the client getting what they expected? Ask directly.
- ☐ Worklog entry recording the state of the engagement

---

## Offboarding

When an engagement ends, run the offboarding checklist in the client's
`access-and-accounts.md`, then:

- ☐ Final report delivered
- ☐ Assets and documentation handed over
- ☐ Our access removed; their credentials removed from our vault
- ☐ Learning written — why did this end, and what would we do differently?
- ☐ Client folder moved to [`../../archive/`](../../archive/) with a closing note

Offboard well. A clean exit is the difference between a lost client and a future
referral.
