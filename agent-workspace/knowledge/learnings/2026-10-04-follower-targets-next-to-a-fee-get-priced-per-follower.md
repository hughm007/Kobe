# A modest follower target next to a multi-month fee gets priced per follower

## What we did
- **The pitch:** a 90-day organic Instagram growth plan to a premium sports-travel client (anonymised).
- **Price:** about $10k–12k for 90 days.
- **The measurement slide** showed an honest follower target: about +8% over 90 days, from roughly 4,700 to 5,100. Enquiries and bookings were on the same slide, but both were "unknown" and had blanks.

## What happened
- The client divided the fee by the follower gain. Their words, via the meeting notes: too much "for growing from about 4,700 to 5,100 followers".
- **Result:**
  - the deal dropped to a one-month test at about 15% of the 90-day price;
  - the client asked for a fee that scales with follower growth.

**Source:** Karl's meeting notes, 2026-10-04. One meeting, so a sample of one.

## What we think it means
- The only concrete outcome number on the page was followers, so followers became the unit of value: roughly $25–30 a follower.
- The booking-value case (one corporate booking can be worth thousands) was blank, so it couldn't compete.
- The plan was right to set conservative follower targets. Putting them beside the fee, with no money outcome next to them, framed the deal badly.

**Confidence:** Low to medium. It's one client and one meeting, and the price objection may have existed anyway. The mechanism is plausible and matches the client's own words.

## How far it generalises
- ☐ Specific to this client
- ☑ Likely true for organic-social proposals to owner-run businesses
- ☐ Likely true for this channel generally
- ☐ Probably a general principle

It transfers wherever the proposal has no filled-in money outcome (margin per booking, value per lead) and a small audience-growth number is the most concrete figure.

## What we'd do next
- **Before pricing a multi-month organic plan,** get the client's margin per booking or lead on the discovery call. Put the break-even ("pays for itself at N bookings") on the page, ahead of follower targets.
- **If followers must appear,** show them as a leading signal under the money outcome, never as the headline result beside the fee.
- **Offer a paid test month by default** for new organic engagements. It's where this client landed anyway.
