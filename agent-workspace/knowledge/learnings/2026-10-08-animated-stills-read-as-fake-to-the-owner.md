# An animated still photo reads as fake, even with a clean push-in

## What we did
In TripNerd's "Augusta, handled" Reel, shot 2 (2.3 s) was TripNerd's published photo of the Private Executive Home, animated by Seedance 2.5 as a slow push-in to the door. TripNerd had no video of the house. The take was checked frame by frame: no warping, and the door and windows stayed stable.

## What happened (FACT)
- Jason (TripNerd) approved the advert.
- The owner scored it **7.7 / 10**: "I just really don't like how the house is a still image and it looks fake."
- The rest of the advert was not faulted: the real lawn and veranda footage, the voice, the end shot and the card.
- This is the second owner mark-down on stills. "Book It Now" earlier drew "I hate still images".

## What we think it means
- **A technically clean animated photo still reads as a still.** A small push-in on a photograph does not pass as footage to this owner.
- **For any shot of the product itself (the house), get real video.** Ask TripNerd for a phone walk-up of the house before the build. If none exists, cut the shot rather than animate the photo.
- **Generation is for gaps that are not the product.** This is consistent with decision 0008 (real client material first).

**Confidence:** High for this owner (two independent mark-downs on stills). Untested with audiences.
