# A stills-only Reel of event hospitality plateaued below the ServicePow-6 floor

## What we did
An organic 9:16 Reel for a sports-hospitality client was cut from the client's own iPhone stills of one hosted day. The stills were timed by their EXIF timestamps ("by the clock"). The event itself was never photographed: phones were not allowed at the venue.
- **Cuts:** five, each re-gated by a fresh creative critic (ServicePow-6, floor 8.0) and an isolated Skeptic.
- **Edits tried between passes:** opening on guests' faces, opening on the brand, different photos, the racing-clock device, original music, and retouching.
- **Spend:** $0. Everything was rendered by script.

## What happened
| Cut | Critic score (± 1.5) | Hook axis | Skeptic |
|---|---|---|---|
| v1 | 6.2 | — | BLOCK (3 × S3) |
| v2 | 7.0 | — | BLOCK (S4 event marks) |
| v4 | 7.7 | 6 (opened on guests) | CONDITIONAL (no S3/S4) |
| v5 | 7.3 | 6 (opened on the brand) | see the campaign Bible §14 |

**Source:** [the campaign Bible, §13–14](../../clients/tripnerd/campaigns/2026-10-04-launch-reels/campaign-bible.md).

## What we think it means
- **Edit-level fixes cleared every truth and rights problem.** They could not lift the hook above 6. Both critics named the same root cause: "the thing the client sells is never on screen".
- **When the core experience is absent from the footage, the ceiling is in the asset, not the edit.** More recuts trade one weakness for another (faces-first spends the payoff; brand-first is "the least aspirational moment").

**Confidence:** Medium. It rests on one client and five cuts, with one critic instance per cut.

## What we'd do next
- **Before scripting a Reel, ask:** "is the paid-for moment on camera?" If it isn't, get footage first. For this client: their own short video from the venue; or event-side clips with crowd sound, which are the Reel ideas that rank highest in the growth plan.
- **Stills fit carousels.** A carousel doesn't need a motion hook.
- **Ask the critic for a ceiling estimate** after the second failing pass, before cutting a third.
