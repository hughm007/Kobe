# The keyword DM is part of the ad: most blocking findings on a no-AI storyboard sat in the destination and the claims

## What we did
Storyboarded a 35-second organic Instagram Reel for TripNerd (a real staff member recreates
and ranks five Augusta concession foods at home). Every shot was real phone footage plus
composited text, so generation risk was nil. The storyboard went to an isolated Skeptic
subagent (Pass 1) with the Reel, caption, hook variants and the keyword DM reply.

## What happened
| | Count |
|---|---|
| Findings, total | 27 (3 S4, 5 S3, 11 S2, 8 S1) |
| Shots rated above LOW generation risk | 0 of 10 |
| S4 findings about the **DM reply** | 2 of 3 (unsourced event-rule claims; unsourced offer claims) |
| S4 findings about consent | 1 of 3 |
| S3 findings about claims, parity, pacing, affiliation | 5 of 5 |

Things the producing session had missed and the Skeptic caught: the DM promised a "cheat
sheet" but delivered three notes and a pitch; a full grocery receipt was being compared with
five single servings; spoken lines ran at roughly 220–360 wpm against the shot timings; text
sat outside the registry's 15–70% burned-text band; the comment keyword ("Augusta") would fire
the sales DM on ordinary ranking arguments.

**Source:** `clients/tripnerd/campaigns/2026-10-05-augusta-menu/qc/2026-10-05-skeptic-pass1.txt`.

## What we think it means
On a real-footage piece, Pass 1's generation-risk job is trivial, but the pass is not: the risk
moves to truth, rights and the destination. The keyword DM reply is ad copy with its own claims
and must go into the Skeptic packet and the Evidence Records like the Reel itself. Two cheap
habits would have pre-empted most findings: (1) count words per spoken line against the shot
duration before writing timings; (2) check every comparison is like for like (same items, same
quantity) before it becomes a hook.

One finding was partly wrong on the facts: "free payphones" and "grandstands first come, first
served" were flagged as unsourced, but AP/WRDW (2025) report the phones as free and TripNerd's
own Augusta page states the grandstand rule. The packet simply didn't carry those sources: a
claim not in CLIENT-FACTS is a finding by design.

**Confidence:** Medium — one storyboard, one client. Promote to the storyboard/script playbooks
if the DM and pacing findings recur on the next two packets.
