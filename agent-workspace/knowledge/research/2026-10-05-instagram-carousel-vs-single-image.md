# Carousel vs single image on Instagram (researched 2026-10-05)

**Question (Karl, for TripNerd):** for brand awareness on Instagram, does a carousel or a single-image static get better reach?

**Answer:**
- For organic posts, carousels beat single images on reach (slightly) and on engagement and saves (clearly).
- Reels remain the main format for reaching people who don't follow you.
- For paid awareness bought on reach alone, single images are cheaper per impression.

## Evidence

All figures are as reported. The primary pages were blocked from this environment, so these are quoted from search excerpts of the sources' own pages.

| Source | Carousel | Single image | Reels | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Socialinsider 2026 Instagram benchmarks ([page](https://www.socialinsider.io/social-media-benchmarks/instagram)) | Reach rate **4.50%**; engagement rate **0.55%** | Reach rate **4.00%**; engagement rate **0.33%** | Reach rate 4.10%; engagement rate 0.50% | Brand accounts. Reach rate is mostly follower reach. Platform reach fell 14% YoY; static images' engagement fell 17% YoY |
| Metricool 2026 Instagram study ([press release](https://metricool.com/press-release-instagram-study-2026/)) | Leads on interactions and saves | Reach **−21.96%**, interactions −25.41%, engagement −45.98% YoY | Highest raw reach | 24.4M posts, 375k accounts, Jan–Feb 2025 vs Jan–Feb 2026 |
| Instagram (Adam Mosseri, as reported by [RouteNote](https://routenote.com/blog/instagram-carousels-perform-better-than-single-photos-according-to-the-head-of-instagram/) and [Planoly](https://planoly.com/blog/instagram-carousels-are-making-a-comeback)) | Can be re-shown to followers starting at slide 2 if they scrolled past; music makes carousels eligible beyond the feed | — | — | A mechanism, not a number |
| Paid (Meta) blog benchmarks ([Lebesgue](https://lebesgue.io/facebook-ads/facebook-carousel-ads-benchmarks-and-performance), [Embryo](https://embryo.com/blog/the-data-behind-the-performance-of-carousel-ads/)) | Better conversion/CAC | Lowest CPM (cheapest reach) | Highest CTR | **Low reliability:** vendor blogs, unclear samples |

**Conflict in the sources:** older Socialinsider data (as quoted on third-party blogs) says Reels get about 36% more reach than carousels and about 125% more than images. The 2026 Socialinsider figures put carousels first on reach rate. Treat Reels as the non-follower reach format and carousels as the strongest feed post.

## What it means for TripNerd (RECOMMENDATION)
- **Keep statics as carousels** where the content has steps (the hosts checklist): best organic feed format and saves, and an extra chance to be shown.
- **Use single images** only for one-message posts, or as paid boost creative when the goal is cheap reach.
- Real non-follower growth still comes from Reels (the 17th-hole video).
