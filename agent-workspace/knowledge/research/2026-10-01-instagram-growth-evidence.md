## Ranking signals

- **FACT** — No single algorithm; Feed, Stories, Explore, Reels each rank separately.
  Reels predicts reshare, watch-to-end, like, audio-page visit. Explore predicts likes,
  saves, shares. Stories ranks on viewing/engagement history and closeness.
  [Instagram Ranking Explained, 2023](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained)
- **FACT** — Mosseri, Jan 2025: the three signals that matter most are watch time, sends
  per reach, likes per reach. Likes weigh more with followers; sends weigh more with
  non-followers. [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-insights-2025/738034/)
  — the popular "sends are worth 3–5× likes" claim is **not** an official figure.
- **FACT** — Originality: on 30 Apr 2026 the repost penalty extended to photos and
  carousels. Accounts reposting non-original content 10+ times in 30 days lose
  recommendation eligibility until 30 days after the last one.
  [MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/414773/instagram-algorithm-update-discourages-reposted-co.html),
  [PetaPixel](https://petapixel.com/2026/04/30/new-instagram-policies-target-reposted-content/)
- **FACT** — Trial Reels (Dec 2024): shown to non-followers only; public professional
  accounts with 1,000+ followers. [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-expands-access-trial-reels/752895/)
- **FACT** — Public professional posts indexable by Google/Bing from 10 Jul 2025;
  hashtags capped at 5 per post (announced Dec 2025) and not a reach lever.

## Creators, collabs, paid

- **FACT** — Collab posts: up to 5 collaborators; appears on every grid and in each
  audience's feed. [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-collab-post/)
- **FACT (Meta's own claim)** — Partnership ads: 19% lower CPA, 13% higher CTR vs
  standard brand ads. [eMarketer](https://www.emarketer.com/content/meta-expands-partnership-ads-turn-creator-content-performance)
- **ESTIMATE** — Creator rates: nano $100–500, micro $500–2,500, macro $2,500–10,000+
  per post; +20–30% Reels, +30–50% whitelisting. [Later, Jul 2026](https://later.com/blog/influencer-pricing-benchmarks-the-complete-2026-guide/)
- **FACT** — Paid: Traffic objective with "Instagram profile" destination optimises for
  profile visits, not follows; an "Instagram follows" reporting metric appeared Jul 2025.
  [Jon Loomer](https://www.jonloomer.com/create-a-traffic-campaign-with-instagram-profile-conversion-location/),
  [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/meta-introduces-instagram-follows-metric-for-ads-reporting/)
- **ESTIMATE, low reliability** — Cost per follower $2.50–$4.00 (blogs only). Measure it.

## Giveaways

- **FACT, weak/dated** — 6 sponsored giveaways (2019–20): 61% of participants unfollowed
  within two weeks; effective CPF rose from $0.06–0.09 to $0.16–0.23 after churn.
  [Social Media Examiner](https://www.socialmediaexaminer.com/do-sponsored-instagram-contests-really-work-case-study/)
- **FACT** — Instagram Promotion Guidelines: organiser runs it lawfully; entrants release
  Instagram; no inaccurate tagging. [Help Center](https://help.instagram.com/179379842258600)
- **FACT** — NY (GBL §369-e) and FL (§849.094) require registration and bonding when
  total prizes exceed $5,000 in a game of chance. Confirm with counsel.

## Growth services and fake influence

- **FACT** — FTC Rule on Consumer Reviews and Testimonials (16 CFR 465), in force
  21 Oct 2024, prohibits buying or selling fake indicators of social media influence
  (bot/fake followers or views); civil penalties up to $51,744 per violation.
  [Sidley](https://datamatters.sidley.com/?p=7426), [Perkins Coie](https://perkinscoie.com/insights/update/ftc-finalizes-rule-banning-fake-reviews-and-testimonials)
- **FACT** — Instagram has said it removes inauthentic likes, follows and comments from
  accounts using third-party apps to boost popularity; its Community Guidelines ask
  accounts not to artificially collect likes, followers or shares.
- **OBSERVED** — Ascend Viral is a manual follow/like service (account manager engages
  on your behalf); reviews mixed on follower quality.
  [ExpertBeacon](https://expertbeacon.com/ascend-viral/), [Trustpilot](https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/ascendviral.com)
