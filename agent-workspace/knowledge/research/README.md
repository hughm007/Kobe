# research/

Market, competitor, audience and channel research. The reference material behind strategy.

**Filename:** `YYYY-MM-DD-subject.md`

## Belongs here

- Competitor analysis
- Audience and customer research
- Market and industry context
- Channel or platform research
- Pricing and positioning research

## Doesn't belong here

- Results from our own campaigns → [`../learnings/`](../learnings/)
- Client-specific strategy → that client's folder
- Choices we made → [`../decisions/`](../decisions/)

## Rules

- **Date everything and cite everything.** Research decays. A statistic with no date and
  no source is unusable six months later, and worse than nothing if it gets quoted.
- **Separate observation from inference.** What you found vs. what you concluded. Mark
  which is which.
- **Note the method.** What you looked at, how many, over what period. It's how a reader
  judges the weight to give it.
- **Re-check before reuse.** Before citing research in a pitch or a strategy, confirm it's
  still true. Especially platform behaviour and competitor claims.
- **Index it** in [`../index.md`](../index.md).
