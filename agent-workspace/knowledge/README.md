# knowledge/

What the office has learned. This is the folder that makes the workspace worth more each
month rather than just larger.

Three kinds of knowledge, three subfolders:

| Folder | Holds | Answers |
|---|---|---|
| [`decisions/`](decisions/) | Choices we made, and why | "Why do we do it this way?" |
| [`learnings/`](learnings/) | Evidence from doing the work | "What actually happened when we tried X?" |
| [`research/`](research/) | Market, competitor, audience research | "What do we know about this space?" |

Plus [`index.md`](index.md), the curated map, and [`glossary.md`](glossary.md).

## The loop

```
do the work → observe → learnings/ → (confirmed by repetition) → promote to playbooks/
```

A **learning** is evidence from one instance. A **playbook** is settled practice. When the
same learning shows up three times, fold it into the playbook and link back to the
learnings that earned it.

A **decision** is different: it's a choice with consequences, recorded so that future-you
knows the reasoning and can tell a deliberate choice from an accident. Decisions are
immutable — to change one, write a new decision that supersedes it and link both ways.

## Rules

- **One idea per file.** Files with three learnings in them get found for the wrong one.
- **Be specific and falsifiable.** "Shorter subject lines work better" is nearly useless.
  "Subject lines under 35 characters lifted open rate from 21% to 29% across four sends to
  this list" is usable.
- **Include the numbers, and the sample.** A result without its volume can't be judged.
- **Log losses.** A record of what doesn't work is a competitive advantage precisely
  because everyone else throws it away.
- **Anonymize before generalising.** A learning that leaves this folder for a playbook or
  a pitch must not carry another client's identifiable data. See the confidentiality
  guardrail in [`../CLAUDE.md`](../CLAUDE.md) §10.
- **Index it.** An unindexed knowledge base is a landfill. Update
  [`index.md`](index.md) when you add anything.
