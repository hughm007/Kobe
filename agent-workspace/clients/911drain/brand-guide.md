## The one rule above all others (LB24)

**The logo, wordmark, and van are real files, never generated. Ever.** No AI
recreation, no "close enough" re-draw, no regenerated wrap in a video frame. If the
real asset isn't on hand, the work waits until it is. A generated logo that's 2% off
is a different company's logo.

## Voice

- **Attributes:** urgent, not panicked · plain-spoken, not folksy · confident, not
  boastful (every claim must be on the signed claims sheet — and it isn't signed yet)
- **Person / address:** "we" for the company, "you/your" for the homeowner. Direct
  address always — this is someone standing in an inch of water.
- **Formality:** contractions yes; humor sparingly and never about the emergency
  itself; no slang.
- **Spelling variant:** US.
- **Words they use:** drain, clog, backup, emergency, 24/7, licensed, East Valley,
  the 480 number spoken as digits.
- **Words to avoid:** **"sewer" (until CR-37 scope is verified — see below)**,
  **"commercial"** or any B2B framing (CR-37 is residential-only), "cheapest,"
  "guaranteed" without a signed substantiation, any price not confirmed by Will.

**On-brand example**
> Drain backed up at 2 AM? We answer at 2 AM. Licensed, local, East Valley —
> 480-992-3541. ROC 366870.

**Off-brand example**
> Your trusted full-service plumbing & sewer solutions partner for residential and
> commercial needs!

**Why the difference matters:** the second one makes three claims the license doesn't
support (full-service, sewer, commercial), addresses no one, and would fail the
compliance gate before it failed on taste.

## Their customers' language

Homeowner in an active emergency: "backed up," "overflowing," "smells," "how fast can
you get here," "how much is the call-out." Search behavior is need-it-now — the ad's
job is the phone number and the reassurance, not brand-building.

**⚠ TAGLINE DRIFT (open, Tier 1):** the logo says "DRAIN REPAIR"; the wrap says
"DRAIN & SEWER REPAIR." Two taglines advertising two different scopes, and the wider
one is unverified against the CR-37 license. A van is an advertisement. Until Will or
the ROC confirms sewer scope: **new assets carry the logo tagline only.**

**Compliance in the visual layer:**
- **ROC 366870 appears in advertising** — on every ad, inside the platform-safe area
  (not where TikTok/Reels UI overlays it).
- Nothing marketing commercial work, anywhere, in any asset.

**Accessibility:** white-on-black and red-on-black pass for large display text; body
text in red on black fails WCAG AA — use white for body copy.

## Standing dos and don'ts

**Always:**
- ROC number on every ad, safe-area placed.
- Real footage for the "during" state; the claims sheet check before any claim ships.
- The 480 number as the call to action — measurable, and what an emergency customer wants.

**Never:**
- Generate the logo, wordmark, or van (LB24).
- Say "sewer" or "commercial" until scope is verified/licensed.
- Describe LSA or any campaign as "running" — nothing has ever been published for this
  client yet.
- Ship a price line the owner hasn't confirmed (the v8 kill included a price flashed
  too fast to read — speed is also a claims problem).
