## 2. Strategy
*Owner: `servicepow-strategy`.*

### Offer verdict
**Is the offer strong enough to advertise?** **ADEQUATE — with one structural strength and one
structural gap.** For need-it-now emergency demand, the offer *is* the response: answer, arrive,
fix. That clears the bar for demand capture. The gap: with the claims sheet unsigned there is no
speed promise, no price anchor, no guarantee — nothing quantitative to advertise. What would make
it STRONG: a signed claims sheet (any one substantiated response-time or fee fact).
**Reasoning:** an emergency-service ad does not need superiority claims to work — it needs to be
the number the homeowner trusts at the moment of need. Trust can be built claim-free from a
verifiable fact: the license. That is the only proof asset this client currently owns.

**Target:** East Valley homeowner, two states: (a) mid-emergency (backed-up sink/shower/toilet,
need-it-now), (b) pre-emergency (saves the number). Primary: (a).
**Awareness level:** problem-aware and urgency-maximal; brand-unaware (nothing has ever been
published for this client).
**Core message:** **You don't have to gamble on who you let in — check the license, then call.**
**Angle (chosen, and the ranked alternatives):**

   mandatory ROC line from fine print into the argument. Claim-free (the license is a public

   as runner-up and as a natural future pack.

   does not exist yet. Revisit after the media ask lands.
**Core promise:** a licensed, verifiable local crew answers when your drain backs up.

## 4. Creative spine
*Owner: `servicepow-creative-spine`. The anti-choppy authority.*

**Core message:** you don't have to gamble on who you let in — check, then call.
**Core promise:** a licensed, verifiable local crew answers when your drain backs up.
**Primary emotion:** **control regained** (one emotion, shared by the whole pack; relief is its
final beat, not a second emotion).
**Viewer starting state:** unease — mid-emergency, or jolted into imagining one.
**Viewer ending state:** equipped — knows the check exists, has the number that passes it.
**Narrative question:** when it happens, how will you know who to let in?
**Why the viewer keeps watching:** the question is about *their* front door; each beat answers a
step (check → door → drain → calm) with no beat skippable.
**Final payoff:** the house sounds normal again — and the number on screen is the one you just
watched pass the check.
**CTA logic:** the viewer has just watched verification happen; "look us up" + the number is the
same action made theirs. No hard turn — the CTA is the demonstrated behavior.
