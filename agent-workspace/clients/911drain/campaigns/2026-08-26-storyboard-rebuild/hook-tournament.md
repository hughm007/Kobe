# Hook Tournament — 10 candidates in, 3 survivors out

**Protocol:** 10 candidates written with mechanism · first frame/action/text/audio · why the
target stops · declared expected failure mode. Then handed to `servicepow-skeptic` in a **fresh,
isolated context** for a paper attack. The survivors are the pack's variants.

## Context-isolation record (behavior test 6c evidence)

The Skeptic was given ONLY: client facts (business, license, platform, target, brand-voice
attributes), the hard compliance constraints, the production reality constraints, and the ten
candidates verbatim. It was NOT given: the creative reasoning behind any candidate, any ranking,
any preference, the chosen angle's rationale, or the concept-family write-up. Its instruction
block opened: *"you have deliberately NOT been given the creator's reasoning or preferences."*

## The pack

1. **A (lead) — H8 "Stop using water"** — stops the exact person the client needs
   (mid-emergency), works fully muted, intrinsic frame-1 motion, cheapest compliant build,
   mildest worst-case failure.
2. **B — H3 "Water doesn't wait"** — dread + intrinsic motion lands the beat under 1s with zero
   audio and zero people; its one serious wound (sewage misread) closes in the grade, not the
   concept.
3. **C — H1 "Six digits"** — everything it says is true and publicly verifiable; everything it
   needs is real and buildable today; covers the save-the-number segment the other two do not.
   **Trust variant, never the lead. Digits ECU-legible or it dies.**

Skeptic's pack note, kept verbatim: *"The three survivors split cleanly across the two declared
viewer states (H8/H3 emergency, H1 pre-need), which is the only reason a 3-survivor pack is
defensible rather than thin."*

## Post-attack repair (visible, not silent)

- H1's burned line changed from "Six digits tell you who's **really** licensed" to
  **"Six digits tell you exactly who you're letting in."** Reason: the Skeptic's client-lens
  finding that "really licensed" leans on doubt about everyone else. The repair removes the
  implied comparative while keeping the curiosity beat. (Severity source: H1 attack, client
  lens.)

## Beaten hooks worth a future pack

- H9 "Fridge magnet" → belongs to the preparedness angle (strategy runner-up) as a *concept*,
  not a hook — the object needs a format where it can be read.
- H2's anti-ad energy → usable someday as a static placement, where a text card is native and
  the motion floor does not apply.
