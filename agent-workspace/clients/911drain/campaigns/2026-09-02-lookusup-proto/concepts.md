# 911 Drain — three concepts, ranked on effectiveness x feasibility (2026-09-02)

Client truth used: emergency **drain** repair, **residential only** (CR-37), East Valley AZ ·
AZ ROC **366870** · 480-992-3541 · real `logo.png` exists · claims sheet **UNSIGNED** ·
**"sewer" and "commercial" are barred words** · LB24 — logo/van/wordmark never generated.
Evidence route: hands-on-tool shots are a proven generation failure (0 of 6 usable across two
controlled tests); environments are our strongest generated class; text must be composited.

---
## Concept 1 — "LOOK US UP" (licence as the selling idea)
**Central idea:** you are about to let a stranger into your house at night. Anyone can own a
drain machine; not everyone is licensed — and you can check, yourself, in ten seconds.
**Hook:** "Who do you let in?"
**Why it works:** it turns our single hardest *verified* fact — ROC 366870, which is legally
required on the ad anyway — into the persuasive spine. It sells trust, which is the actual
purchase anxiety for an emergency trade, and it needs **zero unsigned claims**.
**Assets required:** residential environments, a door, water beats, real logo, composited text.
**Effectiveness 9 · Feasibility 9**
**Status: owner-endorsed already.** Revision 18 of this script exists, its caption is
Owner-picked ("You can check your plumber's license yourself — state registrar"), and the beat
map is compliance-checked. What was killed on 2026-08-20 was the *execution* — motion inert,
dead-space open, dissolve-only cuts — not the idea.

## Concept 2 — "STILL DRAINING SLOW?" (illustrated / educational)
**Central idea:** the slow drain you have been ignoring is a clog forming; show what is
happening inside the pipe, which no camera can show.
**Hook:** an illustrated cutaway of a pipe narrowing.
**Why it works:** plays directly to our strongest-rated lane (illustrated ~7/10 vs realistic
~4/10) and explains a mechanism, which realism cannot do.
**Effectiveness 7 · Feasibility 8**
**Why not now:** it is a *retargeting/education* ad, not an emergency-response ad. It sells a
slower problem than the one that makes someone dial at 11pm. Best kept as ad #2 or #3.

## Concept 3 — "2:47 AM" (the emergency moment)
**Central idea:** drains do not fail at convenient hours.
**Hook:** a dark house, a sound, standing water.
**Why not:** three strikes on evidence. It repeats the angle of the **killed** Video Pack 01
("2:07 AM"); it depends on exactly the dark/moody realism the owner scored 4/10; and its
emotional peak wants hands-on-tool footage we have proven we cannot generate.
**Effectiveness 8 · Feasibility 3**

---
## SELECTED: Concept 1 — "Look Us Up"
Highest on both axes, already owner-endorsed at script level, and — decisively — it is the
only one of the three whose strongest beats route entirely through production methods we have
*evidence* we can execute: environments (generate), text (composite), logo (real file).
