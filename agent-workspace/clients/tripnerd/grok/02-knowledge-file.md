## 2. Audiences (write for one per post)

1. **Corporate hosts.** People taking clients to big events. They care about:
   - the day running right: arrival, being in the right spot for the big moment, food close to the action, getting everyone home;
   - impressing clients;
   - enjoying the day themselves.
2. **Fans and friend groups.** People chasing a bucket-list event. They care about:
   - being there for the moment;
   - the seat and the view;
   - who they'd bring.

## 8. Growth strategy (what makes a post work)

**Topic first:** each Reel commits to one big topic people already follow, visible in frame 1. The niches:
- golf-fan moments;
- golf-trip and buddies culture;
- corporate hospitality;
- the sports bucket list.

TripNerd is the payoff, not the opener.

**Reel checklist:**
1. A stranger can name the topic from frame 1 with the sound off.
2. Real motion and sound in the first second.
3. One topic; TripNerd is the payoff.
4. 7–15 seconds, with an ending that loops.
5. Made to be sent (name a person or a plan), never "send this".
6. Search words on screen and in the first caption line; 3–5 hashtags.
7. Original footage only.
8. Test 2–3 openings as Trial Reels.
9. Judge by watch-through, sends, follows and non-follower share, not likes.

**Formats:** carousels beat single images for organic reach and saves in 2026 benchmarks. Reels are the main way to reach non-followers.

## 9. Client-approved adverts (what TripNerd liked, and why)

### "Their Camera Roll": 25 s, vertical 9:16. Approved 29 Sep 2026; Karl ranked it above the 8.4 below
- **The idea:** a phone camera roll of real TripNerd guests at every event. Grid, tap, Derby, Daytona, Phoenix; the 17th-hole suite video "comes alive" with the real roar; an event list; then "We have everything. Which one's yours?" and the end card.
- **Built from:** real TripNerd photos (their "NERDS in Action" gallery) and their own 17th-hole suite videos. Touch-up only (upscale, auto-tone). **No AI-generated people, places or footage.**
- **Why it won:** "We need to be able to capitalize on the real client footage and this advert is a good example of that." Real material, with the motion design telling the story.
- **Before any paid boost:**
  - guest releases (one photo shows a child, which needs a parent's OK or a swap);
  - replace the placeholder music with a licensed track;
  - check "this year" and "February";
  - "We have everything" is puffery.

### "Hosting spot" (drone opening): 25.8 s, horizontal 16:9. Approved 27 Sep 2026, scored 8.4/10
- **The flow:** "THE PLAYERS / VIP ON 17", food, drinks, connections, the roar, the host, real suite footage, end card.
- **Why 8.4:**
  - "Idea was great… I liked how [it] only used its strengths in making adverts because the realism was there."

- **Open items:**
