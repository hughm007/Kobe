# TripNerd — Brand Guide

How this client sounds and looks. **This governs every asset produced for them** — it
overrides Service Pow's own house voice and visual identity in all their materials.

If the client supplied a formal brand guide, record where the master lives and summarize
the working essentials here. If they didn't, build this from their existing materials and
mark what's inferred rather than confirmed — the distinction matters when a client
disputes a choice later.

**Source:** ☐ Client-supplied guide ☑ Inferred from existing materials (September client-approved adverts, 2026-10-05). No formal guide is on file; ask TripNerd for one.

## Voice

- **Attributes** (three to five, each defined by contrast — "warm, not saccharine"):
- **Person / address:** (we/us, I, third person; do we say "you"?)
- **Formality:** contractions, humour, slang — where's the line?
- **Spelling variant:** UK / US / AU
- **Words they use:** (their own terms for their products, services, customers)
- **Words to avoid:** (banned terms, competitor language, anything legally risky)
