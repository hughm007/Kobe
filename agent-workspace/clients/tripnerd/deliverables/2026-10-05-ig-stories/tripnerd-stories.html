Rules these drafts follow

  • 3–5 frames per set. Viewer retention stays above 75% up to about 6–7 frames a day (Rival IQ).
  • The hook is frame 1, which has the highest exit rate at 23.8% (Socialinsider).
  • One set a day. That matches what accounts of 10k–50k followers do. In Hootsuite's one-account test, 1–2 Stories a day got more reach and engagement than 5–10.
  • Captions on every video. Around 40% of Story video is watched with sound off (Instagram).
  • A link sticker on every set. Every account can use them now, and link taps are the main conversion number to track.

How this research was done: the research could only use search-engine summaries of the linked pages, because direct page loads and Instagram itself were blocked from our environment. We didn't view any profile or Highlight directly. The Story formats we couldn't find any brand evidence for, such as what's-included walkthroughs, countdown stickers and FAQ Highlights, are presented here as our recommendations, not as what competitors do. TripNerd's Instagram handle and follower count are unconfirmed.

Decisions for the owner

  • Event names. TripNerd's site says "Super Bowl" and "Augusta." The NFL actively enforces its "Super Bowl" trademark against non-sponsors, so these drafts say "the Big Game" and use city names. Ask TripNerd whether to keep that.
  • Experience claims. TripNerd's site says "20+ years of industry experience," but records show the company was founded in 2017. Don't use either figure in Stories until they confirm which one is accurate (it's probably the founders' experience).
  • Ownership. TripNerd was acquired by All Access Golf Travel & Events in 2023. Confirm with them that the brand, handle and approver haven't changed.
  • No league or tournament logos and no broadcast footage in any frame
  • Post time: use Instagram Insights' "most active times" once we have access. Until then, late morning in TripNerd's main market is a guess, not data.
