Ready-to-post checklist

From TripNerd today

  • Instagram access for ServicePOW (posting rights through Meta Business Suite, not a shared password)
  • Logo files and the exact brand blue (the blue in these mockups is a stand-in)
  • 15–25 real photos and clips from past trips: seats, hospitality, hotels, groups, Augusta week
  • Written confirmation of what's included in the packages shown in Stories 2 and 3
  • Which 2027 events are on sale now, with their page URLs for the link stickers
  • One or two Google reviews plus guest photos they have permission to share
  • Who answers DMs Wednesday through Friday

Decisions for the owner

  • Event names. The NFL actively enforces the "Super Bowl" trademark, so these drafts say "the Big Game" and "Augusta week" instead of the official names. TripNerd should decide whether to keep that or use the names it already uses on its own site.
  • No league or tournament logos and no broadcast footage in any frame
  • Approve the copy for all five Stories (Story 1 is due Monday morning)
  • Post time: use Instagram Insights' "most active times" once we have access. Until then, late morning in TripNerd's main market is a guess, not data.
