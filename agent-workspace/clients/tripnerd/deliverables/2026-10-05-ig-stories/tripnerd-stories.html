Rules these drafts follow

  • 3–5 frames per set. Viewer retention stays above 75% up to about 6–7 frames a day (Rival IQ).
  • The hook is frame 1, which has the highest exit rate at 23.8% (Socialinsider).
  • One set a day. That matches what accounts of 10k–50k followers do. In Hootsuite's one-account test, 1–2 Stories a day got more reach and engagement than 5–10.
  • Captions on every video. Around 40% of Story video is watched with sound off (Instagram).
  • A link sticker on every set. Every account can use them now, and link taps are the main conversion number to track.
