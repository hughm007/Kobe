The marketing angle

Sell the Nerd, not the trip

Everyone in premium sports travel says "VIP" and "unforgettable." TripNerd's name is the one thing a competitor can't copy, so every Story has to prove that a Nerd knows something useful. Each set does at least one job: teach something the viewer didn't know, show what goes wrong without a Nerd, prove it with TripNerd's own reviews, or give a real reason to act now. "Here's our trip" photos appear only as supporting evidence.

Each concept was put through a separate red-team review: would a competitor run it unchanged, what would a viewer remember, and is it actually marketing. Concepts that failed were cut: "Nerd vs Google", the client-value slider, the Add Yours chain (which stays empty on an account getting 2 likes), the 3 a.m. call (we can't promise overnight support until TripNerd confirms it), and 17th-hole trivia.
