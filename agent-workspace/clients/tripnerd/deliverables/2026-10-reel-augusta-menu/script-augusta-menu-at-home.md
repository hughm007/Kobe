# Reel: "The $1.50 Augusta menu, made at home (and ranked)"

**Format borrowed:** food review / taste-test ranking, the format food creators use to get
watched to the end.
**TripNerd twist:** the menu is the famous Augusta concessions menu, and a real Nerd
(TripNerd staff) makes it at home and ranks it.
**Goal:** awareness. Targets watch time (ranking keeps people watching to #1), sends ("send to
the golf group chat") and comments (a ranking people will argue with).
**Length:** 30–35 seconds, vertical 9:16.
**On camera:** one real TripNerd team member. No AI people, voices or faces (TripNerd plan,
p.6).

## Three alternative first seconds (Trial Reels test)

1. **Price shock:** a receipt fills the frame. Text: **"One of everything at Augusta costs $75.75."**
2. **Controversy:** egg salad held up. Text: **"Egg salad is better than pimento cheese. Here's the proof."**
3. **Challenge:** the Nerd at the counter. Text: **"Can we make Augusta's $1.50 menu for $1.50?"**

Post the main version. Run 2 alternates as Trial Reels (non-followers first), and keep
whichever opening wins on 3-second hold and watch time for the next food Reel.

## Shoot list (one person, one phone, about 1 hour)

- **Groceries (~$30–40):** white bread, sharp cheddar, mayo, pimentos, eggs, chips, cheese
  straws (or bake them), peach ice cream, cookies for the ice cream sandwich.
- **Props:** plain green wax paper (generic, no logos), five hand-written price tag cards,
  1–10 score cards.
- **Camera:** phone vertical, 1080p or 4K at 30 fps, window light, steady hand. Shoot every
  item twice: close-up and reaction.
- **Sound:** record the bites and crunches clean. Close-up food sound helps people watch
  with sound on.
- **Edit:** burned-in captions; on-screen text inside the safe area (clear of the top 14%
  and bottom 20%); cuts every 1–2 seconds; quiet music bed from Instagram's commercial-use
  library under the Nerd's voice.

## Rule check

- Real person, real food, real opinions. No AI.
- No tournament logos and no footage from the grounds (phones aren't allowed there anyway).
- Says "Augusta," not that TripNerd is official. Calls them "our versions, not the official
  recipes."
- Every price is sourced. The grocery bill uses the real receipt total.
- The marketing line ("the house, the passes, the logistics") matches TripNerd's Augusta page:
  private executive home, course passes included, concierge-managed logistics.
