Growing your followers

What we do in every post and every day to reach new people, and one decision for you on boosting.

How we'll grow your followers

  • Open on the moment. Every Reel starts on a big event moment from your footage, so Instagram shows it to people into that event.
  • Test before we commit. Reel 2 runs as a Trial Reel, shown to non-followers first. The winning opening gets used again.
  • Made to be sent. Each Reel gives people a reason to send it to the friend they'd take. Sends are one of the signals Instagram weighs most.
  • A reason to follow. Your bio and every caption say what following TripNerd gets you.
  • Collab posts. When a post features a venue or partner, we invite them as a collaborator, so it shows on their account too.

Ranking signals: Adam Mosseri, head of Instagram, January 2025.

Is there a budget for boosting posts?

Boosting pays Instagram to show a post to more people, including people who've never seen TripNerd.

  • Our suggestion: no boost until a Reel proves itself on its own. From week 3, put a set amount behind the best one.
  • You set the ceiling. $250–$500 for October is enough to learn from.
  • On top of the $1,500, paid to Meta from your ad account. Nothing is spent without your sign-off.
  • Reported separately, so you always see organic and paid results apart.

Beyond the posts: what we do every day

20 minutes every weekday, as TripNerd. We reply to every comment and DM, and leave real comments on posts from event accounts, venues and fans heading to the events you sell. It's done from the Instagram app, so on day one we agree who handles it: us, or someone on your team working from our daily list.

The guest loop. At every trip, your host asks guests to tag @tripnerd. We reshare their Stories the same day and, with their OK, turn the best into Collab posts on their account too. Tell us which trips run in October and November.

Your customers, pointed at Instagram. We write a follow link and QR code for your booking emails, itineraries, email signatures and website. Past guests are your warmest followers.

What we won't do: follow-for-follow, mass liking, bots or bought followers. They break Instagram's rules, put your account at risk, and those followers don't stay.

To start, we need

Partner access in Meta Business Suite (no passwords), one approver who replies within two business days, and all your trip footage in one shared Drive folder. Taylor's 17th-hole clips first.

Then you decide

Every Monday you see followers, views, the share from non-followers, sends, saves and follows on each video. In the first week of November we review October together, and you decide what comes next.
