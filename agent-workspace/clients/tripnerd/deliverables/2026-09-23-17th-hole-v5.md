## Rules applied
- LB24: the only marks are the real door sign (first frame of a real photo) and the real logo file on the end card; nothing generated carries a readable mark; the lounge screens keep what the real photo shows.
- LB25: the chosen 360 keeps faces at backs and profiles; two alternates with a laughing face near camera were rejected.
- LB26: one bed plus one roar source; the 360 clip's own crowd is held under it, not chained with other crowd samples.
- LB30: real references for the cup, the ball, the suite, the balcony, the crowd; commentary phrasing from broadcast research, no real broadcaster's signature line, no real player.
- Claims: none. "No experience like the TripNerd experience" is puffery, not a measurable claim.
- Delivery spec: H.264 yuv420p tv-range bt709, 24 fps, −14 LUFS, TP ≤ −1 dBTP (measured above), captions inside safe zones.

## Research and review used
- An 8-agent research and review pass ran alongside production. Its commentary research (broadcast call anatomy: hushed read before the ball goes, sparse reads during the roll, a two-word burst on the make, then lay out; fictional surnames checked against tour lists) and its roar and mix plan (one roar source, swell 0.8–1.2 s before the drop, attack 120–200 ms after, no re-trigger at cuts, strip or hold the clip's native crowd) shaped v5b directly. It also surfaced the seed_audio delivery controls (loudness_rate, speech_rate, pitch_rate) used for the hushed lines. Its prompt panel, judge and skeptic passes had not returned when the masters were rendered; their findings will be logged in the worklog when they land.
