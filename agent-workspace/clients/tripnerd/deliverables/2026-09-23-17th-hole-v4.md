## Rules applied
- LB33: every generated shot ≤ 5 s in the cut (longest 4.8 s).
- LB25: the 360 alternate with a laughing generated face at readable distance was rejected; the used take keeps backs and profiles near camera. The look-around shot shows one calm generated profile at mid distance (derived from the real photo's guests).
- LB26: one concatenated bed; the roar under the drop is the same source as the 360 clip that follows. Ducking is applied per voice line, not across the whole VO span, so the roar hits full level between lines.
- LB30: the ball, cup and green were generated against real reference photos; the model that ignored them (dark oversized cup) was rejected.
- LB24: no logo generated; the TripNerd logo is the real file on the end card. The Gemini clips drew a "108 mm" dimension label on the cup for their first second; that second is cut, none of it is in the master.
- Claims: no superlative, no availability or date claim.
- Delivery spec: H.264 yuv420p tv-range bt709, 24 fps, loudness normalised to −14 LUFS, TP ≤ −1 dBTP (measured values in QC below).
