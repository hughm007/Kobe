# TripNerd — Instagram follower growth plan

**Deck (32 slides, private until Karl shares it):** https://claude.ai/artifact/PZicQM3VjVj2n3YHbPXBQQ
**Status:** DRAFT. Not sent to TripNerd. Karl reviews before anything goes to the client.
**Builds on:** the Sept 2026 "TripNerd Social Direction" deck (Drive, 22 slides, data
observed 11 Sep 2026). This plan keeps its five Reel formats and 90-day logic and adds
the follower-growth layer the client asked for: creators, carousels, giveaways, paid,
conversion, calendar and targets.

## The recommendation
1. Put people and access on the feed: 4 Reels/week of real trips, hosts and guests.
   Headline cards over stock photos come off the calendar.
2. Borrow audiences: one hosted creator trip a quarter (Collab post + partnership-ad
   rights), Collab posts with venues and past guests in between.
3. Pay only behind proven posts: boost Reels at ≥2× median to profile visits; retarget
   viewers with statics. Test $500/mo for 60 days, scale to $1,000/mo before tentpoles.
4. Convert: link-in-bio notify-me lists feeding the scoped email program.

## Direct answers to the client's questions
| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Creator collabs? | Yes — biggest lever (Golfbreaks creator collab = 7.4× its median Reel) |
| Carousels? | Yes, 1–2/week for saves and sends; not the reach engine |
| Giveaways? | Rarely — one per season, filtered, legally compliant (61% churn in tracked study) |
| Video vs static ads? | Both: video earns reach/follows; statics retarget and carry the offer |
| Paid follower-growth services? | No (guidelines, account risk, wrong audience; FTC fake-influence rule) |
