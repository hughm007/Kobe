## "A golf shot with people cheering at Augusta"

**No: not real footage, not broadcast, and not an AI version.**
- **Patron rules:**
  - Phones and recording devices are banned at all times inside Augusta National.
  - On tournament days cameras are banned.
  - On practice days still photos only, for personal use.
  - A real clip can't exist within the rules, and using one would risk the badge holder's credentials.
- **Broadcast footage:** controlled by Augusta National (CBS, Paramount+, ESPN, Amazon Prime Video from 2026). A licence is needed.
- **AI version:**
  - It creates people, which breaks TripNerd's rule.
  - It carries trade-dress and false-association risk (Masters and Augusta National marks, the green jacket colour mark, the recognisable holes).
  - It carries FTC deception risk if presented as TripNerd guests.
- **TripNerd's real roar clips** are from THE PLAYERS (17th hole). That's the wrong event for an Augusta ad.
- **The real fist-pump photos** at the lawn (P045/P046/P164/P165) show shirts parodying the Masters logo ("MASTERED"). Excluded.
- **Better:**
  - capture a real guest cheer at the hospitality lawn next April (releases signed; TV out of frame);
  - use V25's live music as this cut's energy moment.
