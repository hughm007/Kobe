# "AUGUSTA" keyword auto-reply

**Why:** the Reel tells viewers to DM "AUGUSTA", and the video shows an instant reply. That reply has to exist, **word for word**, before the Reel posts.

## The reply (exact text, as shown in the video from v4)
> Thanks for messaging TripNerd! Augusta week with us: a private executive home, daily hospitality and food & drink, hosted by TripNerd. Tap below and a Nerd will send dates and details.

**Changed 2026-10-06 (v4):**
- **Shorter, so it can be read on screen:** 29 words, was 39. The v3 gate found the old reply couldn't be read in the time it held.
- **"Course passes" is removed** until TripNerd says in writing what it covers. Both the gate's claims reviewer and its verifier read it, in an Augusta-week ad, as tournament access (S3).
- **Adding it back:** if TripNerd confirms the scope, it returns in their exact wording, here and in the video together.
- **Superseded text (v1–v3):** "Thanks for messaging TripNerd! Augusta week with us includes a private executive home, course passes, daily hospitality and food & drink, hosted by TripNerd. Tap below and a Nerd will reply with dates and details."

**Button:** "Get the Augusta details"
- Link to TripNerd's Augusta page on tripnerd.com. Karl checks the link on his phone.
- If buttons aren't available in the tool TripNerd uses, put the link at the end of the message instead. Tell me, and I'll update the video to match.

## Set it up (TripNerd does this on their account; menu names change, so verify in the app)
1. Open Meta Business Suite and go to **Inbox → Automations**. (Or in the Instagram app: **Settings → Business tools → Automated responses / Saved replies**.)
2. Create a **keyword** automation. Keyword: `AUGUSTA`, case-insensitive. Also add `augusta` and `Augusta` if the tool needs them listed.
3. Paste the reply above exactly. Add the button or link.
4. Turn it on for **Instagram**.
5. **Test it:** from a personal account, DM "AUGUSTA" to @tripnerd. Screenshot the reply with the time visible, and send me the screenshot. It's the receipt that clears this gate.

## Who answers next
- Name the **DM owner** before posting: the person who sends dates and details within business hours.
- The reply promises "a Nerd will reply". An unanswered DM costs more than no ad.
- Log each "AUGUSTA" DM in the Monday report: DMs received, replied within 24 hours, became a quote.

## Rules
- No prices, dates or availability in the automated message. A person sends those.
- Never use the tournament's name or "Masters" in the reply.
