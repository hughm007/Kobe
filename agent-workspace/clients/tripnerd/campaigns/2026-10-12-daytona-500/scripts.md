# Daytona 500: five scripts (draft, 2026-10-12)

**Brief (owner, 2026-10-12):** Daytona 500 is the next advert. Analyse all the real Daytona footage and draft five scripts that can become client-ready adverts.

**Material:** [`footage-inventory.md`](footage-inventory.md). Every shot below names its source and seconds.

**Why now (RECOMMENDATION):** the race is on 21 February 2027, about four months out. That is the nearest buying window TripNerd sells into, alongside Phoenix.

## Rules every script follows
- **Real TripNerd material only.**
  - Touch-ups allowed: upscaling, grading, cropping.
  - No generated people, cars or crowds.
  - Text is composited.
  - Engine and crowd sound come from a licensed library, or from the real 2024 clips once reviewed.
  - Music is licensed.
- **On-screen place line:** "Daytona Beach · February" until owner decision 2 on event names. If the race is named, TripNerd's own disclaimer goes on the end card.
- **Claims:**
  - Words to avoid: no "VIP", "best", star ratings or counts.
  - "Seats over pit road", "hospitality suite" and "oceanfront hotel" match TripNerd's page. They stay **DRAFT** until TripNerd confirms the 2027 package by email.
  - "Garage tour" and "on the track" only after TripNerd confirms them.
- **Faces:** from behind, wide or in crowds by default. Named consent items are listed per script.
- **End card:** the approved brand-blue card with the real logo, "Talk to a Nerd", tripnerd.com.
- **Call to action:**
  - Send-to-a-friend on organic posts until someone covers DMs.
  - "Talk to a Nerd" on enquiry and paid versions.
- **Format:** 9:16, 1080x1920. 16:9 material sits in a viewer frame over a blurred ambient fill (the approved Camera Roll treatment), or is upscaled before any full-bleed crop.
