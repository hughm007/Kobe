## 2. Hard rules (do not break any of them)
1. **Real media only.** No AI-generated people, cars, crowds or scenery. No AI animation, interpolation or AI upscaling of the photos. Text and graphics are added in editing.
2. **No third-party marks on screen:** no NASCAR, speedway, sponsor or team logos. Where they appear in a photo, they are hand-blurred or cropped.
3. **No claims without written proof from TripNerd.** Not yet confirmed: seats over pit road for 2027, the hospitality suite, the oceanfront hotel, track access, garage tours. Do not write them as promises on screen. Questions are fine ("Where would your group sit?").
4. **Banned words and numbers:** VIP, best, number-one, guaranteed, star ratings, and any count of reviews, followers or guests.
5. **People:** no identifiable faces without consent. Backs of heads and crowds only. No children.
6. **Truth labels:** footage from 2022 must say 2022; footage from 2024 must say 2024. Photos from 19 Feb 2023 may show their real camera times. Nothing may claim to show something it doesn't (date, place, moment).
7. **Format:** 9:16, 1080x1920, 15–20 s. All readable text between y=288 and y=1344 px (Instagram's interface covers the top and bottom). It must read with the sound off.
8. **Calls to action:** a send-to-a-friend prompt, plus "Link in bio". Use the button wording "Talk to a Nerd" only if TripNerd confirms that page exists. No "DM us" (nobody covers DMs yet).
9. **The race name** ("Daytona 500") goes in the caption only, and only with: "TripNerd is not sponsored by, affiliated with, or a partner of NASCAR or The Daytona 500." On screen, use "Daytona Beach".
10. **Sound:** the real race audio from Videos F and G comes first; licensed sound effects and music only fill gaps, plus the real room tone from Video E. Sound must match the picture: a single car's burnout is not "the field passing", and F/G audio stays on F/G pictures (or bridges into the next beat as a short J-cut or L-cut). Music: only sounds cleared for business accounts (Meta Sound Collection).

11. **One year per story.** Never present footage from different years as one day. The clock may run only over Photos A and B (both 19 Feb 2023). Videos F and G (2024) and Video E (2022) sit outside the clock with their year label on screen, or the clock is dropped. Copy may not say "one race day" if more than one year is shown.
