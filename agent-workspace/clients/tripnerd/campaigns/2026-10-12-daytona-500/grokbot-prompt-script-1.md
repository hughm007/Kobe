You are a senior performance creative director and Reels editor for a premium sports-hospitality brand. Improve the Instagram Reel script below. You cannot see the media, so I describe every file exactly. Use **only** the media listed. Do not invent shots, footage or facts.

## 1. The client and the goal
- **Client:** TripNerd (TripNerd, LLC, Chicago). It sells fan-experience packages and corporate hospitality at major sports events.
- **This Reel sells:** the Daytona 500 package for **February 21, 2027**, Daytona Beach, Florida.
- **Account:** @tripnerd on Instagram, about 4,700 followers. Organic Reel, posting Friday.
- **Audiences:**
  1. Race fans and friend groups who might book a race-day trip (primary for this Reel).
  2. Corporate hosts (secondary).
- **What counts as success, in order:** 3-second hold and reach to non-followers; sends (shares by DM); profile visits and follows; enquiries via "Talk to a Nerd" (link in bio).
- **Standard to beat:** TripNerd's owner approved a 25-second Reel called "Their Camera Roll". It shows real guest photos from several events as a phone camera roll (grid, tap, swipe, a suite video that "comes alive", event list, brand-blue end card). This new Reel should feel fresh next to it: no grid, no camera-roll interface.

## 2. Hard rules (do not break any of them)
1. **Real media only.** No AI-generated people, cars, crowds or scenery. No AI animation, interpolation or AI upscaling of the photos. Text and graphics are added in editing.
2. **No third-party marks on screen:** no NASCAR, speedway, sponsor or team logos. Where they appear in a photo, they are hand-blurred or cropped.
3. **No claims without written proof from TripNerd.** Not yet confirmed: seats over pit road for 2027, the hospitality suite, the oceanfront hotel, track access, garage tours. Do not write them as promises on screen. Questions are fine ("Where would your group sit?").
4. **Banned words:** VIP, best, number-one, guaranteed, star ratings, counts.
5. **People:** no identifiable faces without consent. Backs of heads and crowds only. No children.
6. **Truth labels:** footage from 2022 must say 2022. Photos from 19 Feb 2023 may show their real camera times. Nothing may claim to show something it doesn't (date, place, moment).
7. **Format:** 9:16, 1080x1920, 15–20 s. All readable text between y=288 and y=1344 px (Instagram's interface covers the top and bottom). It must read with the sound off.
8. **Calls to action:** a send-to-a-friend prompt, plus "Talk to a Nerd". No "DM us" (nobody covers DMs yet).
9. **The race name** ("Daytona 500") goes in the caption only, and only with: "TripNerd is not sponsored by, affiliated with, or a partner of NASCAR or The Daytona 500." On screen, use "Daytona Beach".
10. **Sound:** licensed sound effects and music, plus the real room tone from TripNerd's video. Sound must match the picture: a single car's burnout is not "the field passing".

## 5. Known weaknesses (an independent review predicts this cut at about 7.2/10; 8 is the bar)
- **Mostly stills.** The only real motion is a pre-race 2022 video with empty stands, so the "race day" feeling depends on stills and sound.
- **No faces:** the story has no one person to follow through the day.
- **Late offer:** the 2027 offer appears at 9 s; the payoff beats (7–8) are the weakest pictures.
- **Corporate hosts** get nothing specific.
- **Soft sources:** the photos are 1024-px web copies, so big crops look soft.
- **Familiar end card:** it repeats the look of the approved "Their Camera Roll" Reel.

## 6. What I want from you
1. **Diagnose:** in five bullets, what would make a non-follower stop, watch to the end and send it, and where this cut loses them. Be blunt.
2. **Rewrite:** produce an improved version of the script using only the media in section 3 and all the rules in section 2. Same table format: time, picture with the exact source and seconds, big text, caption, sound. Aim for 14–18 s.
3. **Hooks:** give three alternative opening-2-second hooks (picture + text + sound) built on Photo A or Photo B, and say which two you would A/B test and why.
4. **Copy:**
   - five caption first lines (under 125 characters, no banned words);
   - three end-card headlines;
   - one send-prompt line.
5. **Sound:** a short beat-by-beat sound plan that matches each picture.
6. **Sanity check:** for every change, check it against the ten rules and flag anything that would need TripNerd's written confirmation or consent.

**Do not:**
- add footage, people, cars or places that are not in section 3;
- promise anything in rule 3;
- use the race name on screen.
