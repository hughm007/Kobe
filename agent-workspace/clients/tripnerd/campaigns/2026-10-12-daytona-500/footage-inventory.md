# Daytona 500: real TripNerd material (checked 2026-10-12)

**Method (FACT):**
- **The video:** downloaded from Higgsfield storage into the Higgsfield sandbox (MD5 matches the 2026-10-06 record). Every second viewed (1 frame per second), key frames at 640 px, a scene-cut scan, and the audio level and speech transcription checked.
- **Photos:** all Daytona images on tripnerd.com (event page and the NERDS gallery) downloaded at full size, viewed at 760 px, and their camera data read.
- **Drive:** searched for Daytona material.

**Not done:**
- Nothing was listened to by ear.
- The two Drive videos could not be opened (see C).
- Nothing was generated or spent.

## The short version
- **Three race days on record:** 2022, 2023 and 2024.
- **There is no real race-action video in hand.** The only video we have watched is pre-race. Race moments exist only as photos, plus two unreviewed Drive clips from race day 2024.
- **Strongest real moments:**
  - the TripNerd tent with the TripNerd sign;
  - the climb up to the seats over pit road;
  - the jet flyover above the grandstand, shot from the seats;
  - guests on the track at the start/finish line wearing credentials;
  - pit road at dusk;
  - a car smoking its tyres under the lights.
- **Biggest limits:**
  - **Resolution.** The video is 1280x720 16:9; the photos are about 1024 px on the long side.
  - **Brands everywhere.** Speedway and sponsor marks are in almost every frame.
  - **Consent.** Recognisable guests, including at least one child, and probably teenagers in the track photo.

## E. Rules for any Daytona cut
1. **Event name.** No "Daytona 500", NASCAR or speedway marks on screen until owner decision 2. Default on screen: "Daytona Beach · February". If TripNerd wants the race named, use their page disclaimer word for word.
2. **Sponsor and team marks** (Love's, Sunoco, Lexmark, AdventHealth, Hendrick, Chevrolet, McDonald's, Goodyear) cropped or passed over. Never the TV broadcast.
3. **Faces.**
   - Use backs, wides and crowds by default.
   - Gallery_08 and gallery_020 need consent, and parent consent for the minors.
   - The man in the video needs a release, or start after 6.0 s.
4. **Years.** The material spans 2022–2024. Never say "this year" or "last year", or imply the photos are from one day, unless they are. Script 1 uses 2023 only for that reason.
5. **Real material only.**
   - **Allowed touch-ups:** upscaling, grading, cropping.
   - **No generation:** nothing generated, no altered people. Engine sound comes from a licensed library or the real 2024 clips.
