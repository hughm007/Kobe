## 1. What I could and could not verify

**Checked (FACT):**
- Every draft was decoded in full.
- Contact sheets at 2 frames per second, plus 4 per second for D1.
- Dense strips at 6–10 frames per second at every problem moment, and full-size crops of every flag, pin, ball, badge and end card.
- Cut maps, and still holds of half a second or longer.
- Loudness: integrated level, loudness range and true peak.
- Each soundtrack split into a voice/crowd stem and a music/ambience stem. Each stem was transcribed offline (Whisper base.en) and its level measured every quarter or half second.

**Not checked:**
- **I cannot listen.** Voice naturalness, music taste and fit, and how the mix *feels* are not judged by ear. Audio scores rest on transcripts, levels and timing only.
- **The approved references could not be opened.** The storage host is blocked from this session, and neither file was uploaded. The comparisons use the approved adverts' build records and your recorded verdicts:
  - the hosting spot, 8.4, 16:9;
  - THEIR CAMERA ROLL, ranked above it: all real material, motion design.

  So the comparison column is provisional.
- **Where the footage came from:**
  - **D2 and D5's suite footage looks real.** Several shots aren't in our register (the cheering in the suite, the cup, guests' faces). They are probably your raw suite clips (ASSUMPTION).
  - **D5's generated putt shots appear to be our September 17th-hole generations** (putter, stroke, ball drop). Not confirmed.
  - **D3 and D4 are almost entirely generated.** The exception is D3's brick house, which matches the tripnerd.com house photo.

## 2. Recommendation

**Work on D5, "Your suite. On 17.", first. Use D2 as the backup. Archive D1 and D3. Park D4's idea.**

- D5 has the clearest offer of the five: a suite on 17, the roar, bring your people, Talk to a Nerd. It also has the end card that matches our approved work, and its weaknesses are mostly *subtractive*.
- Cut its 6.3 s of generated golf. That removes the weather mismatch, the putt that happens twice and the only artificial-looking shots in one move. What's left is an all-real advert in the style you ranked highest (THEIR CAMERA ROLL).
- Estimated: no generation credits; about half a day of editing.
- D2 is the same story told shorter and more naturally. It is the fallback if D5's re-cut fails its stopping rule.

### D1, ROAR (8 s) · ARCHIVE
**In one sentence:** a sound-led loop (quiet, then roar, then "Hospitality. Handled.") meant to stop the scroll. **Purpose:** awareness. **CTA:** a handle only, which is acceptable for awareness.

**Scores:**

### D4, "The back of someone's hat" (20 s) · REBUILD THE IDEA (parked)
**In one sentence:** "you made it, but all you can see is someone's hat; experience it differently with TripNerd." **Purpose:** awareness. **CTA:** "Explore your next event" is generic and mismatched with "The Augusta Experience".

**Scores:**
- **Hook 7:** the best hook of the five. A universal pain, said in the voiceover and shown immediately.
- **Idea 4:** **the payoff contradicts the promise.** The "different" experience is guests in lawn chairs at the rope (11.0–15.5 s), which is what any patron does. TripNerd's Augusta package is course passes, a private home and hospitality, not a better view. As cut, it implies a viewing advantage TripNerd doesn't sell (**misleading-claim risk**).
- **Story 4.5:** see the critical problems below.
- **Visual 4:** see the critical problems below.
- **Audio 5.5:** a clean, complete voiceover (it transcribes word for word) with the music almost absent (−45 dBFS). Whether the voice sounds human was **not judged by ear**.
- **Branding 4.5:** the real logo on the end card, but a low-contrast button and **no non-affiliation line**, while the imagery reads as the Augusta course.

**Critical problems:**
- **The putt (8.4–10.9 s) has no golfer.** The ball enters from above at 9.6 s, rolls to the lip and stops; it never drops.
- **The two women's jump-and-hug (11.0–12.8 s) celebrates that miss.** The seated man faces the camera, not the green.
- **A white framed-photo flash (13.0–13.5 s)** reads as a template effect.
- **Footprints are pressed into the putting green.**
- **Visual trade dress:** azaleas, pines, a yellow flag and a green-clad gallery, with no non-affiliation line.

**Why rebuild, not fix:** the hook deserves a home on an event where TripNerd *does* sell a better view: the suite on 17, from real gallery and suite footage.

**Why parked:**
- That rebuild is the "Two ways" angle you stopped on 2026-10-06.
- It would split effort away from D5.
- The real gallery clips are 404x720.

**In one sentence:** a bored man texts TripNerd "AUGUSTA", gets "handled", and ends up at the party ("Way better than the couch"). **Purpose:** enquiries. **CTA:** DM "AUGUSTA". That fits the purpose, but **nobody is set up to answer DMs yet**, so the CTA makes a promise nobody covers.

- **Story 4:**
  - a 2.1 s static chat hold (5.2–7.3 s);

  - a 3.8 s course hold;
  - a 3.8 s end card;
  - the phone screen's messages (3.2 s) differ from the full-screen chat (3.5–7.3 s), and the phone's own text adds an unverified "practice round access" claim.
- **Visual 3:** see the critical problems below.
- **Audio 4:** a music bed, plus one line spoken by a generated guest. That is a synthetic testimonial; it needs a label at minimum.
- **Branding 5:** a clean end card *with* a non-affiliation line, which is the one thing it does better than the others.

**Critical problems:**
- **Two pins on one green** (21.0–23.3 s; confirmed at full size).
- **The course reads as Augusta National:** azaleas, a creek, a yellow flag.
- **A cloned-looking gallery:** identical blue shirts and identical stances.

**Why archive:**
- **AI-led:** it runs against your own standing rule that real client material comes first.
- **Needs new generation:** fixing the double pin and the course's resemblance to Augusta National needs new shots.
- **Better versions exist:** the "DM a keyword" mechanic is better tested later on real footage, once someone is covering DMs.
- **Worth keeping:** the real house photo and the end-card disclaimer wording.

## 6. Stopping rule

**Stop investing in D5 and switch to D2 if any of these happen:**
1. **The real roar won't carry it.** After E1–E2, the opening 3 s and the real putt-to-roar section don't hold attention without the generated golf: the re-cut's quality review scores under 7.5, or your own watch says the middle sags.
2. **The roar can't be synced.** It can't be lined up with the real crowd rising to within about 0.3 s (no raw clip, and the separated audio smears).
3. **The cleanup guts the cut.** Removing the tournament marks (E3, E5, E6) and the unconsented faces (E7) leaves under about 18 s of strong material.
4. **It runs over budget.** The essential edits (E1–E8) take more than one working day.

**For D2, the backup:** stop if its revised cut doesn't reach 7.0 after fixes 1–4. At that point, stop cutting 17th-suite Reels from this footage. Return to the Camera Roll format, or wait for Taylor's native clips.
