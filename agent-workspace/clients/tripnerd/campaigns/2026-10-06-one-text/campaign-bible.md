## 2. Strategy (skipped — answered by the 90-day plan)

first step with TripNerd is one message. Offer verdict: not applicable (no price or package

## 3. Concept and pack
**One Text** — owner-selected 2026-10-06 from a five-concept pack (concept #2). One idea: *it
starts with one text.* World: one man's group trip, seen almost entirely from behind.
Angle rotation vs live ads (BC-24): no island green, no host to camera, no photo grid, no
slogan reuse; opens on a message, not a venue.

| Hook variant | Opening line (bubble 1) | Status |
|---|---|---|
| A (main) | "6 of us. Can you make it happen?" | built |
| B | "Need 6 seats and zero stress." | copy only — "zero stress" is an absolute; Skeptic to rule |
| C | "Can you sort the whole trip? There's 6 of us." | copy only |

Body and end card are shared; only bubble 1 changes per variant.

## 4–5. Spine and script
No voice-over, no dialogue (no generated voice — p.6). Text carries the story:
1. Bubble out: **"6 of us. Can you make it happen?"** → typing dots → TripNerd bubble: **"On it."**
2. Journey, no text.
3. End card: **"It starts with one text."** / **"DM @tripnerd with your group size."**

Claim check: "On it." promises effort, not availability. "It starts with one text." describes
the first step only (a booking takes more than one message — the line does not say otherwise).

## 7. Cast and performance
All people are **generated actors** (scenario ad, never customers, no testimonial, no names,
no faces in readable close-up except none — the hero is shown only from behind). Realism floor:
no celebrating, cheering, hugging or clapping at readable distance; the payoff is stillness at
the rail with a hand on the shoulder.

## 8. Continuity annex
Hero wardrobe: navy polo with white collar, navy shorts, white sneakers, short brown hair.
Light: late afternoon, sun from frame left, warm. Venue: invented clubhouse — white panelling,
woven wicker pendants, beamed ceiling, open French doors to lagoon and course (keyframes 145,
144 share this room). Shot 1 is at home: only hands and a grey sleeve are seen.
