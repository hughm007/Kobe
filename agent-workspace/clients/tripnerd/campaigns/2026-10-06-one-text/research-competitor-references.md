# TripNerd "One Text": edit rules for a 15s 9:16 Reel

**Read this first.** None of the 27 reference items below was verified live. facebook.com, tiktok.com, instagram.com, youtube.com and every trade-press domain were blocked, and the shared web-search budget ran out. No ad was watched. Meta's Ad Library shows no likes or views for commercial ads, and no start dates or run lengths were recorded. That means **no competitor ad in this research has public proof of strong views, likes or comments.** The rules below rest on published platform and field studies, and none of those studies covers sports hospitality.

**How rules are labelled**
- **PROVEN (cite):** backed by published platform or field data that states a method or sample. These sources were seen only as search snippets and were not opened. Treat this label as "sourced", not "confirmed".
- **REASONABLE HYPOTHESIS:** supported only by indirect evidence (another category, a survey, unverified snippet numbers, opinion) or by the concept's own logic.
- **NEEDS TESTING:** no evidence, or the evidence conflicts. Settle it with an A/B test.
- **TRIPNERD RULE / POLICY:** a fixed constraint, not a claim about performance.

## 2) Recommended cut: **B (mix)**, with one change to the hook

**Why B over A**
- **A conflicts with TripNerd's own rules.** AI may "only bridge between real shots", and an all-AI cut has no real shots to bridge. A venue day built by AI either shows AI people, which is banned, or shows AI venues and food as if TripNerd delivered them. *TRIPNERD RULE.*
- **AI people do as well only when viewers can't tell.** The large field studies (Taboola/Columbia/Harvard 2026; Exner et al. "AI in disguise", 16B impressions) found AI imagery matched or beat human-made imagery only when it didn't look like AI. Both studies cover display ads, not Meta video. *PROVEN (cite, other format).*
- **Labels cost engagement.** Meta's policy (newsroom snippet) puts an "AI info" label next to "Sponsored" when an ad shows a photorealistic AI human. Copenhagen Business School (8 experiments plus 1M+ TikTok posts) found AI-labelled posts got about 7–8% fewer likes, worst for emotional content. USC's 6 preregistered studies point the same way. One CHI 2025 study (n=911) found no change. *PROVEN (cite), mixed.*
- **High-ticket, experiential buyers distrust AI.** WSU (6 experiments): mentioning AI lowered purchase intent most for expensive purchases. Travel surveys from Icelandair, Talker Research and Raptive point the same way, but they are surveys. *REASONABLE HYPOTHESIS.*
- Hive.co (opinion): fully AI creative "isn't realistic for events". Tourism Malaysia and Trivago pulled AI videos after backlash (unverified news anecdotes). *REASONABLE HYPOTHESIS.*

**The change to the hook.** B's AI hands-only POV hook has two problems:
1. It opens the ad, so it does not sit "between real shots".
2. AI hands may count as "a person" under TripNerd's rule, and may trigger Meta's photorealistic-human label (unconfirmed).

Better options, in order:
1. Real footage of a real phone in a real hand, shot by TripNerd.
2. A thread graphic composited in TripNerd's own design, with no generative AI.

The research's policy reading rates both as lower risk. Keep AI hands only if the APPROVER rules them acceptable, they are labelled, and the SPEND_APPROVER clears the generation.

## 3) Shot-by-shot edit rules (cut B, 15.0s)

| Shot | Time | Source | Picture | On-screen text | Audio |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | 0.0–1.2s | Real phone in a real hand, or a composited graphic (AI hands only as above) | Thread, fully visible on frame 1 with no typing animation | Bubble: "6 of us. Can you make it happen?" | Commercial-library track starts |
| S2 | 1.2–2.2s | Same | Reply arrives from "TripNerd" (the brand's first appearance) | Bubble: "On it." | Music |
| S3 | 2.2–3.0s | **Real TripNerd footage** | Hard cut to the strongest real arrival, access or reveal moment | Optional one-line location super: event or venue name, only to say where guests went | Music plus real ambience |
| S4 | 3.0–12.5s | **Real TripNerd footage only** | The group's day in order: arrival → hospitality → the event moment → the group reacting. Includes at least one large, clear real face. | Burned-in captions for any speech. Few or no other supers. | Music plus real speech or ambience, or a real human VO |
| S5 | 12.5–15.0s | Composite using the real TripNerd logo file | End card | "It starts with one text." / "DM @tripnerd with your group size." | Music; CTA spoken only if a real voice was recorded |

**Rules**
1. **Format.** 9:16, designed for sound on, all text inside Meta's Reels safe zones (exact dimensions are not in this research; take them from Meta's current spec). *PROVEN (Meta 2024 internal data: 9:16 plus sound on plus safe zones plus one extra element gave 56% higher CTR and 20% lower CPC; sample not stated).*
2. **Length 15s.** *REASONABLE HYPOTHESIS (Meta's ≤15s advice for top-of-funnel Reels, seen second-hand).* A 10s cutdown *NEEDS TESTING* (posh.vip, opinion: ≤10s for event ads).
3. **Hook line readable on frame 1, no typing animation.** *REASONABLE HYPOTHESIS (Facebook/Nielsen 2015, 173 studies: up to 47% of a campaign's value came in the first 3s; TikTok: over 63% of top-CTR videos show the key message within 3s).*
4. **Thread resolved and first real venue shot on screen by about 3.0s.** *REASONABLE HYPOTHESIS (research read-out: a real crowd, access or reveal moment within 1–3s).*
5. **Brand and core message by 5s.** The "TripNerd" reply lands at 1.2s. *PROVEN (Meta × Toluna: 1.7x; 100 CPG/e-commerce ads, correlational, forced exposure).*
6. **Brand shown more than once, but on screen for under 25% of runtime (≤3.75s in total).** Budget about 1.0s for the S2 reply label plus 2.5s for the end card, and do not keep a persistent brand header on the thread. *PROVEN (Toluna: 1.8x for repeat branding; 4.8x for branding under 25% of runtime).*
7. **Thread design.** Unbranded and in TripNerd's own style. No OS status bar, no push-notification banner, no reply or send box, nothing that looks tappable, no iMessage, WhatsApp or Instagram DM look, and no Apple glyphs. *POLICY (Meta Ad Standards rules on non-existent functionality and UI screenshots; TikTok misleading-content policy; Apple trademark guidelines; all from snippets).*
8. **Everything after the hook is real TripNerd footage. No AI fill, no AI venue, food or room.** *TRIPNERD RULE, with the PROVEN cites from section 2.*
9. **Lead with people.** Real guests or hosts experiencing the day, with at least one large, clear real face. *REASONABLE HYPOTHESIS (Taboola: a large human face was the strongest trust cue, in display ads; organic snippets: the person-led tours in items 8–9 drew about 182K and 244K likes vs 55–62 for posts led by price or inclusions; unverified and a small sample).*
10. **Show benefits in the picture rather than a price or inclusions list.** Whether to add one short benefit super *NEEDS TESTING*: Toluna found benefit or feature messaging 5.3x more likely to rank top, while the organic posts led by price or inclusions did poorly. Any benefit super is a claim and goes through the claims-and-proof policy.
11. **Events.** Event names appear only to say where guests went. Crop or blur any legible event logo, or don't use the shot. *TRIPNERD RULE.*
12. **Text.** Keep supers short. *REASONABLE HYPOTHESIS (TikTok: 40% of top-view-rate ads use concise text overlays; descriptive, not a measured lift).* An emoji in the thread *NEEDS TESTING* (Toluna: 2.5x for native emoji, CPG only).
13. **Captions.** Burn in captions for every line of speech or VO, and let the on-screen text carry the story with sound off. *PROVEN (Facebook internal tests 2016: +12% view time; Verizon/Publicis survey of 5,616 adults: 80% more likely to finish a video with captions; both from before Reels).*
14. **Music.** Instagram's commercial library only (*TRIPNERD RULE*). Pair music with real speech: a real VO, or real dialogue or ambience from the footage. *PROVEN (Toluna: 2.1x for speech plus music; Meta Cannes 2023: music plus VO scored about 15 higher in positive response, method not shown).* Never an AI voice (*TRIPNERD RULE*). Track and tempo: *NEEDS TESTING* (no evidence).
15. **CTA on screen for about 2.5s, spoken too if a real voice exists.** *PROVEN (Toluna: 1.9x for a visual or spoken CTA).* Keep the DM CTA because it closes the "one text" loop. A comment-keyword variant (keyword chosen by the APPROVER) *NEEDS TESTING*. The workspace record shows Golfbreaks used a comment keyword on every active ad found, but that was not re-checked live.
16. **If any AI shot survives:** label it per TripNerd's rule and keep the AI seconds to a minimum. Label wording that shows the work put in may soften the engagement hit. *REASONABLE HYPOTHESIS (USC).* The size of the effect *NEEDS TESTING* (CHI found no change).
17. **Hook test.** H1: thread on a plain background, as planned. H2: a real venue moment from frame 1 with both bubbles composited over it. *NEEDS TESTING.* No source compares text-thread openers with other openers. In Benly's travel-and-hospitality data (9.8K creatives), lifespans by hook type ranged only from 43 to 48 days.
18. **Gates.** Any AI generation needs SPEND_APPROVER sign-off. Delivery follows the canonical blocking-check registry. Validate everything with TripNerd's own A/B tests and treat how long an ad keeps running as the performance proxy.

## 5) What could not be verified
- **Every reference item.** All 27 are unverified live, because of egress blocks and the exhausted search budget. No ad was watched, so every hook, every on-screen format and every real-vs-AI judgement is unknown.
- **Competitor performance.** No Meta Ad Library start dates or run lengths exist, so the 30+ day longevity test is unverified for every competitor ad. Meta shows no likes or views for commercial ads. "Proven high-engagement competitor Meta ads" therefore cannot be established from this research.
- **Direct competitors.** No ad-level data was found for On Location, PrimeSport, Quint, Elite Sports Tours, Roadtrips, Sports Traveler, Event Hospitality Group or Derby Experiences. The 2026-10-05 pull found no relevant active video ads for the first three.
- **Organic numbers.** All likes and comments are snippet snapshots of unknown age, sometimes with two conflicting readings. There are no view counts, and no Instagram Reels; every item is a TikTok post.
- **Text-thread openers.** No travel or hospitality direct-response ad was confirmed to open on a text-thread UI. The two sources on Abu Dhabi's opening contradict each other.
- **Edit craft.** The research has no evidence on shot count, shot length, cutting on the beat, which track to use, or caption styling.
- **Applicability of the studies.** Toluna, the Meta 2024 setup data, Facebook/Nielsen, the TikTok tips and the caption studies cover other categories and older formats, and their pages were not opened.
- **Meta's AI-label rules.** Not confirmed on Meta's own pages, including the reported July 2026 update and whether AI hands count as a "photorealistic human". The Meta UI-policy text also comes from snippets only.
- **For the APPROVER to decide:**
  - Whether AI hands breach TripNerd's "no AI person" rule.
  - Whether a staged customer text needs a dramatisation note under the realism-and-disclosure policy. This research does not address it.
- **Not covered by the research:**
  - What real TripNerd footage exists, for example a group of about 6 at a venue.
  - Rights and releases for the people and event footage in it.
- **Unverifiable workspace claims.** "Golfbreaks creator Reel 7.4x median" and "On Location people-on-camera Reels 10x brand graphics".
- **Other unverified figures.** Accor's +15.3 pts recall, the Coca-Cola System1 and Kantar figures, and the Malaysia and Trivago takedowns.
