# "Door to roar": script v2 (build-ready)

**Your idea, refined:** one guest's day, from the door to the roar and back out the door.
- **First half (Higgsfield):** *it's ready before you are.* Empty, prepared spaces waiting for the guest.
- **Second half (TripNerd's real footage):** the people, the main event and the roar.

**Format:**
- Instagram Reel, 9:16, 1080×1920, about 26 s.
- Sound on, but it must work with sound off.

**Rules for this build (your decisions, 5 Oct):**
1. **Higgsfield makes only the 7 frames marked HF.** None of them contains a person, hand or face. TripNerd's agreed rule is "AI never creates a person, a voice or a testimonial."
2. **Every beat with people is TripNerd's real footage.** The table and the food are real photos we already have (sent with this file).
3. **No text or logos inside any generated frame.** Add all on-screen text and the TripNerd logo in your editor, from the real logo file. AI garbles text and logos.
4. **AI frames carry no factual claims.** "Ours:" proof lines go only on real shots.
5. **Turn on Instagram's AI label** when posting, because the Reel contains generated frames.

### The "best trip ever" line (your end card idea)
It can't be generated, written into a fictional guest's mouth, or put on screen as if a guest said it. It **can** be real:
1. At TripNerd's next event, film the group walking out (backs or fronts, with their consent).
2. Ask "How was today?" and don't feed them the line.
3. If someone says "best trip ever" on their own, keep it.
4. They sign a release.
5. It replaces beat 14 as their real audio, with their first name on screen.

## 2. The 7 Higgsfield frames: how to make each one

**Workflow for each frame (cheapest first):**
1. Make the **still** with the image prompt.
2. Generate 2–4 options and keep the best.
3. Animate the still with the **video prompt** (image-to-video, start frame = your still).

**Video settings for all 7 frames:**

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Aspect | **9:16** |
| Resolution | **1080p, native** |
| Audio | **Off** (sound is added in the edit) |
| Length | Generate the model's minimum (4–5 s); use 1.4–2.2 s in the edit |

**Models:**
- **Use:** **Seedance 2.5** or **Cinema Studio Video 3.0**, both at 1080p (checked in Higgsfield's live model list, 5 Oct). Seedance 2.5 can make a cheap 480p preview, then finish the same take at 1080p.
- **Avoid:** Grok Imagine Lite (its 1080p is upscaled from 720p) and MiniMax H3 Max (768p maximum).

**One look across all 7 frames:**
- documentary phone-video realism;
- natural colour; daylight 5600K unless a frame says otherwise;
- gentle handheld;
- shallow depth of field;
- empty spaces with clean, plain surfaces.

### HF-1: Doors at dawn (beat 1)
**Image prompt**
```
SCENE CONTEXT
Dawn outside a clubhouse-style hospitality building before guests arrive. Glass double doors, warm light glowing inside, the entrance completely empty.

LOCATION MAP
Foreground: dewy stone path leading to the doors. Midground: glass double doors with plain dark frames, centred. Background: warm interior glow, soft and out of focus. Low sun from camera left.

FIRST FRAME / BLOCKING
Vertical 9:16 frame, doors centred in the upper two-thirds, path leading up from the bottom edge. The scene holds only architecture, light and dew.

OPTICS
Wide shot, 47° field of view, rectilinear, gentle depth of field.

LIGHTING
Cool blue dawn sky at 6500K outside, warm 3200K interior light spilling through the glass, thin morning haze at 20%.

STYLE
Photoreal, documentary phone-camera look, natural colour, fine grain.

POSITIVE LOCKS
Empty path and empty doorway. Plain glass and plain frames with clean, unmarked surfaces.
```
**Video prompt** (start frame = your still)
```
One continuous shot, the camera does not cut on its own. Handheld camera at chest height walks slowly toward the glass doors at 2 km/h, gentle natural sway. Morning haze drifts slowly left to right. Warm light inside brightens slightly as the camera nears. The path and doorway stay empty for the whole shot. Photoreal documentary phone-video look, natural colour.
```

### Reject a take if…
- **Anyone appears**, even a silhouette, or a hand.
- **Any text appears**: letters, numbers, logos, signs, flags or labels.
- The scene looks like a **real, recognisable course or event** (an island green ringed by water, grandstands with branding, a scoreboard).
- The grass shimmers or warps, chairs melt, glasses deform, or liquid behaves strangely.
- It looks plasticky or over-sharpened next to the real phone footage.
- **Resolution: native 1080p only.** Never upscale a 720p take.

## 3. Building it in your editor

**On-screen text:**
- Montserrat ExtraBold.
- **Left-aligned** at about x 60.
- Keep it **above 80% of the height** and **clear of the right edge** (x ≤ 950), where Instagram's buttons sit.
- Navy #202838 text on a white or site-blue #5896E9 plate, or white text on navy.

**Logo:** the real file only (`brand-assets/tripnerd-logo-colour-1633x601.png`), placed only on site blue.

**Real stills:**
- Beat 2 (table): pan only. The crop is already at full resolution, so no zoom.
- Beat 6 (food): push at most 114%.

**Audio:**
- Real room tone, murmur and the real roar from V24.
- No AI voice, no AI music.
- If you add music, use only a commercial-use track (Instagram's commercial library).

**Export:** H.264, 1080×1920, about 18 Mbps; loudness about −14 LUFS.

**Posting:** turn on Instagram's AI-content label. Send the cut back here for the checks before it goes to TripNerd's approver.
