# "Door to roar": script v1

**Idea:** one guest's day, from the door to the roar and back out the door.
**Format:** Instagram Reel · 9:16, 1080×1920 · 30.0 s · sound on, but it must work with sound off.

## 0. Read first: why Grok does not make this advert

Karl asked for Grok to generate this advert (AI people arriving, receiving a "VIP lanyard", eating, drinking, a group walking out saying "this was the best trip ever"). That version can't be posted, for four reasons:

| Problem | Source |
|---|---|
| **TripNerd's own agreed rule:** "AI never creates a person, a voice or a testimonial." Every beat of this idea is people | [`grok/02-knowledge-file.md`](../../grok/02-knowledge-file.md) §6 (from the plan TripNerd saw) |
| **The end card is a fake testimonial.** Generated people saying "this was the best trip ever" is a synthetic customer endorsement. Disclosure does not cure it (FTC Consumer Reviews and Testimonials Rule). It is the same form that killed "The Parking Lot" in August | `_servicepow/policies/realism-and-disclosure.md` §2; [`client-brief.md`](../../client-brief.md) |
| **It would show services nobody has confirmed.** Car pickup has no record, and "TripNerd handles transport" is barred. "VIP" is barred. Whether guests get a credential from TripNerd is unknown | Knowledge file §5 and §10 |
| **It breaks the standing rule from TripNerd's own approvals:** "Real client material first" and "never build a finished advert from footage below 1080p". Generated video is not real footage, and TripNerd docked the Hosting spot for exactly that softness | Knowledge file §9 |

**What Grok does instead (section 5):**
- drafts the storyboard;
- makes empty-scene concept frames (no people) for an internal pitch;
- drafts the shot list and the questions for TripNerd.

The finished Reel is cut from TripNerd's real footage, in our pipeline, through the normal checks.

### The "best trip ever" end card

**Only a real guest can say it.**
1. At the event, ask the group on the way out: "How was today?" Don't feed them the line.
2. If someone says something like "best trip ever" unprompted, film it.
3. They sign a release.
4. It runs as their real audio, with their first name on screen.

Then it's a genuine endorsement: allowed, and much stronger than a scripted line. Until then, beat 15 runs as written.
