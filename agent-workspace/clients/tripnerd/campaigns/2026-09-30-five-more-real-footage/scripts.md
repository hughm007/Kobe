# Five more, from real material (concept pack, draft only: no spend, no generation)

**Family:** THEIR CAMERA ROLL, approved 2026-09-29 and ranked by the owner above the 8.4 hosting spot (`../../Client approved adverts/2026-09-29-their-camera-roll-25s.md`). Decision 0008 makes real client material the default picture source. The renderer, sound pipeline and end card already exist (`../2026-09-29-all-events/build-v1/`).

**Angle rotation.** The last three angles were *Hospitality. Handled.* (host the clients), *YOUR PEOPLE* (who you bring; killed) and *breadth/envy* (the camera roll). These five use time, proof, product clarity, qualification and real voices. None revives YOUR PEOPLE.

**Anti-generic gate (logo swap and memory test).** Each idea rests on material only TripNerd has: its photos, its suite, its published package lines, its reviews. A competitor could copy the device but not the content.

**Scores are my judgment (ESTIMATE), not performance data.** They weigh fit to the approved pattern, buildability today, rights friction and likely commercial role.

**Inventory these rely on:** `real-material-inventory.md`, read 2026-09-30. The website adds 15 attributed Google reviews, published package lines per event and five Vimeo videos that were not in the register.

## Rules every script follows
- Every picture is TripNerd's own material. Higgsfield and Adobe are used for touch-up only (upscale, tone, reframing). No generated people, venues or voices.
- Nothing shows third-party marks (tournament names and logos, sponsor boards, broadcast graphics). Event names appear as shorthand unless TripNerd approves full names with its own non-affiliation wording.
- **No star ratings, review counts, "#1", "VIP", "best" or "guarantee"**: none is evidenced.
- Guest faces need TripNerd's confirmation of consent for paid use. A child's photo needs a parent's.
- Music is licensed before paid use. The synthesized bed is a placeholder.
- Any "included" line is a DRAFT line until TripNerd confirms it in writing (an Evidence Record).
- Timing is film time at 30 fps, 9:16. Every idea ships as a master and a 12–15 s cutdown, with the same end card as the approved advert.

## 4 · HOW MANY ARE YOU BRINGING? (15 s, plus four variants) · 7.0 (ESTIMATE)

**Role:** qualification and modular variants. **Angle:** personalize the pitch by group size. The website form asks **Group Size: 1–4 · 5–10 · 10–20 · 20+**, so the ad and the destination match (ad-to-destination parity). **Hypothesis:** an opening question about the viewer's own group lifts lead quality, and four variants that each open on one size let the performance team match audience to size (**UNPROVEN**).

**Device:** a story-style poll sticker with the four options from the form. A finger picks one and the answer cuts to real photos of a group that size.

| Time | Picture | Source (real) | Text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–1.2 | The poll appears over the grid | composed | **How many are you bringing?** |
| 1.2–4.0 | **1–4:** three quick cuts | Phoenix couple (gallery 030), Sawgrass trio (085), Derby four (09) | 1–4 |
| 4.0–6.5 | **5–10:** logo-wall groups | gallery 084, 079 | 5–10 |
| 6.5–9.0 | **10–20:** the largest logo-wall group | gallery 038 (**count at full size first**) | 10–20 |
| 9.0–10.5 | **20+:** no real photo, so text only | composed | 20+ → *ask a Nerd* |
| 10.5–12.5 | Everything pulls back into the grid | composed | **Whatever your group, tell a Nerd.** |
| 12.5–15.0 | End card with the pill tapped | real logo file | Tell a Nerd your group size · tripnerd.com |

**Variants:** four 15 s versions, each opening on one size and ending with that size in the card. They reuse the same body.

**Checks:**
- **A poll the viewer cannot tap can be flagged as simulated functionality on some platforms.** Build it as a finger demo, and check Meta's and TikTok's current rules before spend (**UNVERIFIED**).
- Count the people in each photo at full size before labeling it 5–10 or 10–20.
- We show no 20+ photo, because none is real.
- The same consent rule for guest faces applies.

**Build:** one session plus almost nothing per variant (ESTIMATE). Generation spend 0.

## 5 · THEIR CAMERA ROLL, LITERALLY: guests' own clips and photos (25 s) · ceiling 9.0 (ESTIMATE), **blocked on assets today**

**Role:** the voice of real guests, the one thing the approved advert lacks. The first footage pack already flagged real faces and voices as the strongest proof we do not have. **Angle:** the camera roll is *theirs*. **Hypothesis:** real guests speaking on their own phones earn more trust than any device above (**UNPROVEN**: I have no TripNerd data and have not sourced how this format performs; it is a bet on authenticity, like the last advert).

**Device:** the approved camera-roll grid, but the tiles are guests' own trip photos. Tapping one opens a guest's own phone clip (5–8 s), with captions burned in for muted feeds. Three or four guests, cut on the beat, and the same end card.

| Time | Picture | Source | Text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–1.5 | The grid of guests' own photos flicks; the hook | guest-supplied photos | **Their camera roll. Literally.** |
| 1.5–16 | Three or four guests, 3.5–4.5 s each, opened from the grid | guest-supplied vertical clips | captions of what each says |
| 16–22.5 | Flicks through their photos, then the roar | guests' photos; V24 | **We have everything. Which one's yours?** (owner's line) |
| 22.5–25 | End card | real logo file | Talk to a Nerd · tripnerd.com |

**What this needs from TripNerd (nothing exists today):**
1. **Eight to twelve past guests** each record one vertical phone clip of **one sentence**: *what did you tell your friends when you got home?* The best first asks are people who have already praised TripNerd publicly (the reviewers above).
2. **Three favorite photos each** from their own phones, original files, not chat-compressed.
3. **A signed release per guest** covering paid advertising. **NEEDS INPUT:** who drafts it (a lawyer should review the wording; I am not giving legal advice).

**Why it may be the best idea and is listed last:** it depends on other people. The four above do not.

**Recommendation:**
1. **Build 1 and 2 first.** They are the two commercial roles the approved advert does not cover (a date, and other people's words). They reuse the renderer, and they carry the least rights friction.
2. **Send TripNerd one request today** (the inventory's asks, below). It unlocks idea 5, raises every idea above it, and fixes the thin Derby.
3. **Hold 3 and 4** until 1 and 2 show what the audience responds to. Five adverts at once is not the efficient use of a test budget: two new ads against the approved one is.

**Considered and cut:**
- *THE LISTING* (a booking-page tour): it duplicates the earlier POV script and adds a fake booking button.
- *MEET THE NERDS*: too few staff photos, and it needs staff releases.
- A countdown to the Derby: the site's countdown is a placeholder (999 days).
- *SAME HOLE* from the first pack is still on the shelf; it needs the original masters, because the gallery clips are 404x720.

## The consolidated ask to TripNerd (**NEEDS INPUT**)
1. **Permissions:** quote reviewers by name; guest consent for paid use; staff consent (and who the staff at the check-in table are); a parent's consent for the Daytona photo (or we swap it).
2. **Written confirmation** of the package lines (food and drink included, concierge support, private home, hospitality every day) so they become Evidence Records; and a fix for the THE PLAYERS 2027 dates on the website.
3. **Assets:** the source files of the five Vimeo videos (especially the 17th-hole and Daytona suite tours, and the Derby film), the 578 MB December 2025 `.mov` in Drive, the raw suite clips from the owner's computer, and the original full-resolution gallery photos.
4. **Owner decisions:** full event names with non-affiliation wording, or shorthand; "We have everything" versus "Pick your event."; a licensed music track.
