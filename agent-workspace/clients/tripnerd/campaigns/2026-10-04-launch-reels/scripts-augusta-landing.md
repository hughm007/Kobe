# Five scripts around the landing beat

**Ask (owner, 2026-10-06):** five scripts for a video advert "using the photos" from the reference board, with a 1–10 rating of how I would perform making each, and the same for ChatGPT (GPT-6) Astra.

**Standing constraint:** the reference frames (`reference-augusta-12.md`) are Augusta National's footage. They are the shot brief for the landing beat (framing A/B/C on the board) and never appear in a cut. Each script needs one filmed shot: a ball landing beside a pin from low behind the green, 3–5 s, with sound (practice day at Masters week 2027, or any course now, no venue named). No tournament or venue name on screen; "Augusta, GA" in the caption only if the owner takes decision 1 of the week-1 brief. Fictional text messages are dramatisations and carry no outcome claims.
