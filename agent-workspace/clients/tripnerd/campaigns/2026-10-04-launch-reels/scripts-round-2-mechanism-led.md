# Five scripts, each built on a mechanism the category is paying to run

Source: [`../../marketing-plan/2026-10-06-competitive-picture-meta-ad-library.md`](../../marketing-plan/2026-10-06-competitive-picture-meta-ad-library.md). Every script uses TripNerd's own material; the one shot still missing from the library (a ball landing beside a pin) is not required by any of them. 9:16, no music, real sound where TripNerd has it. Nothing posts without the owner and TripNerd's approver.

## 1. "The thread." — 15 s · mechanism: the planning pain is the villain (Epic Golf Club, 206 days running)
- 0.0–1.0: black; one notification tone; a composited group thread, no names, avatars as initials. First bubble, dated **March 2025**: "Masters this year?"
- 1.0–7.0: the thread scrolls faster than you can read, the dates jumping: "who's doing badges", "I'll look into the house", "let's regroup after Q2", "next year for sure", "anyone?" Stamps: Apr 2025, Jul 2025, Nov 2025, Jan 2026. Tones overlap into a clatter.
- 7.0–8.0: silence. One bubble: "Booked. TripNerd."
- 8.0–13.0: the real week, three cuts: the check-in table with the TripNerd drape (Drive photo), the house patio at dusk (photo), then the rail over 17 as the roar hits (V24, real sound, 2.5 s).
- 13.0–15.0: lockup. "Hospitality. Handled."
- Needs: nothing new. Photo faces checked at full size; the roar clip is cleared. Caption carries the venue only if the owner takes decision 1.

## 2. "6:12 AM." — 20 s · mechanism: the day by the clock (Black Tomato, 172 days) with location stamps (Epic)
- Each shot carries one timestamp, upper left, Montserrat, and nothing else. No voice. Room tone, then the real sound at the end.
- 6:12 AM · coffee on the house patio (photo, slow push) · 8:40 AM · the walk in with the crowd (Gallery walk 1) · 11:05 AM · the suite door with the TripNerd sign (photo, cropped to drop the tour mark) · 1:30 PM · the rail, the banner walk-in (V23) · 4:48 PM · the roar (V24, real sound, the only sound in the piece) · 7:30 PM · dinner (buffet or patio tables photo) · 11:58 PM · black card: "Same again tomorrow." · lockup.
- Needs: the real run-of-show times from TripNerd (ask 3 in the week-1 brief); the times above are placeholders and must not post. This is the same device as "Augusta, by the clock" in the week-1 brief; the 17th version is the one TripNerd's footage can carry today.

## 3. "Ask Taylor." — 35 s · mechanism: a real host's face with insider specifics (TrueFan Travel; Epic's founder)
- Taylor (or whoever at TripNerd hosts the week) on camera, phone-shot, in the suite or at the house, no script read. Three questions, one take each, cut to the best 8–10 s per answer, keyword captions burned in (one word per beat, the Epic device: SUITE · SOUND · MONDAY).
  1. "What do people get wrong about the 17th?" (an insider detail only a host knows)
  2. "What's the first thing you hear from the suite?" (ties to the real roar, which plays under the answer)
  3. "What do your guests say on Monday?" (paraphrase only; no invented quotes)
- 0–1.5 s hook: the answer to question 1 starts mid-sentence, no intro, no name card until 4 s.
- End: "Hospitality. Handled." and the handle.
- Needs: Taylor's consent and a 20-minute phone shoot; nothing is written for them to say. No synthetic person, no scripted testimonial (the Parking Lot rule). Specifics stated on camera become claims and are checked before posting.

  5. the roar (V24, sound up) · "The sound."
  6. the father and son at Daytona (gallery 020, release needed) · "Someone to turn to."
- 10–12 s: "Hospitality. Handled."
- Needs: the package sheet as an Evidence Record for lines 2 and 3; the Daytona photo release. Variants: the same cut with the Daytona suite or the Phoenix suite swapped in (the Liverpool pattern: one cut, many sports).

## 5. "Monday." — 14 s · mechanism: memory over logistics (Black Tomato) carried by real words (TrueFan's social proof), aimed at the corporate host nobody else talks to
- 0–2: black. "Monday, 8:04 AM." A real review excerpt types on, verbatim, labelled "from a Google review", no name until the OK (ask 5).
- 2–11: three excerpts over three real moments: the group at the patio (release), the rail (V23), the roar (V24, real sound rising under the third quote).
- 11–14: "The trip your clients bring up for years." then the lockup. (Only if a review says so in its own words; otherwise the card reads "Bring the people who matter.")
- Needs: the OK to quote Jon Reader and Sean Moseley by name (ask 5); every excerpt exact, ellipses only; no star rating, no counts; no photo paired with a quote in a way that implies the reviewer is in it.

## What this round changes versus the first five
The first five hung on an Augusta landing shot nobody can legally supply before April 2027. These five run on mechanisms that the longest-running advertisers in the set keep paying for, and on footage and photos TripNerd already owns. The two with the most evidence behind them are 1 and 2 (pain, by the clock). The one with the highest ceiling and the most friction is 3 (a real face). Ask for 3 now; it takes a month to get a person comfortable on camera.
