# Real client footage — what exists and where (logged 2026-09-29)

**Method, FACT:** every video in the Higgsfield account (100 items) was probed and contact-sheeted
(5 frames each). Six are real TripNerd camera footage. The rest are our own renders, the owner's cuts, the
August generated TripNerd spec ads ("The Seat", "Stop saying next year", the phone-at-the-window series) and
other clients' work. Five items return 403 and could not be read (`b9ccae85`, `0abf8c6d`, `10c1b0ae`,
`f7bd0cd3`, `7bdf7322`); `0abf8c6d` was uploaded in the same batch as gallery clip `45e0a985` and may be
another real clip. Each real clip was logged at 1 frame per second (sheets `a4c8862a`, `bcb54e66`,
`d654275c`) and its audio run through speech recognition.

**Resolution is the constraint, FACT:** V23 and V24 are 720x1280; the three gallery clips are 404x720;
the suite footage in the owner's cut is 1280x720 with a burned-in caption. Nothing here is native 1080p.
The approved hosting spot lost its points for exactly this (720p body). **The originals from Taylor's
phone are the single biggest quality lever.**

**All of it is vertical (9:16)**, so the new advert's master is 9:16.

## Standing rules for any cut from this footage
- No THE PLAYERS, PGA TOUR, scoreboard, player name or broadcast on screen, and none named in copy
  (registered marks of PGA TOUR, Inc.). "17" and "the island green" are fine.
- Guest faces only with consent (ask Taylor once whether event terms include photo consent). Default cuts use
  backs, wides and crowds.
- AI only for minor touches: upscaling, cleanup, reframing, removing a burned caption. Nothing that changes
  what happened.
