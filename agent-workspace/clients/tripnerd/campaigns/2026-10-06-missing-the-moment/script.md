## Package details to confirm before production (CLIENT_APPROVER)
1. **Vantage:** what viewing position does the advertised THE PLAYERS package actually give? (Grounds access only? A hospitality venue overlooking a specific hole? Which hole? Is viewing elevated?) Shots 6–9 cannot be finalised without this.
2. **Clear view of play:** is an unobstructed view guaranteed, typical, or not promised? If not promised, the core contrast becomes an implied false claim and the concept must change.
3. **Seating:** seated or standing? Shots 6–9 show whichever is true.
4. **Hospitality:** food and drink included? Covered or shaded area? Shot 9 shows only what's included.
5. **Group size:** can a guest bring companions in the same area? (Shot 8)
6. **Which year and package** the ad sells, and whether it's still available (on sale for 2027?).
7. **Event naming in ads:** does TripNerd name the event in paid media, and has it ever had a trademark complaint?
8. **Footage rights:** written OK to use archive clip V18 and any other archive clips, including that people visible in the archive are not identifiable or have consented.
9. **Logo master file** (vector), plus brand-blue HEX and font.
10. **Real guest option:** is there a past guest who would appear on camera with consent? That replaces generated shots 7–9 and makes the payoff more credible.
