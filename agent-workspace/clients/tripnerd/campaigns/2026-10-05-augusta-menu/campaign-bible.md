## 2. Strategy
*Owner: `servicepow-strategy`. Mostly answered by the Growth Plan and the content system;
offer verdict and CTA written fresh here.*

### Offer verdict
**Is the offer strong enough to advertise?** **ADEQUATE** for an organic awareness Reel.
**Reasoning:** The thing being "sold" in this piece is attention, not a booking. The hook is
a sourced, widely covered fact ($1.50 since 2002, reported by NPR and others each April) and
the ranking gives a reason to watch to the end. The downstream offer (Augusta Experience) is
strong but unpriced here; the CTA offer (a free Augusta cheat sheet by DM) is useful but
small. It would become STRONG with a sharper keyword reward (e.g. "the 4 things first-timers
get wrong at Augusta"), which the existing DM reply already nearly is.

**Target:** US golf fans and group-trip organisers who know of Augusta but have not been.
**Awareness level:** Problem-unaware / brand-unaware. They know the sandwich, not TripNerd.
**Core message:** Lunch is the easy part of Augusta. The hard part is what a Nerd is for.
**THE ANGLE (chosen):** *"The famous cheap thing vs the actually hard part"* — borrow the
event's most-shared fact, then hand off to what TripNerd solves.
Ranked alternatives: (2) "Things at Augusta that make zero sense" (phones banned → payphones)
— strong, kept for a later Reel; (3) "Augusta by the clock" (launch-queue Reel 01) — useful,
weaker hook; (4) AI person food review — **rejected** (compliance, §1).
**Core promise:** You'll know which sandwich to order — and who to call for the rest.
**Proof strategy:** Demonstrate, don't assert: real person, real food, real receipt; every
price on screen sourced (EV-tripnerd-001…006); the DM's notes sourced (EV-008, 009).
**Objection strategy:** No ad feel until 0:30; brand arrives as the punchline.
**Platform:** Instagram Reels, organic; main post Friday 11 AM ET Trial slot or Monday 2 PM
Reel slot (Growth Plan schedule); Story push within the hour of posting.
**CTA (recommendation, needs APPROVER):** **"Comment NERDNOTES for our Augusta notes"**,
ManyChat comment trigger on that unique word (a comment that only mentions Augusta does not
fire it), sending [dm-reply.md](dm-reply.md) as **one** message (Instagram allows one private
reply to a comment until the person answers): notes, then the offer, then a reply window
(ServicePOW, one business hour, Mon–Fri 9–6 ET). Why: every
active Golfbreaks US video ad found uses a comment-keyword CTA (CONFIRMED, ad copy), and
comments are a primary KPI. Whether Instagram demotes keyword-comment prompts: UNKNOWN.
Alternative: "DM NERDNOTES for our Augusta notes". The Growth Plan's DM keyword AUGUSTA is not
used here: it would catch genuine Augusta Experience questions in the canned flow (consider
the same change for the Story kit). Both overlays are rendered; one is chosen. "Cheat sheet" was dropped: the reply is three notes, so the promise
now says notes.
**Success metric:** vs the account's own Reel median once known: 3-second hold, average watch
time, sends per reach, comments. Trial Reels decide the winning first frame (HYPOTHESIS to be
tested, not assumed).

*Owner: `servicepow-creative-spine`.*

**Core message:** Lunch is the easy part of Augusta. The hard part is what a Nerd is for.
**Core promise:** Know the winner; know who handles the rest.
**Primary emotion:** Amused curiosity → mild indignation ("egg salad?!") → "ha, fair".
**Viewer starting state:** Scrolling; knows Augusta is famous; doesn't know TripNerd.
**Viewer ending state:** Has an opinion on the ranking; remembers "the Nerds"; maybe comments.

less than the groceries), which sets up the brand line.
