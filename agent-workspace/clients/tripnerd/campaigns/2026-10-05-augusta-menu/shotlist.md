# Shot list — "Augusta's cheap eats, made at home (and ranked)"

Instagram Reel, 9:16 (1080×1920), 35.0 s, organic. One real TripNerd staff member ("the
Nerd") in a home kitchen, filmed on a phone. **No shot is AI-generated.** All text is
composited in the edit in Poppins (headings) and Inter (labels), inside the burned-text band
**y 288–1344 px (15–70% of frame height)** and clear of the right-hand 140 px where
Instagram's buttons sit. TripNerd wordmark = the real file from the tripnerd.com CDN
(SHA-256 `11bfe474…6f00b40`), composited on a navy plate.

**Ranking rule.** The Nerd tastes all five off camera first and writes down an order and a
score for each. Only then are verdicts filmed. Lines in [brackets] are filled from that
sheet; reaction lines are said only if true. HERO treatment follows the position (#1 always;
the pause goes on whichever item the Nerd actually hesitated over), not a particular food.

**Voice.** Shots 2 and 8 are voiceover, recorded at the counter on the same phone right after
the take (same room tone). Shots 3–7 and 9 are spoken on camera. Shot 1 has no voice.

Angle for the whole piece: 45° / overhead food close-ups, eye level for verdicts to camera.

## Set and props rules
- No packaging, brand labels, appliance badges or logo clothing in frame. Decant chips and ice
  cream; plain plates and bowls.
- Plain white deli paper, not green (avoids echoing the tournament's sandwich wrapper).
- Two identical wrapped pimento cheese sandwiches (one for shot 1, one for shot 10).
- Hand-written price cards must read exactly $1.50 (pimento), $1.50 (egg salad), $1.50
  (chips), $2.50 (cheese straws), $3 (peach ice cream sandwich).
- Shoot within about an hour of steady daylight; lock exposure and white balance on the phone.
- Music: chosen in the edit from Instagram's commercial-use library; track name recorded in
  the production log before export.

## Call to action (APPROVER picks one; the other is dropped)
