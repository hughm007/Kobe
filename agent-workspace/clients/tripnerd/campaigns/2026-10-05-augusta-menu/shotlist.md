# Shot list — "Augusta's cheap eats, made at home (and ranked)"

Instagram Reel, 9:16 (1080×1920), 35.0 s, organic. One real TripNerd staff member ("the
Nerd") in a home kitchen, filmed on a phone. **No shot is AI-generated.**

**Composited text.** Poppins (headings) and Inter (labels); white text; navy #07283d plates;
gold #e6a310 rank badges and price accents; blue #2ea3f2 CTA and labels. Every text block sits
inside y 288–1344 px (15–70% of frame height) and at x ≤ 940 px (right 140 px clear for
Instagram's buttons). `build/render.js` measures both on every render; the final overlays are
re-rendered from the shoot's real values (`scores.json`) and **must pass that check before
export**. TripNerd wordmark = TripNerd's live file from the tripnerd.com CDN (422 px wide,
shown at 300 px, never upscaled) until TripNerd supplies or accepts a master.

**Ranking rule (live tasting).** Every first bite and verdict is filmed live, in any order.
The Nerd says the score out loud and writes it on a card on camera right after each bite.
The edit then sorts the clips into #5 → #1. A tie is broken on camera at the end. No verdict
is restaged.

**Voice.** Shots 2 and 8 are voiceover, recorded at the counter on the same phone right after
the takes. Shots 3–7 and 9 are live on camera. Shot 1 has no voice.

**HERO.** Shot 1 is the hook item by design (the $1.50-since-2002 fact belongs to the pimento
cheese and egg salad sandwiches). In the ranking (shots 3–7), HERO follows the #1 position,
whatever food lands there.

Angle for the whole piece: 45° / overhead food close-ups, eye level for verdicts to camera.

## Shoot prerequisites (all before the camera rolls)
1. **Consent.** An email from the Nerd agreeing that TripNerd may use their likeness, voice and
   first name in this Reel, its hook variants and Trial Reels, the caption and the keyword DM,
   on TripNerd's Instagram and Facebook, for as long as the posts stay up; withdrawal by email,
   with the posts archived within two business days. **Plus** the CLIENT_APPROVER's written
   confirmation that the consent is on file.
2. **Location.** The Nerd's own kitchen (or the owner's written OK). Frame check: no window
   views that identify the place, no mail, house numbers, family photos or screens.
3. **Evidence.** EV-tripnerd-001…008 filed (APPROVED). EV-010's scope fixed before shopping.
4. **Props.** Two identical pimento cheese sandwiches in white deli paper (A is bitten in shot
   1 and held in shot 9; B stays wrapped for shot 10). Ice cream sandwiches kept frozen, fresh
   one per take. No packaging, labels, appliance badges or logo clothing in frame.
5. **Light.** Film the tasting block within about 45 minutes; check exposure between items.
