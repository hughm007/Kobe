# Shot list — "Two Nerds judge Augusta's cheap eats"

Instagram Reel, 9:16 (1080×1920), 35.0 s, organic. **Format: a judging table.** Two real
TripNerd staff members ("the judges", Nerd A and Nerd B) sit side by side behind a table with a
white cloth, facing camera. Five Augusta classics arrive one at a time under silver cloches.
Both judges taste live, each writes a score out of 10 on a dry-erase paddle and raises it. The
average sets the rank. Played dead serious about $1.50 sandwiches: the deadpan is the joke, not
mugging. **No shot is AI-generated.**

**Composited text.** Poppins (headings) and Inter (labels); white text; navy #07283d plates;
gold #e6a310 rank badges and price accents; blue #2ea3f2 CTA and labels. Every text block sits
inside y 288–1344 px (15–70% of frame height) and at x ≤ 940 px (right 140 px clear for
Instagram's buttons). `build/render.js` measures both on every render; the final overlays are
re-rendered from the shoot's real values (`scores.json`) and **must pass that check before
export**. TripNerd wordmark = TripNerd's live file from the tripnerd.com CDN (422 px wide,
shown at 300 px, never upscaled) until TripNerd supplies or accepts a master.

**Ranking rule (live judging).** Each item is tasted on camera by both judges, in any order.
Each writes a score on their paddle straight after the first bite and raises it. Nothing is
restaged. The edit sorts the items by average score into #5 → #1; a tie is broken by the judges
on camera at the end.

**Cameras.** Camera 1: phone on a tripod, locked-off two-shot at eye level (the master for
every verdict). Camera 2: second phone, handheld, for cloche lifts and food close-ups. If only
one phone is available, shoot each item twice: close-up pass, then two-shot pass.

**Voice.** Shots 2 and 8 are voiceover by Nerd A, recorded at the table right after the takes.
Verdict lines and shot 9 are live on camera. Shot 1 has no voice.

**HERO.** Shot 1 is the hook item by design (the $1.50-since-2002 fact belongs to the pimento
cheese and egg salad sandwiches). In the ranking (shots 3–7), HERO follows the #1 position,
whatever food lands there.

Angle for the whole piece: eye-level locked two-shot for judging; 45° / overhead close-ups for food.

## Shoot prerequisites (all before the camera rolls)
1. **Consent, both judges.** An email from each judge agreeing that TripNerd may use their
   likeness, voice and first name in this Reel, its hook variants and Trial Reels, the caption
   and the keyword DM, on TripNerd's Instagram and Facebook, for as long as the posts stay up;
   withdrawal by email, with the posts archived within two business days. **Plus** the
   CLIENT_APPROVER's written confirmation that both consents are on file. Whoever brings the
   plates appears as hands only.
2. **Location.** A TripNerd space or a home dining table with the owner's written OK. Frame
   check: no window views that identify the place, no mail, house numbers, family photos or
   screens.
3. **Evidence.** EV-tripnerd-001…008 filed (APPROVED). EV-010's scope fixed before shopping.
4. **Props.** White tablecloth; five silver cloches (party-store or restaurant-supply; plain,
   no logos); two dry-erase paddles and markers; plain plates. Two identical pimento cheese
   sandwiches in white deli paper (A is bitten in shot 1 and held in shot 9; B stays wrapped
   for shot 10). Enough of each item for two judges and a second take. Ice cream sandwiches
   kept frozen, fresh one per take. No packaging, labels, appliance badges or logo clothing.
5. **Light and sound.** Shoot the judging block within about 45 minutes; check exposure
   between items. Phone mics close to the judges, or one clip-on mic per judge if available.
