## 1. What the meeting decided

**FACT** (from the notes):
- **Price:** TripNerd found $4,000 a month ($12,000 total) too high for growing from about 4,700 to 5,100 followers. They agreed **October as a $1,500 intro test month**. The bigger package waits on results.
- **Sliding scale:** TripNerd would invest more only if follower gains are shown, "like a sliding scale tied to growth".
- **Already-paid work:**
  - $1,200 covered 3 videos and 2 statics (the original offer was $1,175).
  - 2 videos are delivered and the statics aren't posted.
  - Already-paid content gets posted without being charged again.
- **Total:** $1,200 + $1,500 = **$2,700 over about five weeks**, as a first test.
- **Volume:** the plan recommended 8 Reels a month at set posting times, and Wyatt offered to tone that down.
- **Analytics:** Wyatt wants Supermetrics connected, to see analytics on every video.
- **Pricing benchmark:** about 10 marketing businesses. We sit below agencies and above freelancers ($150–200 a Reel), at about $300–325 a Reel.
- **Next step:** TripNerd reviews October's posts and follower results at month end, before deciding on the bigger package.

**Note:** TripNerd heard $12,000 for 90 days. The deck said $9,750 ($1,750 + $4,000 + $4,000). It's moot now, but don't repeat $12,000.

## 5. Scorecard and the November tiers

**Every Monday** (`monday-report-template.md`) reports:
- followers (start, now, net new, with September beside them);
- per video: views, non-follower share, watch time, sends, saves and follows;
- profile visits and DM enquiries;
- what worked and what changes.

**November tiers** (**PROPOSAL**; Karl confirms before Jason sees slide 5):

| Net new organic followers, 1–31 Oct (Insights) | November | Rate-card value |
|---|---|---|
| Under 100 | $1,500 · same as October, or stop | $1,600 |
| 100–199 | $2,500 · 8 Reels, 6 statics, Stories 5 days | $3,050 (18% off) |
| 200+ | $4,000 · 16 Reels, 8 statics, Stories 5 days, first-line DMs (the Deck A month) | $5,800 (31% off) |

**The honest expectation (ESTIMATE):**
- Buffer's cadence data gives about +0.26% a week at 3–5 posts a week (as cited in the [deliverable](../../deliverables/2026-10-01-instagram-growth-plan.md)). At 4,730 followers that is **about 50 net new in October**.
- So 100 means double the benchmark and 200 means four times it. **The most likely November is $1,500** unless a Reel breaks out to non-followers.
- That's the right design for TripNerd (they pay more only for real growth). For ServicePOW it means October's Reels have to be strong. That is a footage and hook problem, not a volume problem.
- **Don't present the tiers as likely to be reached.**

**Watch out:** a fee tied only to followers rewards follower-chasing, and followers aren't bookings. Keep DM enquiries in every report. Ask again at the review for the average booking margin (the measurement slide's blank), so the conversation can move to enquiries and bookings.
