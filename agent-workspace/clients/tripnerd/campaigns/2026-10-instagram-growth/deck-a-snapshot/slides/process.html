The process

How every post gets made

1 Capture

Every trip filmed to our shot checklist: arrival, hospitality, the crowd, reactions.

TripNerd · Google Drive

2 Plan

Footage becomes the week's posts, hooks and captions, on the week's topics.

Claude · ChatGPT · SuperGrok

3 Edit

Vertical Reels with a hook in the first second, captions and sound on.

Adobe Creative Cloud

4 Enhance

Fills short gaps between real shots and upscales footage. Always labelled as AI.

Higgsfield

5 Check

No watermarks, full resolution, no heavy text or politics, AI labelled.

ServicePOW checklist

6 Approve

One weekly batch. Nothing posts without your sign-off.

Your approver

7 Publish

Scheduled at your best times; a Story pushes it within the hour; keyword DMs answered.

Meta Business Suite · ManyChat

8 Report

Every Monday: what worked, what we double down on, what stops.

Instagram Insights · Claude
