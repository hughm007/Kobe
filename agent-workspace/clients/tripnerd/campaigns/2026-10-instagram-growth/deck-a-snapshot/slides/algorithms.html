The algorithms

The four algorithms we're working

Instagram doesn't show posts in order. Each part of the app ranks them with its own algorithm, predicting who will watch, like, share or save.

Feed

Shows: your followers

Rewards relationships. People who comment, DM and reply see you first.

We attack it with replies to every comment, proof posts and carousels worth saving.

Stories

Shows: your closest followers

Rewards people who watch and reply to your Stories again and again.

We attack it with Stories five days a week: polls, questions, behind the scenes, DM prompts.

Explore

Shows: people who don't follow you

Rewards momentum: how many people like, comment, share and save, and how fast.

We attack it with saveable guides, and Stories that send followers to each new post in its first hour.

Reels

Shows: mostly people who don't follow you

Rewards Reels watched to the end, shared and whose audio gets tapped.

We attack it with a hook in the first second, real footage, original sound and a reason to send it.
