# Pointing TripNerd's customers at Instagram

This is the "Your customers, pointed at Instagram" promise on PDF page 2.
- **We** write and test everything.
- **TripNerd** places it, because we have no access to their booking system, email tool or website.
- **Effort:** about 2–3 hours for us and about 1 hour for them.

## Step 1: Ask on the setup call
1. What sends the booking confirmations and trip emails, and who can edit those templates?
2. Who edits tripnerd.com?
3. Is there a past-guest email list? Roughly how many people, and did they agree to marketing email?
4. What do guests get in print or PDF (itinerary, welcome pack, lanyard, table cards)? Which trips run in October and November?
5. Who on the team sends email (for signatures)?

## Step 2: Make the tracked links
- Make one short link per placement, so each one's clicks are counted:
  - emails;
  - print/QR;
  - the website;
  - signatures.
- All of them point to https://www.instagram.com/tripnerd/.
- **Tool:** create them in a link shortener that counts clicks, in **TripNerd's own account** so they own them. Check that the free plan still includes click counts before choosing one.

## Step 3: Make the QR code
- **For print, use the tracked print link,** so every scan is counted.
- Export it as SVG or high-res PNG.
- Print it at least 0.8 in (2 cm) wide, dark on light.
- **Test it with two phones before anything is printed.**
- **Fallback:** Instagram's own QR (app → profile → Share profile). It's free and branded, but it can't count scans.

## Step 4: Write the copy (DRAFT; TripNerd's approver signs off)

TripNerd's brand guide has no voice rules yet, so these are plain and factual.

| Where | Draft |
|---|---|
| **Booking confirmation** (one line, under the booking details) | "Follow @tripnerd for photos from our trips and first looks at next season's events." + button "Follow on Instagram" |
| **Pre-trip email / itinerary** | "During the trip, tag @tripnerd in your Stories. We reshare the best ones." + QR |
| **Printed** (welcome pack, table card, lanyard back) | "Tag @tripnerd · Scan to follow" + QR |
| **Post-trip thank-you** | "Your photos from [event] are here. If you post them, tag @tripnerd and we'll share the best ones." |
| **Email signature** (everyone) | "Follow our trips on Instagram: @tripnerd" (linked) |
| **Website** | Instagram icon in the header and footer. On the enquiry thank-you page: "While you wait, see the trips: follow @tripnerd." |
| **One-time past-guest email** (opted-in only) | Subject: "We're showing more of the trips". Body: "We've started sharing more from our trips on Instagram: the seats, the setups, the moments. Follow along at @tripnerd. Got photos from your trip with us? Tag us, and we'll share the best." |

## Step 5: Hand over one pack
- **What goes:** the copy, the links, the QR files, and one line per item saying where it goes.
- **Route:** to TripNerd's approver; their team pastes it in.

## Step 6: Test once it's live
- Click every link.
- Scan every QR.
- Ask for a test booking email sent to us.

## Step 7: Measure (Monday report)
- **Weekly:** clicks per link, and follows that week.
- **Past-guest email:** compare follows in the two days after it goes out with a normal day.

## Timing and rules
- **The past-guest email goes out only after 3–4 strong posts are live,** so the profile is worth following when people land on it. That means week 2–3, not day one.
- **Booking confirmations are transactional.** Keep the follow line short and below the booking details.
- **The past-guest email is marketing.** Send it only to people who agreed. It needs an unsubscribe link and a physical address (CAN-SPAM); their email tool normally adds both.
