## Recommendation: post a real-footage-only cut organically

Remove the three AI bridges, the AI cup motion and the cloned voice. The organic post is then 100% TripNerd's own
footage and sound.
- **No AI label needed:** none of the content is synthetic.
- **Matches the plan's promise:** real footage, original sound.
- **Removes the riskiest frame:** a generated golfer holing a putt at a real PGA TOUR event.
- **The paid version is unchanged:** the approved v15 stays available for paid use, with Instagram's AI label on.
- **Approval:** the new cut goes in TripNerd's week-one approval batch like every other post.
