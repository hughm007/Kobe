# Algorithm and niche strategy: getting TripNerd onto strangers' Reels feeds

**Karl's ask (2026-10-04):** fit TripNerd into niches that still relate to it but have far more potential to land on non-followers' Reels feeds, and use the algorithm to our advantage.

**The short answer:**
- **Instagram doesn't push a brand. It pushes a topic to people who've shown interest in that topic.** It then expands the Reels that those people watch to the end and send to friends.
- "Premium sports hospitality" is a small topic. **Golf, football and sports-fan moments are huge topics**, and TripNerd has a real right to play in them.
- **So every Reel picks one big topic, makes it obvious in the first second, and lands the TripNerd payoff at the end.**

## 2. What that means for the Augusta advert (diagnosis)
- The Augusta stills Reel failed our gate five times: the best critic score was 7.7 against an 8.0 floor, with the hook stuck at 6.
- **Its algorithm problem is the same as its creative problem: no golf is in frame.**
  - The classifier and the viewer see a check-in desk, a porch and a posed group. The only "golf" signal is the word "Augusta" and some pine trees.
  - So it would be tested on a vague "lifestyle / group photo" audience. It has no motion hook, and nobody has a reason to send it.
- **Recommendation:** don't make it TripNerd's first Reel.
  - Re-use the photos as a **carousel**, "Augusta week, hour by hour". Carousels earn saves and Explore reach and don't need a motion hook.
  - It posts once TripNerd confirms the venue's city.

- **Golf-fan moments** and **golf-trip culture** carry the Reels: two adjacent topics, one clear profile.
- **Insider tips** and **"what it costs"** are the save engines: carousels and Nerd talking-heads.
- Corporate stays as carousels.
- Football waits for Super Bowl footage.

## 4. The rules every Reel follows (the algorithm checklist)
1. **Frame-1 topic test.** Could a stranger name the topic from the first frame with the sound off? (green, crowd, course = golf). If not, re-cut.
2. **Motion and sound in the first second.** A real moment, not a still. Original sound wherever the moment has it (the roar). That also drives audio-page visits.
3. **One topic per Reel.** TripNerd comes in as the payoff ("this was our suite" / "hosted by TripNerd"), not the opening.
4. **Built for watch time.** 7–15 s; the payoff late; an end that loops back to the start.
5. **Built for sends without asking.** The content names a person ("the friend who…") or a plan ("we're doing this"). Don't write "send this to…" in the caption, because explicit share instructions risk being read as engagement bait.
6. **Keywords.** On-screen text and the first caption line use the words people search ("17th hole at TPC Sawgrass", "golf trip"). At most 5 hashtags, and they're no reach lever.
7. **Original only.** TripNerd's own footage or our edit of it. No broadcast clips, no reposts, no other platforms' watermarks, no low-res upscales without approval.
8. **Test before committing.**
   - If TripNerd has 1,000+ followers, run 2–3 hook variants as Trial Reels.
   - The one that wins on watch time and sends per reach goes to the grid.
9. **Measure the right numbers:** watch-through, sends per 1,000 reached, follows per post and the share of non-followers. Likes come last.

## 6. Honest limits
- Nothing guarantees a viral Reel. These rules raise the odds that a Reel gets a fair test with the right audience and expands if people watch and send it.
- Topic size and the "fewer topics" effect are estimates and practitioner views.
