Each format has one job and one success metric: Reels get watched to the end and sent to someone; carousels get saved; Stories keep followers close and push each new post. The counts now match the price exactly: 4 posts a week in month one, 6 from Day 31, Trial Reels included in the Reel total. Trial Reels show to non-followers first, so we pick winning hooks before committing. Event weekends get live Stories plus a same-day Reel that swaps out a weekday slot, so the monthly total doesn't change. Times come from industry studies and get re-tuned from TripNerd's own Insights in week two.
