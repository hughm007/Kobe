How it works

How every post gets made

1 Capture and plan

Your trips filmed to our shot list, then turned into the week's posts.

2 Edit and check

Vertical, captioned, sound on. Full resolution, no watermarks.

3 You approve

One weekly batch. Nothing posts without your approver's yes.

4 Publish and report

At your best times, then into the Monday report.

OUR RULES

Venues: we follow each venue's camera and phone policy.

Event names: only to say where guests went, never as an official partner.

Guests: shown only with consent; anyone who opts out is left out.

Reviews: real guests, word for word. AI never creates a person or a testimonial.

AI: mainly to bridge short gaps and upscale, always labelled.

Music: original audio or Instagram's commercial library.

Our promises: a post you want down comes down within 2 hours. If Day 30 shows no clear winner, two more test weeks at the same price.

Tools: Claude, ChatGPT and SuperGrok to plan · Adobe to edit · Higgsfield for labelled AI · Meta Business Suite to publish.

04

Four steps, and step 3 is the one leadership cares about: nothing posts without TripNerd's approver, in one weekly batch. The rules protect TripNerd's relationships with the venues it sells: Augusta is the strictest example, with phones restricted on tournament days, so that footage only comes from where filming is allowed. An AI person giving a testimonial breaks the FTC rule on fake reviews, and a label doesn't fix it, so we never do it. Close on the two promises.
