Posting rules

What we will and won't post

AreaOur rule
Venue filmingWe follow each venue's camera and phone policy. Some majors restrict phones on tournament days, so that footage comes only from where filming is allowed.
Event namesUsed only to say where guests went ("our guests at THE PLAYERS"). Never presented as an official partner; event logos are never TripNerd branding.
Guests on cameraIdentifiable only with consent: a simple photo release at check-in. Anyone who opts out is cropped, blurred or left out.
ReviewsReal guests and real reviews only, quoted word for word. AI never creates a person, a voice or a testimonial.
AIMainly only to bridge short gaps between real shots and to upscale. Always labelled.
MusicOriginal audio or Instagram's commercial-use library. No trending copyrighted tracks on a business account.

Every post is checked against these rules at step 5 and approved by TripNerd at step 6.

06

This slide protects TripNerd's relationships with the venues it sells. One unlicensed logo, or a guest filmed without consent, costs more than any Reel earns. Augusta is the strictest example: phones and cameras are restricted on tournament days, so tournament-day footage comes only from where filming is allowed, and practice-round and hospitality-area footage is cleared in advance. The reviews rule matters legally too: an AI person giving a testimonial breaks the FTC rule on fake reviews, and a disclosure doesn't fix it.
