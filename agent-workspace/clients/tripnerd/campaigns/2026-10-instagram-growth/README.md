## 1. The goal

> Reels reach people who don't follow TripNerd yet, Stories and replies keep them, and DMs turn them into
> enquiries.

Aim, as worded to the client: *make TripNerd the account fans follow for the biggest events.*
We sell certain work, never certain outcomes. **Do not promise any number to TripNerd.**
The targets below are labelled targets and are reset at Day 30.

| Metric | Today (FACT, 11 Sep 2026) | Day 90 target | Month 12 target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Followers | 4,730 | about 5,100 | about 6,300 organic; about 10,000 with approved boosts and creators |
| Median Reel views | 253 | 1,500+ | 5,000+ |
| Views from non-followers | unknown | over 50% | over 60% |
| Saves and sends per 1,000 reached | unknown | baseline set | +50% on baseline |
| DMs and enquiries from Instagram | unknown | tracked weekly | tracked to bookings |

The week-one Insights baseline replaces the "today" column.

- **Volume:** four feed posts a week to Day 30, then six. Reels go from 2 a week to 4.
- **Trial Reels:** ASSUMPTION — these are hook, opening or ending variants of that week's Reels, not extra Reels on the invoice. Confirm with Karl.

## 6. How Claude works this plan

### Do it (no approval needed: internal drafts)
- Work out today's Day number and week from the Day 1 date in `tracker.md`, and say what's due, late or blocked.
- Draft the week's plan:
  - Reel hooks, openings, endings and captions;
  - carousel copy and layouts;
  - proof-post copy;
  - Stories prompts (polls, questions, behind the scenes, DM prompts);
  - DM keyword replies;
  - shot checklists for upcoming trips.
- Draft the Monday report from the data provided, using [`monday-report-template.md`](monday-report-template.md).
- Keep `tracker.md` current. Log decisions, and at Day 30, 60 and 90 log learnings in `knowledge/learnings/`.
- Prepare the Day 30, 60 and 90 reviews and draft each invoice line for Karl.

### Remind Karl (a person has to act)
- Send the Monday report and the weekly batch to TripNerd.
- Schedule approved posts in Meta Business Suite.
- Invoice on Day 1, Day 31 and Day 61.
- Chase week-one access, the trip archive, the on-camera Nerd and the approver.
- Buy ManyChat (TripNerd's cost, needs its sign-off) and SuperGrok (ours).
- Collect guest consent before any guest appears in a post.

### Never without explicit approval (Service Pow law)
- **Never publish anything:** no posting, scheduling, or replying as TripNerd. Every post needs TripNerd's sign-off (CLIENT_APPROVER).
- **Never send anything** to TripNerd, creators or anyone else without Karl (APPROVER).
- **Never spend anything** — generation (Higgsfield), boosts, creator fees or tools — without Karl's SPEND_APPROVER gate, and TripNerd's written sign-off for its money.
- **Never promise an outcome** or make an unsourced claim.
