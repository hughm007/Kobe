# The guest loop

**What TripNerd was promised** (PDF page 2, "Beyond the posts"):
> "The guest loop. At every trip, your host asks guests to tag @tripnerd. We reshare their Stories the same day and, with their OK, turn the best into Collab posts on their account too. Tell us which trips run in October and November."

**The loop:** the host asks → guests tag @tripnerd → we reshare their Story the same day → the best become Collab posts, with the guest's written OK → future guests see real trips.

**Hard limits:**
- **Augusta:** no Augusta asks, reshares or posts until TripNerd confirms the venue and consent ([`../stories/gate-log.md`](../stories/gate-log.md)).
- **Event names:** use them only to say where guests went ("our guests at THE PLAYERS"). Never as a partnership, sponsorship or official status.
- **Guests' words are their own.** Never invent a quote, review or caption in a guest's voice. A guest's words appear only verbatim and with their OK ([`operations/compliance.md`](../../../../../operations/compliance.md) §1, `_servicepow/policies/claims-and-proof.md`).
- **No rewards for tagging:** no discounts, upgrades or prizes in return for a post or tag. Giveaways are out for the test month, and a reward can turn a guest's post into an endorsement that needs a disclosure (FTC Endorsement Guides). If TripNerd ever wants this, its own legal review comes first.

## 2. The host script

The host asks once or twice per trip, never pushes, and always gives an easy way to say no. The written lines reuse the draft copy in [`../owned-channels-follow-link.md`](../owned-channels-follow-link.md) (Step 4). All of it is **DRAFT** until TripNerd's approver signs off.

| When | Channel | Words (DRAFT) |
|---|---|---|
| **Before the trip** | Pre-trip email or itinerary, plus the QR from [`follow-link-qr-pack.md`](follow-link-qr-pack.md) | "During the trip, tag @tripnerd in your Stories. We reshare the best ones." |
| **On site** | Said by the host at welcome or check-in, once. A reminder at the big moment is optional | "If you're posting today, tag @tripnerd. We reshare the best ones on our Story. And if you'd rather not appear on our account, just tell me." |
| **After the trip** | Post-trip thank-you email | "Your photos from [event] are here. If you post them, tag @tripnerd and we'll share the best ones." **If TripNerd doesn't send photos, use only the second sentence.** [event] names where guests went, nothing more |

**Host notes:**
- **Don't film or photograph guests who said no,** and tell us who they are, so we skip them.
- **Children:** don't ask children to post or tag. Anything showing a child needs a parent's written OK (§5).
- **Weekend trips:** the 20-minute routine runs on weekdays only, so a Saturday or Sunday trip needs a named person to reshare that day (see the table in §1).

## 3. Same-day Story reshares

A guest who tags @tripnerd in their Story sends TripNerd a mention in its DMs. Stories disappear after 24 hours, so reshare **the same day**. A late reshare may not be possible.

**Steps (Instagram app, as TripNerd):**
1. Open **DMs** and find the message saying the guest mentioned you in their Story.
2. **Check before resharing:**
   - the guest's account is **public**, and they tagged @tripnerd (§5);
   - no child is in it, unless a parent has given written consent;
   - nothing a guest in frame would mind (drinking, private moments), and no visible ticket barcodes, credentials or personal details;
   - nothing implying TripNerd is an event's official partner, and nothing about Augusta.
3. Tap **Add to Story** (labels change; look for the same idea).
4. **Credit the guest:** make sure their @handle shows on the reshare. Add a mention sticker if it doesn't.
5. If you add text, keep it short and claim-free ("Great day at [event] with this crew"). No prices, availability or "best seats".
6. Post it. Log the count in [`engagement-log.md`](engagement-log.md) under "notable".

**Business Suite vs the app:**

| | Business Suite (partner access, no passwords) | Instagram app |
|---|---|---|
| See Story mentions | **UNVERIFIED:** check on day one whether mentions show in the Inbox | Yes, in DMs |
| Reshare a mention to TripNerd's Story | **UNVERIFIED:** test on day one | Yes, "Add to Story" |
| Who | Us (inbox, weekdays) | TripNerd's person with the app, unless Karl agrees app access (see [`engagement-daily.md`](engagement-daily.md) §6) |

**Until the test says otherwise:** we watch for mentions in Business Suite and flag them the same day. TripNerd's person taps "Add to Story" in the app.

## 4. Collab posts

A Collab post shows on both TripNerd's account and the guest's, once the guest accepts.

**Before you start:**
- **It's a feed post**, so it needs TripNerd's approver's yes like every post ([`../README.md`](../README.md) §3).
- **Scope:** the PDF lists 8 new posts plus 3 owed. **Karl decides** whether a guest Collab post replaces a planned static or is extra.
- **Originality:** reposts and near-duplicates lose non-follower reach ([`../algorithm-niche-strategy.md`](../algorithm-niche-strategy.md) §1, row 4). Prefer photos or clips the guest hasn't already posted to their feed, or a new edit.

**Steps:**
1. **Ask the guest in a DM** (DRAFT, approver signs off): "Love this. Can we share it on TripNerd's Instagram as a Collab post, so it shows on your account too? We'll credit you. Reply YES if that's OK, and you can ask us to take it down anytime."
2. **Get the yes in writing.** A DM reply counts. Screenshot it and file it (§5). If anyone else is identifiable in the photo, they need to agree too. A child needs a parent's written OK.
3. **Get the original file** from the guest (full quality, not a screenshot of their Story).
4. **Draft the post:** a caption that credits the guest, with no claims, no words put in their mouth and no event affiliation. Send it to TripNerd's approver.
5. **Post it from the Instagram app.** In the post's tagging step, choose **Invite collaborator** and pick the guest's account. **UNVERIFIED:** whether Business Suite can send Collab invites. Test on day one.
6. **The guest must accept.** Until they do, the post shows only on TripNerd's account. If they decline, ask whether they still want it up on TripNerd's account alone. If not, take it down.
7. **Boosting:** a boost makes the post an ad. A post showing a guest is boosted only if their written consent covers paid use, and only through the boost gate (test-month plan §5b).

## 5. Consent

| Use | Consent needed |
|---|---|
| **Story reshare** of a guest's own Story | The guest posted it **publicly** and **tagged @tripnerd**. Reshare only that. Nothing private, untagged, or screenshotted from elsewhere |
| **Collab post** with a guest's photo or video | Written consent first (a DM reply is fine), plus the guest accepting the invite |
| **Original TripNerd post** (TripNerd's own photos or footage) showing identifiable guests | **Written consent first** |
| **Anything showing a child** | A parent's or guardian's written consent first. No exceptions |
| **Boosting** a post that shows a guest | Written consent that covers paid use |
| **A guest's words** in a caption or on screen | Their words exactly, with their OK. Never invented |

**Storing consent:**
- Store consent screenshots and signed releases in **NEEDS INPUT: TripNerd's Google Drive folder for consent records** (name, location, who has access).
- Suggested file name: `YYYY-MM-DD_trip_guest-initials_what-it-covers.png`.
- **Not in this repo.** It holds personal data. The repo records only that consent exists and where.
- **Withdrawal:** if a guest asks for removal, take it down the same day (delete the Story reshare, remove the Collab, archive the post) and note it in the consent folder.

**Existing library:** Karl's 2026-10-04 decision in [`../tracker.md`](../tracker.md) clears the TripNerd library already on file. These rules apply to new guest content from now on. **Augusta still waits** on TripNerd's confirmation of the venue and consent.

## 6. Photo-release wording (DRAFT)

> **DRAFT. NOT LEGAL ADVICE.** This is plain-language starting wording for **TripNerd's own legal review**. ServicePOW is not a law firm. TripNerd's lawyer decides the final wording, whether it suits each state where trips run, and how it's collected (paper, form, or checkbox at booking). Bracketed items are TripNerd's choices.

```
Photo and video permission: TripNerd

I agree that TripNerd may use photos and videos of me taken during
[trip / where guests went, dates] on TripNerd's Instagram account
[and website] [and in paid ads], with or without my name.

I'm not being paid for this.

I can withdraw this permission at any time by emailing [address].
TripNerd will then stop new use and remove posts it controls within
[number] days. I understand it can't recall copies others have
already shared.

I am 18 or older.

[For a child: I am the parent or legal guardian of [child's name]
and I give this permission on their behalf.]

Name: ____________   Signature: ____________   Date: ________
```

**Open for TripNerd's legal review:**
- organic only, or paid ads too;
- how long the permission lasts;
- the withdrawal window;
- how releases are stored and for how long;
- whether trips abroad need different wording (for example, the EU).
