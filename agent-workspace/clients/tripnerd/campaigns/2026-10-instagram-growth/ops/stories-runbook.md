# Stories runbook

**The promise** (PDF page 1): Stories at **10 AM ET every Mon, Wed and Fri**, plus **each new feed post shared to Story within 15 minutes**.

## 1. Who posts what

| Story type | Where it's made | Who taps Post |
|---|---|---|
| **Plain Story** (image or video, text baked in, no stickers) | Can be scheduled in Meta Business Suite, once partner access works | Karl, through partner access |
| **Sticker Story** (poll, question, quiz, slider, link) | **Instagram app only.** Stickers are lost when a Story is scheduled from Business Suite | **TripNerd's poster, on TripNerd's phone.** We send it ready (below) |
| **Share-to-Story of a new post** | Instagram app (paper plane → Add to story) | TripNerd's poster. Whether Business Suite can do a post share is UNVERIFIED; test it with partner access |
| **Reshare of a guest's Story** | Instagram app | See [`guest-loop.md`](guest-loop.md) (SA-OPS-B) |

**What we send TripNerd's poster for every app Story:**
- the background file (1080×1920);
- the exact sticker type and text;
- where the sticker goes: clear of the top ~250 px and bottom ~340 px;
- the time to post.

The poster is named on the setup call (**NEEDS INPUT**: not named yet).

## 2. The gate

- **Any NEW Story creative goes through our gate:** a new image or frame, new text, or a new sticker question. That means static QA and the critic/Skeptic gate as the campaign director routes it, then the approver's yes. Same as a feed post.
- **A plain share-to-Story of an approved post** needs no new gate. It may carry **only pre-approved sticker text**.
- **Pre-approved sticker text:** none yet. **DRAFT list for the approver's one-time yes:**
  - "New Reel"
  - "New post"
  - "Tap to watch"
  - "Swipe through"

  Until the approver says yes to the list, share with no added text.
- **No Augusta or Masters content** in any Story (photos, the word "Augusta", Masters marks, April tournament dates) until TripNerd's written confirmations are filed: venue and town, off the grounds, the property's OK, guest consent, TripNerd hosting. Why: [`../stories/gate-log.md`](../stories/gate-log.md).
- **No Trial Reel shares.** A Trial Reel is meant for non-followers first, and a Story share puts it in front of followers. This is an exception to "share every post"; **Karl confirms**.
