It's done from the Instagram app, so on day one we agree who handles it: us, or someone on your team working from our daily list."

## 1. The 20 minutes, step by step

| Minutes | Step | Where | Done when |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0–5** | **Reply to comments and DMs** | Business Suite → Inbox (Instagram) | Every new comment and DM since the last session has a reply, or was hidden as spam or abuse |
| **5–15** | **10–15 real comments on target posts** | Instagram app, as TripNerd | 10–15 comments, each one about that specific post (rules in §3) |
| **15–18** | **Check the permanent follow list** | Instagram app | New posts from list accounts seen. Any worth a comment get one, and those count toward the 10–15 |
| **18–20** | **Log it** | [`engagement-log.md`](engagement-log.md) | Today's row filled in |

**Inbox rules (minutes 0–5):**
- **Comments:** reply as TripNerd, short and specific to what the person said.
- **Questions about prices, dates, availability or booking:** don't answer them. We can't quote prices or promise seats. Hand them to TripNerd's enquiry person the same day.
  - **NEEDS INPUT:** who that person is, and how we hand over (a DM label, a forward, a message).
  - DRAFT hand-off reply (TripNerd's approver signs off): "Thanks for getting in touch. Someone from our trips team will reply here with the details."
- **Complaints:** don't argue in public. Reply once, offer to continue in DMs, and flag it to Karl and TripNerd the same day.
- **Spam, scams or abuse:** hide or restrict them and don't engage.
- **Augusta:** if anyone asks about Augusta, don't confirm the venue, dates or access. Hand it to TripNerd's enquiry person. Nothing about Augusta posts until TripNerd confirms the venue and consent (see [`../stories/gate-log.md`](../stories/gate-log.md)).
- **Mondays:** the inbox will hold the weekend's messages, so it may take longer than 5 minutes. Log the real minutes.
- **Post days:** replying to every comment in the first hour after a post belongs to the posting routine ([`../README.md`](../README.md) §3), not this 20 minutes. Log it as its own row, with the post's name under "notable".

**Skip an account or post if:**

- it's a reposted clip from another creator or a broadcast. Comment on the original or not at all.

**Event names:** use them only to say where guests went. Never write or imply that TripNerd is an official partner, official hospitality provider or sponsor of any event.

## 3. Comment rules

A good comment shows a person at TripNerd actually looked at the post: it names something in it, reacts to it honestly, or asks the poster something they can answer.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Be **specific to the post**: the shot, the seat, the moment, the caption | "Great post!", "🔥🔥", emoji-only, or anything that fits any post |
| Write it fresh, **in your own words**, every time | **AI-written or copy-paste comments.** The same comment twice is copy-paste |
| Keep it short and human | **Links**, hashtags, or "check out our page" |
| Let the profile do the selling | **Selling, or "DM us"** |
| Talk about the post | **Claims about TripNerd** (guests hosted, years, "best seats", prices, access) |
| Talk like a fan or a host would | **Implied affiliation with an event** ("official", "partner", "our event", event logos) |
| Pace it like reading: about one comment a minute across the 10 minutes | **Burst activity**: many comments in a minute or two, or making up missed days by doubling the next day |
| One or two comments per account per day at most | Several comments on one account in a row |

**Likes and follows:** liking a post you comment on is normal. Follows are only for the permanent follow list (§4). No follow-then-unfollow.

## 5. The "won't do" list

These were stated to TripNerd (PDF page 2: "They break Instagram's rules, put your account at risk, and those followers don't stay").

| Won't do | Why |
|---|---|
| **Follow-for-follow** (follow, wait for a follow-back, unfollow) | Mass follow/unfollow triggers action blocks (test-month plan §5c) |
| **Mass liking** | Instagram's Community Guidelines say not to "artificially collect likes, followers, or shares" (test-month plan §5c) |
| **Bots** or any tool that comments, likes, follows or DMs automatically | Same rule. It also isn't "real comments" |
| **Bought followers** | Same rule. They don't stay, and they make the November review meaningless |
| **Engagement pods** | Out for the test month (test-month plan §5c). Artificial engagement |
| **Giveaways** | Out for the test month (test-month plan §5c) |

## 6. Who does what

**What we know:**
- Outbound commenting on other accounts' posts is done **in the Instagram app, as TripNerd**.
- Partner access in Business Suite covers **TripNerd's own posts and inbox**: content, messages (DMs), comments on its own posts, and Insights ([partner access playbook](../../../../../playbooks/client-lifecycle/meta-partner-access.md)).
- **UNVERIFIED:** whether Business Suite can comment on *other* accounts' posts. **Test it on day one** once partner access works, and write the result in the log's "issues" column and in [`../tracker.md`](../tracker.md).

**Options:**

| Option | Inbox (minutes 0–5) | Outbound comments and follow list (minutes 5–18) | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Recommended** | Us, in Business Suite | TripNerd's person, in the app, from our daily list | Keeps "no passwords". Depends on TripNerd's person doing it every weekday |
| B | Us, in Business Suite | Us, in the app | Needs TripNerd to give us app access, which conflicts with "Partner access in Meta Business Suite (no passwords)" in the PDF. **Karl's decision.** If chosen, record only where the credential lives (pattern in [`access-and-accounts.md`](../../../access-and-accounts.md)), never the credential |
| C | Us, in Business Suite | Us, in Business Suite | Only if the day-one test shows Business Suite can comment on other accounts |

**The daily list (option A):** we prepare it, and TripNerd's person writes the comments.
- The list gives the post and why it's worth a comment. **It never contains a pre-written comment.** Claude may help find posts and write the one-line "what's specific" note; it never writes the comment.
- **Nothing goes to TripNerd without Karl.** Karl sends the list, or approves a standing arrangement for it.
- **NEEDS INPUT:** how the list is delivered (email, text, other) and by what time each weekday.

```
Daily list for [date]
Inbox: handled by us. [N] enquiries handed to [name].
Comment on these, in your own words. Skip any that feel off.
1. [link] · [EV/VN/CU/LF/PT] · what's specific: [one line]
2. ...
Follow list: no changes / add [account] because [reason] / remove [account] because [reason]
Tell us: how many you left, and anything notable (replies, follow-backs).
```

**Agree on day one:**
- [ ] Who does the inbox, and who does outbound comments (A, B or C)
- [ ] Result of the Business Suite comment test
- [ ] TripNerd's enquiry person and the hand-off method
- [ ] How the daily list is delivered, and by what time
- [ ] TripNerd's partners and host hotels, for the follow list
- [ ] Which trips run in October and November (also asked in [`guest-loop.md`](guest-loop.md))

**Watch out: who answers DMs?** The test-month plan (§2 and §4) says TripNerd answers its own DMs and first-line DMs aren't in October. The PDF, which TripNerd saw last, says "We reply to every comment and DM". This routine follows the PDF. Karl confirms on day one. Either way, booking enquiries go to TripNerd's person.

## 7. Measurement

- **In the Monday report** ([`../monday-report-template.md`](../monday-report-template.md)): profile visits and follows for the week from Insights, naming the export and date range, beside the week's totals from the log (see the weekly summary format in [`engagement-log.md`](engagement-log.md)).
- **No promised numbers.** Engagement can't be cleanly separated from the posts' effect on follows, so we report what we did beside what happened and don't claim cause.
- Boosted follows, if a boost runs, are reported separately (test-month plan §5b).

## 8. Cost note

- **About 7 hours of unpriced labor in October:** 20 weekdays × 20 minutes = 400 minutes, about 6.7 hours.
- **Included in October only.** If it continues, it needs a price (test-month plan §5c).
- Preparing the daily list under option A, and Monday's longer inbox, are not in that 7 hours. **Log real minutes**, so any November price rests on the time it actually took.
