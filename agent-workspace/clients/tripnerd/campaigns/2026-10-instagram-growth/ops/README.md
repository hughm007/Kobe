# Ops: running the October test month

The day-to-day operating docs for TripNerd's October 2026 Instagram test month ($1,500, agreed verbally). Each one turns a promise in the PDF given to Jason into steps, owners and records.

## What belongs here, and what doesn't

- **Belongs:** October operating docs for this account: routines, checklists, logs, approval records, drafts of operational messages.
- **Doesn't:** creative work and gate records (they live in the campaign Bible's folder, e.g. [`../../2026-10-04-launch-reels/`](../../2026-10-04-launch-reels/campaign-bible.md)); client-facing deliverables (`clients/tripnerd/deliverables/`); credentials of any kind (pointers only, in [`../../../access-and-accounts.md`](../../../access-and-accounts.md)).

## Standing rules (all files)

- **Nothing posts without TripNerd's named approver's yes.** No approver is named yet.
- **Nothing is sent to TripNerd without Karl.** Drafts are marked NOT SENT until they go.
- **Nothing is spent** without TripNerd's written yes and Karl as SPEND_APPROVER.
- **No Augusta or Masters content** until TripNerd's written confirmations are filed.
- **No passwords** in this repo, ever.
