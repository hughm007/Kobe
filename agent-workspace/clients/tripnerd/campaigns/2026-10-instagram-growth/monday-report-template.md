# How to use this template

- **Rewritten 2026-10-05 for the October test month.** The 90-day version's Day-90 target column is gone: the October deal sets no number in writing, and the November tiers are unconfirmed. **Don't add targets or tiers.**
- **Every Monday:** 12, 19 and 26 Oct, then the review in the first week of November.
- **First report, Mon 12 Oct = setup and baseline.** See §0.
- **Every number has a source.** Put a "Source:" line under every table: Instagram Insights export (file name) or Supermetrics (query), with the date range. If a number can't be traced, leave it out ([`reporting.md`](../../../../playbooks/client-lifecycle/reporting.md)).
- **Reporting week (PROPOSED; Karl confirms):** Monday to Sunday, ending the day before the report. Keep the same window every week.
- **Organic and paid stay apart.** Boosted results go only in §8. The November review reads **organic** follows.
- **Data source:** Supermetrics (free trial ends Sun 18 Oct; its Instagram Insights source isn't logged in yet) or an Instagram Insights export. Say which one in each Source line.
- **Save the sent report** as `clients/tripnerd/deliverables/YYYY-MM-DD-report.md`, and log it in [`tracker.md`](tracker.md) → Monday reports.
- **Order:** what happened, what it means, what we do next. Likes are never the goal.

Everything below the line is the report. Delete this section before sending.
