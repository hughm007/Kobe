## 2. Rules every script follows (from the plan and the brief)
- **Real footage only, with touch-up allowed:** upscaling, grading, cropping and removing burned-in captions. Nothing is generated. The text is composited.
- **No marks:**
  - no THE PLAYERS, PGA TOUR, Masters, NASCAR or sponsor marks;
  - no scoreboard, player or broadcast on screen;
  - event names stay out of copy until owner decision 2 is made.
- **Words to avoid:** no "VIP", "best", star ratings or counts. Every "included" line is a **DRAFT** until TripNerd confirms the package wording by email (the Evidence Record).
- **Call to action:** send-to-a-friend only, until someone is covering DMs.
- **Music:** licensed (Instagram's commercial library or an original track).
