# TripNerd — ten advert scripts (v2, generation-first)

**Owner direction (2026-09-28):** the scripts should reflect what really exists, but they do not have to be
built around real footage where Higgsfield (with Claude or ChatGPT doing stills, compositing, sound and the
edit) can make a shot look real. v2 therefore gives every beat a **generation route that works without any new
footage**, and names the real footage that would upgrade it.

**Tags:** **GEN** generated (disclosure on every platform) · **REAL** client photo/video (see `media-catalog.md`)
· **EXISTS** already generated, no new spend · **COMP** composited in the edit: the real logo file, typed text,
phone UI. Models are named from the live list of 2026-09-28; "proven" means it worked in this workspace,
"untested" means it has not been tried here yet.

## What generation does well here, and what it does not (the rule every beat follows)

| Makes it look real (use freely) | Evidence | Looks fake or breaks rules (avoid) | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Aerials and wide views of the 17th | Frame 1 locked; drone orbit approved in the 8.4 spot | Golfer close-ups, the putt in close-up | Owner: "looks fake" (v3–v6) |
| Empty rooms, bars, tables, food, drink macros | Gemini macro test; Kling 4K from stills | Generated faces laughing, cheering, clapping at readable distance | Policy (realism floor) |
| Crowds at distance; people from behind or in profile; hands at rest | v2 balcony clips (g1, g3) | Readable text or logos made by the model ("TripNers") | Polo fix, 24 Sep |
| One calm actor speaking a short line in a medium shot | The host in the approved 8.4 spot | A generated person speaking as a customer or reviewer | Policy (FTC form) |
| The same frame twice (empty, then full) via a still and its edit | Nano Banana still + reference edit | Pure-generated shots held longer than 5 s | Policy |

**Standard route (ESTIMATE until re-priced at the spend gate):** Nano Banana 2 4K still (≈3 credits) → Kling 3.0
4K image-to-video, 5 s (≈30 credits) · macros on Gemini Omni Flash 1.1 · a speaking actor on Seedance 2.5
omni-reference or Veo 3.1 (untested here). Budget two takes per shot. Every text, logo and phone screen is COMP.

**House rules in every script:** one continuous sound bed; no superlatives, prices, dates or "VIP" until the
package sheet backs them; the tournament-week house is never named; the end card is the real logo file on brand
blue, "Hospitality. Handled.", "Talk to a Nerd", tripnerd.com. VO lines are marked for delivery: "…" is a real
pause, one idea per breath.

## The top three for Higgsfield (quality · realism · flow), generation allowed

| Rank | Script | Quality | Realism | Flow | Estimate | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **1 · The Empty Suite** | 5 | 5 | 5 | 6 stills + 6 animations | Built on the one trick generation does best: the same frame twice, empty then full. No generated faces at all. The real roar is the hinge. Fully makeable today without new footage. |
| **2** | **5 · The Roar** | 4 | 5 | 5 | 0–2 | Mostly exists already. Sound does the storytelling, and the picture is wide crowds and backs. Short, clean, cheap. |
| **3** | **6 · Your People** | 5 | 4 | 5 | 6–8 | With generation allowed, every vignette becomes a profile, a back or a pair of hands, which generation handles well. The VO carries the flow, and it is the strongest pitch to the corporate host. One risk: the hand on the shoulder. |

**Why these beat the others:** Row 58 needs a generated seat view the owner already rejected once. One Putt is
built on the putt close-ups the owner called fake. The List and Talk to a Nerd are claims-heavy until the
package sheet arrives. Day One/Two/Three is the most real of the ten, but it leans on still photos, and it
sells the house, not the 17th. It is the runner-up.

## What would materially change these scripts

1. Which product: the suite on 17, the tournament-week house, or both.
2. Guest consent by group (only matters where a REAL face is used).
3. The package sheet — what is included decides the words in 4, 8 and any "VIP".
4. Format first: 9:16 or 16:9.
5. Raw suite clips and the 25-video library at full resolution — an upgrade now, not a dependency.
