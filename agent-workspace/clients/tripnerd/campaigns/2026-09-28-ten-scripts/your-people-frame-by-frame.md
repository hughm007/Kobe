# YOUR PEOPLE — 30 s, 16:9 — frame-by-frame brief

**From:** Service Pow Campaign Director. **For:** ChatGPT, generating one frame at a time in Higgsfield.
**Owner's rule, restated:** build one frame, show it to the owner, lock it, then the next frame. Never generate
ahead of the owner.

## 0. Read this first, ChatGPT

1. **Work order is not film order.** Build the **master plate (F7 still)** first. It fixes the cast, the clothes,
   the rail and the light. Every other still is made *from* the master plate as a reference, so the six people
   stay the same people.
2. **Two steps per frame:** Step A makes the still, Step B animates it. Show the owner each still before animating.
3. **Settings.**
   - Stills: **Nano Banana 2**, 4K, 16:9. Attach the references listed for that frame.
   - Motion: **Kling 3.0**, mode **4k**, **5 s**, sound **off**, `start_image` = the approved still.
   - F7 only: **Seedance 2.5**, mode `omni_reference`, approved still as `start_image`, **1080p**, 5 s, audio off.
4. **No text, logo or brand mark is ever asked of the model.** Captions, the end card and the logo go on in the
   edit. Clothes, cups and lanyards stay plain.
5. **Two takes per shot, then change the prompt.** A third identical re-roll is wasted. Use the frame's fallback.
6. **Reject a take if any item on its checklist fails.** Faces turning to camera and hands going wrong are the two
   failures that make this advert look fake.
7. **Re-check prices in Higgsfield before you start.** The estimate in section 8 is from earlier jobs, not today's
   price list.
8. **Disclosure:** the finished film contains realistic generated people. Switch on the AI-content label on
   Meta, TikTok and YouTube.

## 1. The advert in one paragraph

A warm voice names the people a host would bring: the client they've chased for a year, the two who hit the
number, the brother-in-law who won't stop talking about this place, and their dad. Each one is shown in a quiet
moment at the rail of a TripNerd suite over the 17th: in profile, from behind, or just a hand. Then the gallery
goes silent, a putter clicks, and the roar hits. The camera pulls back to show they were all standing at the same
rail the whole time. **Your people. Hospitality. Handled.**

| Spine | |
|---|---|
| Core message | You bring the people. TripNerd handles everything else. |
| Primary emotion | Pride in the people you brought, felt quietly |
| Viewer starts | "Hospitality ads are all drinks and logos." |
| Viewer ends | "I know exactly who I'd bring." |
| Narrative question | Who are these people, and why are they here? |
| Payoff | The pull-back: they are all one group at one rail, and the roar hits them together |

## 3. Continuity bible — hold these in every frame

**Location.** An open-air second-floor hospitality suite on the bank of the 17th. White-painted steel posts, a
white metal rail with a flat top, a white roof overhang. Below the rail: a packed spectator bank sloping to the
water. Across the water: the island green's wooden bulkhead edge, the flagstick, and a crowded two-storey white
grandstand with a navy fascia. To the right: the small round tree island ringed with yellow flowers. **Match the
reference frames, not memory.**

**Light.** Bright mid-afternoon sun, high, behind the camera on the left. About 5600 K. Crisp short shadows falling
away from the camera. Blue sky with a few white cumulus clouds. Sparkle on the water. Same in every frame.

**Camera.** Always on the suite side, behind or beside the guests. Nobody ever looks into the lens. Lenses stay in
three steps: 63° wide, 47° medium, 29° close; 12° and 18° for the two detail shots. Movement is slow and
motivated: a truck along the rail, a push, one pull-back.

**Colour.** Natural daylight, clean whites, green grass, blue water. Plain clothes in muted colours so the one loud
polo reads as a joke.

**Cast and wardrobe (left to right along the rail).** Everything plain and unbranded.
