## PART D: Rules (any rewrite must follow them)

1. **Real client footage first.** No AI-generated people presented as TripNerd guests. AI may only be an obvious transition, disclosed, and never proof.
2. **No third-party marks on screen** (tournament, tour, sponsor logos). The event name goes **in the caption only**, with a "not affiliated" disclaimer.
3. **Banned words:** VIP, exclusive, private, owns, handled, best, number-one, guaranteed, "DM us", "send this to…", tag-a-friend; "Book now" unless confirmed.
4. **On-screen text:**
   - **6 words or fewer per card**;
   - readable text between y = 288 and 1344 on a 1080x1920 frame;
   - it must make sense with the sound off.
5. **Not yet confirmed in writing by TripNerd.** Phrase these as questions or soften them until confirmed:
   - that these exact seats were TripNerd's;
   - rail access in 2027;
   - "we've hosted here before";
   - food, drinks, hotel, transport, prices, group sizes.
6. **People:**
   - Guest faces: reported as approved by TripNerd's CEO (written confirmation pending).
   - Identifiable pro golfers: fine organically, a risk in paid ads.
