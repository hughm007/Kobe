TripNerd Phoenix Open: Grok challenge pack

1. Unzip this folder.
2. Open a new Grok chat. Upload all 10 photos (photos/) and the 4 keyframe sheets
   (video-keyframes/). Also try uploading the 4 videos (videos/). If Grok
   refuses or can't play them, the keyframe sheets cover what they show.
   If Grok limits uploads per message, send the files across two messages
   and say "more files coming, don't answer yet".
3. Paste the text of STEP-1-PASTE-INTO-GROK.md and send it.
4. ONLY AFTER Grok answers, paste STEP-2-PASTE-AFTER-GROK-ANSWERS.md.
   (Keeping Step 2 back is what makes it a real blind test.)
5. Bring Grok's two answers back to Claude for a final reconciliation.
