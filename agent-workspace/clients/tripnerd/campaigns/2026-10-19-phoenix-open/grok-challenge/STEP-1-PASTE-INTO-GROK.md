## Hard rules for any ad you propose
1. **Real media only.** No AI-generated people, crowds or scenery, and no AI animation of the photos. Text and graphics are added in editing.
2. **No third-party marks on screen.** That means tournament, tour, sponsor and broadcaster logos, signage and turf logos. Anything you keep must be croppable or blurrable without ruining the shot. Say how.
3. **No identifiable faces** without a signed release; there are no releases on file. Backs of heads and distant crowds are fine. Identifiable professional athletes in a paid ad are also a risk (implied endorsement).
4. **No claims without written proof.** The package contents for next year are not confirmed in writing. Questions are fine; promises are not.
5. **Banned words:** VIP, best, number-one, guaranteed.
6. **Truthful labels only.** A year or place shown on screen must be provable from the media itself.
7. **Readable text** sits between y=288 and y=1344 on a 1080x1920 frame, and the ad must work with the sound off.
