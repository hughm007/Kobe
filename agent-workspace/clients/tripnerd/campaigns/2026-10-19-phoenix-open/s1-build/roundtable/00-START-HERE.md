# TripNerd × Phoenix Open: Reel S1 "From our seats" round table

**Who's at the table:** the owner (Karl), Claude (built this cut), Grok, ChatGPT.
**What we're deciding:** what to change in S1, and the best next steps, to get a Phoenix Open Reel that scores **8/10 or higher** and makes people message TripNerd.

## How to run it (about 10 minutes)
1. Open a new chat in **Grok** and one in **ChatGPT**.
2. In each, upload:
   - `video/TripNerd_Phoenix_S1_FromOurSeats_NoMusic.mp4`;
   - everything in `frames/`.
   - If the AI can't play video, the frames and the contact sheet show every half-second.
3. Paste the whole of `01-BRIEF-FOR-AI.md` into each chat, then send.
4. Fill in `02-ANSWER-TEMPLATE.md` yourself, as the owner (5 minutes, be blunt).
5. Bring all three answers back to Claude. Claude merges them into one decision table using `03-DECISION-LOG.md`, then builds the agreed v2.

**Why the same template for everyone:** it makes the answers line up question by question, so disagreements are visible instead of buried in essays.
