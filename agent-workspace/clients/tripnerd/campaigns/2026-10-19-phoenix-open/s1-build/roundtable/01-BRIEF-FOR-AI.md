## 3. Rules any suggestion must follow
1. **Real client footage first.** No AI-generated people presented as TripNerd guests. If AI is used, it's only as an obvious transition, it's disclosed with Instagram's AI label, and it's never presented as proof.

3. **Banned words:** VIP, exclusive, private, owns, handled, best, number-one, guaranteed, "DM us", "send this to…", tag-a-friend.
4. **On-screen cards: 6 words or fewer.** Readable text sits between y = 288 and 1344 on a 1080x1920 frame. The ad must work with the sound off.
