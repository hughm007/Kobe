# Phoenix 16th awareness scripts v1

**Goal (owner, 2026-10-20):** build awareness that TripNerd sells and hosts hospitality at the Phoenix Open's 16th hole. The one-shot (`build-v2/…/TN-PHX_bunker_oneshot_AI_v1.mp4`) is the candidate opener.

## Is the one-shot a good opener?
- **Yes, for the hook. Not on its own for awareness.**
  - The real swing gives contact at about 0.4 s, then sand, backspin and a real crowd. That stops the scroll.
  - **But for 5.7 s it shows a pro and a crowd, not TripNerd.**
  - Awareness needs **the brand and "we host here"** inside the first 3 s, then real guests.
- **Use it trimmed:**
  - real swing → spin-back (about 2.5–3 s);
  - optionally 1 s of the AI reveal as a flourish;
  - then cut to TripNerd's guests.
- **Do not let the invented AI stadium be the "this is where we host you" proof.** Only real footage can carry that claim.
- **Brand-in-3-seconds fix:** a small lower-third from 0.3 s, "TripNerd · at the 16th". Plus a spoken or written line that names hosting by 3 s.
