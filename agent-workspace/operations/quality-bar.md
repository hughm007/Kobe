# Quality Bar

What "ready to send" means. Everything leaving this workspace toward a client clears this.

The point of a written bar is that it doesn't move when a deadline is close.

---

## Every deliverable

- ☐ **On brand** for whoever's name is on it — the client's, from their `brand-guide.md`;
      ours, from `company/brand/brand-voice.md`. Their voice beats our house voice in
      their own materials, always.
- ☐ **Every claim supported.** Statistics, performance figures, competitor claims and
      testimonials have a source that could be produced if asked. **If you can't source
      it, cut it.**
- ☐ **No invented data.** Not a metric, not a result, not a name, not a quote. Ever.
- ☐ **Specific, not generic.** Names the audience, the benefit and the proof. If it could
      be sent to a different client by changing the logo, it isn't finished.
- ☐ **Complete.** No lorem ipsum, no placeholder images, no `NEEDS INPUT` markers, no
      unfinished sentences. *(Internal drafts may carry markers; client-facing work may
      not.)*
- ☐ **Proofread once more, slowly.** Names, prices, dates, phone numbers and URLs checked
      character by character.
- ☐ **Links tested.** Every one.
- ☐ **Correct spelling variant** — UK / US / AU per the client.
- ☐ **Approved** by whoever the brief says approves it.

## Anything that goes live

Live means: published, sent, posted, deployed, or spending money.

- ☐ Explicit human sign-off for **this specific action**. Drafting is always fine;
      dispatching needs approval. Approval to draft is not approval to send.
- ☐ Tracking installed **and verified firing** — installed is not working
- ☐ Tested end to end as a real user would experience it
- ☐ Reversible, or a rollback plan exists
- ☐ Someone is ready to handle what it produces — replies, leads, enquiries

## Data and reporting

- ☐ Every figure traceable to a named source and date range
- ☐ Date ranges consistent with previous reports
- ☐ Comparisons like-for-like
- ☐ Reported against targets agreed in advance, not chosen after seeing results
- ☐ Anomalies flagged, not smoothed over

## Legal and compliance

- ☐ Claims substantiable by the client
- ☐ Required disclosures present — sponsorship, affiliate, regulated categories
- ☐ Images and fonts licensed for this use
- ☐ Personal data handled lawfully; consent respected
- ☐ Unsubscribe present and working in every marketing email
- ☐ Platform policies met

## The last question

> **If this landed in front of the client's most sceptical colleague, what would they
> pick at first?**

Fix that before sending.

---

## When the bar can't be met

Say so before the deadline, not after. Options, in order of preference:

1. Ship a smaller scope at full quality
2. Move the deadline
3. Ship with the gap **named explicitly** to the client

Never ship a gap silently and hope. That's how a fixable problem becomes a trust problem.
