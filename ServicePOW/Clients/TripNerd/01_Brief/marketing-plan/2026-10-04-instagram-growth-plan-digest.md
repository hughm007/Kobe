## 3. What the plan changes for the roadmap and the rules
- **Order.** The owner has effectively chosen to run step 6 (the calendar) now, with step 2 (baseline and access) happening in week one through partner access and an Insights screenshot. Steps 3, 5, 8 and 9 are now proposals from the plan, to be confirmed against the baseline at Day 30.
- **Approval chain.** Two sign-offs on every post: Service Pow's owner (APPROVER) before the batch goes out, TripNerd's named approver (CLIENT_APPROVER) before anything posts. Nothing posts without both.
- **Rules that stay as they were:** real material first (decision 0008); no claim without an Evidence Record; no star ratings, counts, "#1", "VIP", "best", "guarantee"; guest faces need consent; third-party logos never on screen; music licensed (original or the platform's commercial-use library).
- **Rules the plan loosens, pending the owner:** event names in captions (decision 2); the city name "Augusta" (decision 1).

## 4. Not in the plan, and worth raising
- **No DM coverage exists yet** (ManyChat and partner access are "not set up"). A comment-keyword call to action in week 1 creates promises nobody answers. The week-1 brief uses send-to-a-friend calls to action instead until coverage is live.
- **The start date.** Posting Reel 01 at 2 PM on Monday 5 Oct needs a built, approved Reel and Business Suite access by Monday morning. None exists on Sunday 4 Oct. The plan's own risk rule (a late approval moves the batch back a week) is the honest path; see the week-1 brief's schedule.
- **LinkedIn** is the live channel today (audit §3) and the plan is Instagram-only by design. The plan's Reels and carousels can be re-cut for LinkedIn later; not this week.
