# TripNerd growth plan: the steps

**Goal (owner, 2026-09-30):** help TripNerd gain followers and online presence over the coming months, with a plan and a system tailored to TripNerd, built step by step with the owner.

**Recommendation on the goal:** treat followers and presence as *leading indicators* of qualified inquiries, which is what the business sells. See the audit §11. Targets are set in step 3, from a verified baseline, not guessed.

**How we work:** one step at a time. Each step produces a file in this folder. The owner approves it before the next begins. A step that needs something only the client holds parks as `NEEDS INPUT`, and the work moves on where it can.

## Standing rules for this plan
- **Real material first** (decision 0008): the plan's content comes from TripNerd's own photos, videos and reviews.
- **No claim without an Evidence Record:** not "#1", "100% guarantee", "VIP", star ratings or review counts.
- **Third-party marks stay out** (client brief), and guests' and staff faces need consent before paid use.
- **Nothing is published, posted or spent** without the owner's explicit sign-off for that action.
- **Numbers carry their source and date.** An unverified number is logged UNKNOWN.
