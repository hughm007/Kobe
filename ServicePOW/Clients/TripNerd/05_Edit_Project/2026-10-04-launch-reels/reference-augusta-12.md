# Reference board: "the green only, a ball landing and stopping close to the pin" at Augusta

**Ask (owner, 2026-10-06, after rejecting Two-ways v2 and stopping that line):** find a specific video of a golf shot at Augusta showing only the green and the ball sticking close to the pin; then "screenshot or screen record" it, "from Augusta", with a viewing spot as a TripNerd guest sees it.

Nothing from the video is kept in this repository; the sandbox is discarded.

## What was not done, and why
- **No clip was cut** from this footage, and none goes into a TripNerd asset. FACT: the footage is Augusta National's official/broadcast material; no advertising licence for it exists (Getty's Masters material is editorial-only). The client brief keeps Masters and Augusta names, logos and broadcast graphics out of every advert; the brand-assets policy binds. A still on a reference board for the owner's eyes is a moodboard; the same frames in an ad are an infringement, with a takedown or account-strike risk in the first weeks of the growth plan.
- **No patron video exists.** FACT (secondary sources, 2026-10-06): phones are prohibited on the grounds at all times; cameras are allowed on practice days only.
- **"A viewing spot as a TripNerd guest."** FACT: TripNerd's Augusta product is a private home, course passes and daily hospitality (tripnerd.com "Augusta Experience"); there is no on-course suite in it. A guest watches 12 from the patron gallery behind the tee (the crowd seen in the swing frames), like any badge holder. ASSUMPTION flagged for the owner: an ad that implies a privileged on-course viewing spot at Augusta would be an unevidenced claim (claims-and-proof). The honest line is that the badge gets you there and the house and hospitality are the TripNerd part.

## Recommendation
1. **Film the shot.** Route (a): a practice-day shoot (Mon–Wed) at Masters week 2027 with a proper camera, which also gives the real "guest in the gallery behind 12" shot. Route (b): any course now, a pro-style approach landing beside a pin, no venue named. Adobe Stock has no match (three video searches, 2026-10-06).
2. **Brief the shooter from the sheet:** camera low behind or beside the green, flag centre-right, water or sky as the top band, the ball enters high and checks; 3–5 s from landing to rest; no graphics, no names.
3. **The ask is logged** in the week-1 brief (§8) and in `company/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md`.
