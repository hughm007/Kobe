## Recommendation: Augusta week, built as "real house + Higgsfield course"
1. **The chat already says Augusta.** "Badges" and "the house" are that week's words; the independent Skeptic lenses on v1 flagged, without being told, that the chat sets up one event and the Sawgrass footage pays off another. Naming the event fixes the ad's biggest fault rather than adding a feature.
2. **It is the one event where AI footage is the honest route.** No real on-course video can exist. Real-first stays the rule everywhere else in the piece: the house is real (TripNerd's photos), the course beats are generated, and the piece says so.
3. **The material is already made, in the owner's own taste, at sunk cost.** The clips above were generated in this account tonight and look consistent (same light, same pines, same people). The build spends on nothing new beyond one or two image-to-video moves on real house photos if wanted.
4. **It is the product that sells furthest out and highest.** April 2027 is the booking window now; the thread's "a year of not booking" lands hardest on this one.

**Daytona as the alternative:** fully real, no AI label, the TripNerd sign on the gate is a genuine brand moment, and the engines are a better sound payoff than applause. Against: 2022 phone footage at 720p 16:9 (a 9:16 crop needs a 2.7x upscale, soft), a guest's face at the start (release), Love's banners and section signs in several frames (crop or avoid), and the chat must be rewritten (no "badges", no "house": a suite and a hotel). Right for a second ad in the Daytona sales window (February), not for this one.
